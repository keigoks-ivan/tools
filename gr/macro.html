Bottom line總結 The Greek crisis was resolved on the household balance sheet and not on the sovereign's. General government debt peaked at 209.4% of GDP in 2020 and is 146.1% in 2025, roughly where it stood in 2010. Over the same arc the bank housing loan book fell from €80.3bn in August 2010 to €25.1bn in August 2026, a fall of 68.7%, the non-performing loan ratio went from 45.6% in 2017 to 3.3% at the end of 2025, and the share of the population living in a mortgaged home fell from 17.5% to 8.9%. The banks were cleaned by moving the loss rather than by the loans performing: on the all-lender measure household credit fell only 32.3%, because the Hercules securitisations moved the paper off bank balance sheets to servicers while the households still owed it.

Euro membership removed the adjustment channel, so the adjustment ran through wages and prices instead. This is the sharpest contrast available with Türkiye. Türkiye inflated its household debt away: credit fell from 19.4% to 9.4% of GDP while the nominal stock rose 5.7 times. Greece could not, and deflated into the debt instead: the ratio fell from 67.1% to 38.1% with the nominal stock falling too, across four consecutive deflation years from 2013 to 2016. Same problem, opposite instrument, opposite consequence for property. The Turkish version is set out in Türkiye Macro × Property.

Output has not recovered and the population is shrinking. Real GDP is €206bn in 2025 against €239bn in 2008, still 13.8% below the pre-crisis peak seventeen years later; unemployment peaked at 27.8% in 2013 and is 7.4% in August 2026; population peaked at 11.12 million in 2010 and is 10.41 million on the World Bank count, a loss of about 707,000 people. Natural change now runs at about minus 55,000 a year and the population has stopped falling only because net migration turned sharply positive from 2023. Greek nominal house prices made a new all-time high in an economy that has not regained its pre-crisis size, and the transaction-level consequences of that are set out in the companion Athens Property Market Report. 希臘危機是在家庭的資產負債表上解決的，不是在政府的資產負債表上。一般政府債務在 2020 年見頂，達 GDP 的 209.4%。2025 年為 146.1%，約與 2010 年相當。同一段時間，銀行住宅貸款帳面從 2010 年 8 月的 803 億歐元，降到 2026 年 8 月的 251 億歐元，跌幅 68.7%。不良放款比率從 2017 年的 45.6%，降到 2025 年底的 3.3%。住在有房貸住宅的人口占比，從 17.5% 降到 8.9%。銀行帳面變乾淨，靠的是移轉損失，不是貸款恢復正常。以涵蓋所有貸款機構的口徑算，家庭信用只下降 32.3%。原因是赫拉克勒斯（Hercules）證券化方案，把債權從銀行資產負債表移給服務機構。家戶對這些債務仍然欠著。

歐元成員身分取消了調整的管道，調整因此落在工資與物價上。這是它與土耳其最鮮明的對照。土耳其用通膨消去家庭債務。信用占 GDP 比重從 19.4% 降到 9.4%，同期名目餘額增加為 5.7 倍。希臘做不到，只能向債務內部通縮。比率從 67.1% 降到 38.1%，名目餘額同時下降，其間還有 2013 至 2016 年連續四年的通縮。問題相同，工具相反，對房產的後果也相反。土耳其的版本見《土耳其總經 × 房產》。

Non-performing loans, and where they went不良放款，以及它們的去向

The ratio was 4.6% in 2007, rose to 45.6% in 2017 and reads 27.0% at the end of the published series in 2020. The current reading is 3.3% at the end of 2025. The intervening fall is the Hercules asset protection scheme and it did not consist of borrowers resuming payment: the bank housing loan book fell from €45.8bn to €30.5bn in the single year 2021, which is the transfer. The banking system is now clean on every published metric and the household obligations behind the transferred paper still exist, held by servicers outside the banking statistics. That is why the all-lender credit measure fell by half as much as the bank measure, and why a Greek recovery reading taken from bank data alone will overstate how much household repair has occurred.比率 2007 年為 4.6%，升到 2017 年的 45.6%。公布序列終點的 2020 年為 27.0%。當期讀數是 2025 年底的 3.3%。這中間的降幅來自赫拉克勒斯資產保護方案，不是借款人恢復繳款。銀行住宅貸款帳面在 2021 年一年內，從 458 億歐元降到 305 億歐元。這個降幅就是移轉。銀行體系在各項公布指標上都很乾淨。被移走的債權背後，家戶的義務仍在，由銀行統計之外的服務機構持有。所以涵蓋所有貸款機構的信用口徑，降幅只有銀行口徑的一半。只看銀行資料判讀希臘復甦的人，會高估家戶的修復程度。

  • Foreign inflow × absent domestic credit. These two together are why foreign cash has price impact out of proportion to its transaction share. Where區位

Athens, and not because of momentum. The city index has risen 107.5% off its 2017 trough and stands 14.8% above its 2008 nominal peak, but the derived real index is still 15.6% below its 2007 peak. Athens is where the migration inflow, the tenant depth and the foreign capital arrive, and the Bank of Greece publishes no intra-Athens district index, so submarket selection has to be done on portal asking data that is explicitly tier three.雅典，但理由不是動能。該市指數自 2017 年谷底上漲 107.5%，比 2008 年名目高點高 14.8%。推導所得的實質指數仍低於 2007 年高點 15.6%。雅典是移民流入、租客深度與外資資金的到達地。希臘央行沒有公布雅典市內的分區指數。次市場的選擇只能依平台要價資料，這些資料明確屬第三層。

What產品

Completed stock on the long-let line, outside the three frozen municipal districts. Long leases are expressly permitted under article 100 and are caught by neither the registration freeze nor the golden-visa short-let prohibition, which carries a €50,000 fine and permit revocation. New supply competes against 1.81 million vacant dwellings that are neither rented nor for sale, so a construction-led thesis is the wrong shape for this market.位於三個凍結市轄區以外、以長期出租為承作基礎的已完工物件。第 100 條明文允許長期租賃。登錄凍結和黃金簽證的短租禁令都管不到，後者附帶 5 萬歐元罰鍰與撤銷許可。新供給的競爭對象，是 181 萬戶既沒出租、也沒出售的空屋。所以以營建為主軸的論述，不適用於本市場。

When時點

The decision does not turn on the price index, which is provisional in its last four quarters and has no independent cross-check. It turns on the quarterly non-resident inflow series and the annual migration release, both published and both moving before price does. Inflows fell 25.3% in 2025 and rose 43.4% year on year in the first quarter of 2026, so the series is currently ambiguous and one more quarter settles it.這個判斷不取決於房價指數。指數最後四季是初步值，也沒有獨立的交叉檢核。它取決於季頻非居民流入序列與年度移民統計。兩者都是公開資料，也都先於價格變動。流入 2025 年下降 25.3%，2026 年第一季年增 43.4%。這個序列目前的訊號不明確，再一季即可判定。

What this page concludes本頁的結論

Greece is the only market documented on this site whose crisis was resolved on the household balance sheet rather than on the sovereign's, and the distinction is not academic. Government debt is 146.1% of GDP in 2025 after peaking at 209.4%, roughly where it stood in 2010, while the bank housing loan book fell 68.7% and the non-performing ratio went from 45.6% to 3.3%. The banks were cleaned by moving the loss: on the all-lender measure household credit fell only 32.3%, and the households behind the securitised paper still owe it. Any reading of Greek recovery taken from bank data alone overstates by thirty-six percentage points how much household repair has occurred.希臘是本站所記載的市場中，唯一在家庭資產負債表、而非政府資產負債表上解決危機的。這個區分不只是學術上的。政府債務見頂 209.4% 後，2025 年為 GDP 的 146.1%，約與 2010 年相當。同期銀行住宅貸款帳面下降 68.7%，不良放款比率從 45.6% 降到 3.3%。銀行帳面變乾淨，靠的是移轉損失。以涵蓋所有貸款機構的口徑算，家庭信用只下降 32.3%。已證券化債權背後的家戶，仍欠著這些債務。只看銀行資料判讀希臘復甦的人，會把家戶的修復程度高估三十六個百分點。

Euro membership removed the adjustment channel, so the adjustment ran through wages and prices. Türkiye reduced the same ratio from 19.4% to 9.4% of GDP with its nominal debt stock rising 5.7 times; Greece reduced its from 67.1% to 38.1% with the nominal stock falling, across four consecutive deflation years. The two markets now look alike on the surface, with the large majority of transactions clearing in cash, and they are not alike: Athens has a 3.30% mortgage that almost nobody takes and Istanbul has a 41.4% to 43.7% (late July 2026) mortgage that almost nobody takes. A rate cut reopens one channel and would do very little to the other. The Turkish record is in Türkiye Macro × Property.歐元成員身分取消了調整的管道，調整因此落在工資與物價上。土耳其把同一比率從 GDP 的 19.4% 降到 9.4%，同期名目債務餘額增加為 5.7 倍。希臘把它從 67.1% 降到 38.1%，同期名目餘額下降，其間還有連續四年的通縮。兩個市場表面相似，絕大多數成交都以現金完成，但並不相同。雅典有幾乎沒人用的 3.30% 房貸。伊斯坦堡有幾乎沒人用的 41.4% 至 43.7%（2026 年 7 月下旬）房貸。降息會重啟其中一個管道，對另一個作用很小。土耳其的紀錄見《土耳其總經 × 房產》。

The demand base is the weak point and it is not cyclical. Real GDP is 13.8% below its 2008 peak seventeen years on, population has fallen 707,000 since 2010, natural change runs at about minus 55,000 a year and the birth rate has fallen from 10.7 to 6.3 per thousand since 2008. Greek household formation is now an immigration outcome, which makes the demand base a function of a dated administrative variable rather than of a demographic trend. Against that, real house prices have compounded at 1.5% a year over twenty-nine years and the IMF puts the current level about 10% above fundamentals.
