Bottom line總結 The Greek migration ministry's own golden-visa page still publishes a €250,000 threshold and still cites Law 4251/2014 as the governing statute, in both language versions, as of 12 October 2026. Both statements were superseded on 5 April 2024 by article 64 of Law 5100/2024, which set tiers of €400,000 and €800,000. Enterprise Greece removed its golden-visa page entirely rather than update it. The administering ministry's front door contradicts the statute it administers, and that is a useful warning about how much of the published material on this market is out of date.

Athens is the completed version of the cycle that Bangkok and Dubai are still inside. The Bank of Greece apartment price index for Athens peaked at 101.4 in the second quarter of 2008, fell to 56.1 in the first quarter of 2017, a drawdown of 44.7% across thirty-five quarters, and reads 116.4 in the first quarter of 2026. That is 107.5% above the trough and 14.8% above the 2008 peak in nominal terms. On the BIS real index the same market fell 48.0% peak to trough and is still 18.9% below its 2007 peak. The nominal recovery completed in the first quarter of 2024; the real recovery has not completed after eighteen and a half years. The Bank of Greece reported on 15 September 2026 that Athens apartment prices were 5.0% higher year on year in the second quarter of 2026 and the national index 5.5% higher. Every number on this page has to be read against that gap.

Two dated restrictions on this market have been tested, and both produced front-running rather than compliance. The golden-visa threshold rose on 5 April 2024 with a transitional deposit deadline of 31 August 2024: permit filings in August 2024 were 550, below July's 715, while Bank of Greece non-resident real-estate inflows reached €783.7m and €824.5m in the third and fourth quarters of 2024, the two highest quarters on record, before collapsing to €356.8m in the first quarter of 2025. The money moved before the deadline and the paperwork followed months later. The short-term-let registration freeze in central Athens took effect on 1 January 2025 and central-Athens listings rose about 11% in its first year. A third restriction was announced on 6 September 2026: a 15% transfer tax on residential purchases by non-EU natural persons from 1 July 2027. It is not yet legislated, and no source confirms an exemption for golden-visa buyers. The registration freeze is also to be extended to 31 December 2027. What this report concludes本報告的結論

Athens is the only market documented on this site that has completed a full property cycle within the available record, and the completed cycle says something the markets still inside one cannot. The Athens index fell 44.7% over thirty-five quarters, recovered 107.5% off the trough and stands 14.8% above its 2008 nominal peak, while the real index fell 48.0% nationally and is still 18.9% below its 2007 peak. The nominal recovery took fifteen and three-quarter years. The real recovery has not happened. Any market currently arguing that a nominal recovery is a recovery has an eighteen-year worked example available in Athens.雅典是本站所記載的市場中，唯一在可得紀錄內走完一整個房產循環的市場。完整的循環能說出還在循環中的市場說不出的事。雅典指數三十五季下跌 44.7%，自谷底回升 107.5%。現在比 2008 年名目高點高 14.8%。同期全國實質指數下跌 48.0%，仍低於 2007 年高點 18.9%。名目價格花了十五年九個月收復，實質價格至今沒有。凡是現在主張「名目復甦就是復甦」的市場，雅典都有一個十八年的實例可以對照。

An announced restriction on this market produces front-running rather than compliance, and there are now two independent instances.
