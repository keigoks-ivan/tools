# Bangkok and Hanoi scenery

Original procedural models, researched 2026-10-08. These circuits adapt city scenery into closed racing layouts; they are not surveyed replicas of public roads. No street imagery or third-party 3D models are redistributed.

## Bangkok

- [Tourism Authority of Thailand: Wat Arun](https://www.tourismthailand.org/Attraction/wat-arun-ratchawararam-ratchawaramahawihan): porcelain-clad central prang and its 82 m height, four smaller prangs, temple precinct. Some tourism descriptions round the height differently; this reconstruction uses the 82 m description.
- [UNESCO tentative list: Wat Arun](https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6821): central prang, subsidiary towers and architectural composition.
- The scene includes authored stepped white towers, porcelain-inspired colored details, gold finials, layered Thai roofs, a gold chedi and original tuk-tuks. The river is an adapted Chao Phraya backdrop.

## Hanoi

- [Vietnam National Authority of Tourism: Hanoi city guide](https://vietnam.travel/node/915): Hoan Kiem Lake, Turtle Tower, The Huc Bridge, Ngoc Son Temple and tube-house streets.
- [Vietnam Tourism: Hanoi walking routes](https://vietnam.travel/node/187): Opera House and French Quarter architecture.
- [Official Hanoi 360 tour](https://vietnam.travel/sites/default/files/360Tour/HaNoi/index.htm): primary visual reference for lake, bridge, tower and Opera House composition.
- The scene includes an elliptical green lake, authored three-tier arched Turtle Tower, a curved red bridge and temple island, and a yellow classical Opera House. Lake and street dimensions are adapted for visibility and racing clearance.

## Viewed real-scene photographs

- [Bangkok Art Biennale official venues](https://www.bkkartbiennale.com/venues): the Wat Arun river panorama was viewed to compare gray porcelain tiers, satellite prangs, layered red roofs, riverside walls and long-tail boats.
- [Vietnam Tourism: Old Quarter street photograph](https://vietnam.travel/sites/default/files/inline-images/Hanoi%20Old%20Quarter%20streets.jpg), by Christian Berg, published in the official guide above: viewed to compare narrow yellow shop houses, wooden shutters, mature street trees and dense wires.
- [Vietnam News Agency/VietnamPlus: Hoan Kiem lake](https://en.vietnamplus.vn/hanoi-ranks-25th-among-worlds-most-interesting-cities-in-2026-post341557.vnp): viewed lake and Turtle Tower photograph guided the green water, weathered multi-arched gray tower, low parapets and island composition.

Materials reuse the credited Poly Haven CC0 concrete and rock surfaces. Architecture and sign textures are generated locally; no additional network textures are required.
