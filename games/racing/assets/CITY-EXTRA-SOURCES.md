# Bangkok, Hanoi and Kobe street scenery

Original procedural models, researched 2026-10-08 and visually refined 2026-10-09. These circuits adapt city scenery into closed racing layouts; they are not surveyed replicas of public roads. No street imagery or third-party 3D models are redistributed.

## Bangkok

- [Tourism Authority of Thailand: Wat Arun](https://www.tourismthailand.org/Attraction/wat-arun-ratchawararam-ratchawaramahawihan): porcelain-clad central prang and its 82 m height, four smaller prangs, temple precinct. Some tourism descriptions round the height differently; this reconstruction uses the 82 m description.
- [UNESCO tentative list: Wat Arun](https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6821): central prang, subsidiary towers and architectural composition.
- The scene includes authored stepped white towers, porcelain-inspired colored details, gold finials, layered Thai roofs, a gold chedi and original tuk-tuks. The river is an adapted Chao Phraya backdrop.

## Hanoi

- [Vietnam National Authority of Tourism: Hanoi city guide](https://vietnam.travel/node/915): Hoan Kiem Lake, Turtle Tower, The Huc Bridge, Ngoc Son Temple and tube-house streets.
- [Vietnam Tourism: Hanoi walking routes](https://vietnam.travel/node/187): Opera House and French Quarter architecture.
- [Official Hanoi 360 tour](https://vietnam.travel/sites/default/files/360Tour/HaNoi/index.htm): primary visual reference for lake, bridge, tower and Opera House composition.
- The scene includes an elliptical green lake, authored three-tier arched Turtle Tower, a curved red bridge and temple island, and a yellow classical Opera House. Lake and street dimensions are adapted for visibility and racing clearance.

- [Vietnam Tourism: Old Quarter street photograph](https://vietnam.travel/sites/default/files/inline-images/Hanoi%20Old%20Quarter%20streets.jpg), by Christian Berg, published in the official guide above: viewed to compare narrow yellow shop houses, wooden shutters, mature street trees and dense wires.
- [Vietnam News Agency/VietnamPlus: Hoan Kiem lake](https://en.vietnamplus.vn/hanoi-ranks-25th-among-worlds-most-interesting-cities-in-2026-post341557.vnp): viewed lake and Turtle Tower photograph guided the green water, weathered multi-arched gray tower, low parapets and island composition.

Materials reuse the credited Poly Haven CC0 concrete and rock surfaces. Architecture and sign textures are generated locally; no additional network textures are required.

## Street-level reconstruction, 2026-10-09

The following photographs were opened and inspected as actual pixels, rather than relying on captions alone. They are reference-only: none is copied into a shop texture, the runtime assets, or the release package. The street blocks are original, adapted closed-course architecture; their coordinates are not a street survey. Original store names are generic local-language names and do not claim to reproduce photographed businesses.

### Kobe / Kaigan-dori and the Former Foreign Settlement

- [Kobe Tourism Bureau / FeelKobe: Former Foreign Settlement architecture walk](https://www.feel-kobe.jp/model_course/modelcourse_04/) and its photographs of the [Chartered Building](https://www.feel-kobe.jp/wp-content/uploads/%E3%83%81%E3%83%A3%E3%83%BC%E3%82%BF%E3%83%BC%E3%83%89%E3%83%93%E3%83%AB.jpg) and [Mitsui OSK Building](https://www.feel-kobe.jp/wp-content/uploads/%E5%95%86%E8%88%B9%E4%B8%89%E4%BA%95%E3%83%93%E3%83%AB%E3%83%87%E3%82%A3%E3%83%B3%E3%82%B0.jpg). These official photographs were viewed; no open redistribution license is asserted.
- Chartered Building: viewed the tall lower Ionic colonnade, stone facing with horizontal joints, heavy band above the columns, and relatively plain upper rectangular windows. Original geometry distinguishes the tall lower facade from the simpler upper floors.
- Mitsui OSK Building: viewed the rusticated stone lower floors, warm buff upper facing, paired tall windows, vertical pilasters, projecting cornice with dentils beneath the top floor, and curved corner bay with a rounded upper pediment. Original geometry preserves these different masses and their layered shadow lines; it is not factory architectural CAD or an exact facade survey.
- `world-city-kobe-streets.js` adds these masonry offices and original local cafe/office blocks, vending machines, short posts and planters. It does not duplicate the separately modeled Port Tower or Maritime Museum.
- Native reference captures: `/private/tmp/racing-asian-kobe-chartered-source.png`, `/private/tmp/racing-asian-kobe-mitsui-source.png`.

### Rear streets and resource budget

Each Bangkok/Hanoi primary shop-house block has a separately checked low-rise rear street with a paved service courtyard, narrow storage/workshop bays, shutters, AC units, downpipes, two parked scooters and small planted areas. Warm, varied plaster tones, uneven weathering and gutter-base staining distinguish these streets from new white buildings. Kobe adds two connecting cafe/office blocks beside the masonry offices and four modest rear-office courtyards with original white service vans. These are finite authored street masses using the viewed architectural vocabulary, not claims to reproduce particular photographed courtyards or businesses.

Only the selected city is constructed. The complete extra-landmark module, including the pre-existing heroes, uses 21 material draw batches for Bangkok/Hanoi and 9 for Kobe. Geometry counts after the rear-street refinement are Bangkok 88,216 desktop / 73,476 mobile triangles; Hanoi 81,112 / 70,196; Kobe 14,090 / 13,482. The new rear streets reuse the existing material and atlas batches and add no network requests. Kobe's one local atlas is 1024×512 desktop / 512×256 mobile. These are implementation budgets, not measured frame-rate claims.

The six desktop/mobile city geometry tests passed after the final rear-street change: finite vertices and indices, racing-surface clearance, bounded material batches and resource disposal. The preceding native visual pass inspected all three cities at the starting and near-street views, including the continuous rounded Mitsui corner; the final unified-cache native pass is performed during integration.
