# BMW X3 M50 G45: dedicated original model

Checked 2026-10-09. The user selected the new G45, introduced for model year 2025. `cars-bmw-x3.js` builds its own body, glazing and optical geometry. It does not resize the shared sports-car/SUV shell. This is an original visual reconstruction for the game, not BMW CAD or a vehicle scan.

## References and dimensions

- [BMW's 2025 X3 launch photo gallery](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/usa/photo/compilation/T0443207EN_US/the-all-new-2025-bmw-x3): front, side and rear proportions, the rising glazing belt, thin rear pillars, rolled shoulders, three-bar octagonal kidneys, opposed L/X rear optics, sculpted hatch and black M Sport bumper.
- [BMW technical data and dimensional drawing](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/deutschland/article/attachment/T0442377DE/618967): length 4.755 m, width 1.920 m, height 1.660 m and wheelbase 2.865 m. Front/rear overhangs are .880/1.010 m, so the axles sit at +1.4975/−1.3675 m around the body's longitudinal centre.
- [BMW wheel and equipment catalogue](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/deutschland/article/attachment/T0443865DE/649862), with [BMW's launch description](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/canada/article/detail/T0443201EN/the-all-new-2025-bmw-x3): the selected visual configuration uses optional 21-inch Style 1037M wheels, with 255/40 R21 front and 285/35 R21 rear tyres. Calculated tyre radii are .36870/.36645 m; the rim radius is .26670 m.
- [BMW front design image](https://bmw.scene7.com/is/image/BMW/g45-ice-mp_posi:16to7?fmt=webp-alpha&wid=1919&fit=constrain%2C1) and [rear design image](https://bmw.scene7.com/is/image/BMW/g45_ice_mp_ext_m-sport-diff_fb?fmt=webp&qlt=80&wid=1024): physical lens height, corner wrap and the wide, deep plate depression. Some manufacturer studio images are CGI; they are design references.

Reference photographs remain private research material and are not downloaded by or included in the game. Body surfaces, light guides, wheel geometry, roundel-like geometry and APEX closed-course plate artwork were drawn/generated by the project. No third-party vehicle mesh or factory bitmap badge was copied into this model.

## Construction and resource limits

The side shell has independent shoulder roll, door waist, sill and rounded square wheel arches. The bonnet, roof, windscreen, rear glass, window pillars, rear-window sill and sculpted rear hatch are bounded meshes with physical junctions. The rear plate depression is part of the hatch surface. Headlamp and tail-light housings wrap around the real outer shoulders; their thick LED guides are flat moulded surfaces rather than floating tubes. Four enclosed wheel wells and separate front/rear tyre sizes remain visible from low angles.

End-cap boundaries use the same three-dimensional edge vertices as the adjoining side/top grids. This fixes the triangular openings caused by separately projecting subdivided cap edges around a tight corner. The painted front cap is explicitly symmetric so hole triangulation cannot fill one outer air curtain while leaving the other open. Surface normals come from the underlying continuous curves rather than sparse triangle averages.

Both textures are small original RGBA data: a 64×128 contact mask with four darker tyre-contact patches, and a 256×64 APEX plate. All materials use zero true transmission, so the model requests no extra transmission pass. Opaque privacy glazing is an intentional mobile-friendly approximation. Tiny optical components remain artistic approximations; the counts do not imply a frame-rate guarantee.

The constructor creates only the selected car. Static meshes merge by material, while steering, wheel spin and suspension stay separate. The existing `{ group, dimensions, setPaint, update, dispose }` interface is preserved. Switching cars can dispose every owned live geometry, material and generated texture exactly once.

## Verification

`node --test games/racing/cars-bmw-x3.test.mjs` passes 11 tests covering both quality levels: real concave waist and shoulder roll, deep plate recess, glass bounds, physical lamp wrap, symmetric open front air curtains, rear-quarter closure from both sides, plate UV/first-hit visibility, four tyre-contact patches, steering/reverse wheel spin/suspension/brake/paint behaviour, finite geometry, resource budgets and idempotent disposal.

Silent native-browser review used a fresh `cars-bmw-x3.js?x3-body=13` entry. Six desktop angles were saved as `/private/tmp/x3-g45-final-{front,rear,front-direct,rear-direct,side,top}.png`, plus the same six with `final-white-` and three mobile-LOD views with `final-phone-{front,rear,side}.png`. They are actual browser-rendered screenshots, with body shadows enabled; no reference-photo texture or generated mock render substitutes for the model.
