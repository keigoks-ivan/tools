# Rear-light visual references

Checked 2026-10-09. These are observations of the actual pixels in official manufacturer imagery and explicitly identified authorised-dealer material, not measurements or licensed factory geometry. Reference downloads stay in `/private/tmp`; none are bundled in the public game. The illustrations on some product pages may be studio renders. Visual proportions are not engineering dimensions.

The lamp assembly must include a shallow, body-following dark housing and a larger physical lens surface. The luminous signature occupies only part of that surface. The earlier floating red tubes did not establish a faithful rear lamp, even when their LED pattern is recognizable. Separately model body cut-out, housing, lens, internal dark/clear compartments and narrow luminous inlay. Keep reverse-light chambers and low bumper reflectors distinct from the main rear-light signature.

- **Physical boundary:** Each side is one very wide, shallow red/smoked lens, a tapered inner wedge under the trunk edge and a taller, rounded outer corner that wraps onto the quarter. The trunk seam cuts the assembly near its outer end. It is substantially broader than the light fibres alone.
- **Housing/interior:** Near-black upper chamber, dark lower chamber, red translucent boundary; a clear horizontal reverse-light compartment sits low inside the inner section. Red is not a solid rectangle filling the entire lens.
- **Signature:** Three to four closely spaced, extremely thin horizontal red fibres cross the upper half. Fine lower red strokes form the folded/angled fibre pattern, with darker gaps between them. Follow the observed LCI laser-fibre pattern instead of the old thick generic L outline.
- **Placement:** Flush directly below the trunk lip, outboard of the number plate. No illuminated bridge across the central painted trunk and plate recess. Low vertical bumper reflector is a separate part.
