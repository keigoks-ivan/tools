# Racing audio credits

**REDLINE / 144 — APEX Racing original score**

Original 144 BPM electronic rock composition, 64 bars / 1 minute 47 seconds.
The arrangement includes stereo power guitars, bass, lead melodies, drums,
arpeggios, fills, transitions, a breakdown and a final chorus. All instruments
were generated mathematically by `scripts/render-racing-music.py`; no samples,
third-party songs, or licensed recordings were used. The streaming MP3 keeps
music synthesis work off the phone.

Engine, intake, turbo, gear shift, tyre, wind, surface and collision sounds are
original Web Audio synthesis in `audio.mjs`. Per-car engine layouts guide the
timbre. These are approximations, not recordings or endorsements from the
vehicle manufacturers.
