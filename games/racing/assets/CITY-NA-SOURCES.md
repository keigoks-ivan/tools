# North American city scenery references

`world-city-america.js` contains original procedural geometry and small original
canvas signs. The following primary sources guided architectural proportions and
recognisable city features, checked on 8 October 2026. No website photographs or
third-party landmark models are redistributed.

The surrounding city streets and landmark placements are adapted to original
closed racing circuits, rather than exact drivable reproductions of city roads.

## San Francisco

- [Golden Gate Bridge Highway and Transportation District — Design & Construction Stats](https://www.goldengate.org/bridge/history-research/statistics-data/design-construction-stats/): 1,280-metre main span, 343-metre side spans, 227-metre towers and 67-metre clearance, suspension cables and deck truss.
- [SFMTA — Cable Cars](https://www.sfmta.com/getting-around/muni/cable-cars): the city's preserved cable cars and the bridge's International Orange paint.
- [SFMTA — Historic cable car livery](https://archives.sfmta.com/cms/apress/SanFranciscosNewestCableCarEvokesEarthquakeDays.htm): red-and-cream Powell Street car details.
- [San Francisco Travel — Painted Ladies](https://www.sftravel.com/things-to-do/attractions/iconic-sf/painted-ladies): pastel Victorian frontage, bays, decorative trim and stepped street setting.

Photograph inspected as pixels on 8 October 2026:

- [Royal Pacific Motor Inn — Painted Ladies photograph](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5d3b3c47fa024000019ca6f1/1578111172092-P9IGWH2QD5XMM9HH9PPN/paintedladies.jpg?format=1000w), published on the [hotel's San Francisco attractions page](https://royalpacificmotorinn.com/location): steep filled gables, prominent bay windows, pastel green/pink/ochre paint and light decorative timber trim. Used for visual comparison alongside San Francisco Travel's architectural description; the filled gables and textured paint were improved after inspection.

## New York

- [New York City Department of Transportation — Brooklyn Bridge](https://www.nyc.gov/html/dot/html/bridges/brooklyn_bridge.shtml): stone Gothic towers, suspension cables, approximately 486.3-metre main span and 41.1-metre clearance.
- [Empire State Building — Facts & Figures](https://www.esbnyc.com/about/facts-figures): 443-metre total height including antenna and approximately 380-metre upper roof, reflected in the stepped Art Deco landmark model.

The brownstones, fire escapes and parked yellow taxis are original city details.

Photograph inspected as pixels on 8 October 2026:

- [Empire State Building — Official daytime skyline photograph](https://www.esbnyc.com/sites/default/files/2020-01/ESB%20Day.jpg), published on [Facts & Figures](https://www.esbnyc.com/about/facts-figures): stone Art Deco setbacks, tight vertical window rows, cylindrical upper crown and slender antenna above surrounding midrise blocks. The full-height landmark now sits on a grounded background city shore so the racing camera can show its complete silhouette beyond the river.

The Empire State facade uses an original 128-pixel tiled window/masonry atlas
with 4.4-metre bay and 3.6-metre storey UV spacing. This keeps fine window rows
readable at street-camera distances and replaces thousands of separate window
boxes while retaining geometric setbacks, vertical fins and cornices.

## Vancouver

- [Destination Vancouver — Canada Place](https://www.destinationvancouver.com/things-to-do/listings/canada-place): waterfront convention centre and white sail roof landmark.
- [Destination Vancouver — Vancouver By Water](https://www.destinationvancouver.com/inspirations/outdoors/vancouver-by-water): Burrard Inlet, North Shore mountain backdrop and glass-covered skyline.
- [Destination Vancouver — Greater Vancouver](https://www.destinationvancouver.com/vancouver/greater-vancouver-area): coastal mountains and harbour setting.
- [Destination Vancouver — Watch seaplanes taking off/landing](https://www.destinationvancouver.com/things-to-do/listings/watch-seaplanes-taking-offlanding): harbour floatplane setting.

Photograph inspected as pixels on 8 October 2026:

- [Destination Vancouver — Canada Place Sails photograph](https://images.spaicelabs.com/images/flus6j8v/production/a27fffea1adcc9230fbefa2b8a1e37b56ea66a68-1200x800.jpg?fit=max&fm=jpg&q=75&w=1200), linked from [Canada Place](https://www.destinationvancouver.com/things-to-do/listings/canada-place): five white fabric crests, single high mast per sail, concave hanging edges, tension cables, glass frontage and layered waterfront railings. The roof was revised to five single-ended crests rather than six equal double-ended peaks.

The five fabric sails, reclaimed pier, seawall railing and waterfront brick
station frontage are original geometry. Distant mountain ridges use the active
world's existing shared CC0 rock scan. All city architecture is merged by material
and generated only when its track is selected; mobile versions reduce subdivisions
and omit alternating background-storey window rows.

Reference photographs remain temporary inspection files and are not distributed
with the game. All boats, floatplanes, docks, shores and skyline meshes are
original lightweight geometry; water uses the shared original 128-pixel wave
normal map generated only for the selected city.
