# Original racing audio

**REDLINE / 144** is an original 64-bar, 144 BPM electronic rock instrumental
created for this game. The composition, arrangement, synthesized guitars,
bass, lead, drums and effects were generated by the included
`scripts/render-racing-music.py`. No recorded samples, sample libraries,
third-party music, or melodies from existing songs were used.

The real-time engine, tyre, wind, surface, shift and collision effects in
`audio.mjs` are original mathematical synthesis. Engine timbres approximate
engine configurations; they are not recordings from or endorsed by vehicle
manufacturers.
