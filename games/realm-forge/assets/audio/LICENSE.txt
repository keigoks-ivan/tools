Realm Forge sound effects: third-party recordings, all released under CC0 1.0 Universal
(public domain dedication, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
CC0 needs no attribution; sources are listed so every file can be traced and re-checked.

Each .mp3 here is a mono, 32 kHz, trimmed, loudness-matched and MP3-encoded excerpt (or a simple layered mix) of the original file(s) named below.
The untouched downloads are kept outside the repository.
