# 球場材質 v46

Created on 2026-10-04 with the built-in image_gen tool.

- `turf-v46.webp`: short turf albedo detail, resized to 1024 × 1024 for browser delivery.
- `clay-v46.webp`: infield clay albedo detail, resized to 1024 × 1024 for browser delivery.

The images supply surface detail. Mowing patterns, field lines, dirt boundaries and playing dimensions remain separate in `stadium.js` and `logic.js`.

## Turf prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless albedo texture tile for a professional 3D baseball field. Primary request: a top-down orthographic material scan of very short, dense, freshly maintained baseball turf grass. Full square frame entirely filled with fine overlapping cut grass blades, realistic natural variations in forest green and olive green, tiny dry cuttings and subtle organic grain, consistent fine scale across the frame, approximately a 1 meter square surface. Uniform diffuse neutral illumination, flat exposure, no baked directional shadows, no specular shine. All four edges should tile seamlessly with each other. No mowing stripes, no field lines, no dirt, no perspective or horizon, no objects, no text, no logos, no borders. Photo-real surface detail at high resolution, intended to be repeated as subtle color microdetail over separately modeled mowing patterns.

## Clay prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless albedo texture tile for a professional 3D baseball infield. Primary request: a top-down orthographic material scan of compact, carefully groomed reddish brown baseball infield clay. Full square frame entirely filled with fine compact terracotta soil, tiny crushed clay granules, sparse tiny sand grains, subtle natural mottling and fine raked microtexture, realistic professional ballpark dirt, approximately a 1 meter square surface. Warm medium umber and muted terracotta, no bright orange. Uniform diffuse neutral illumination, flat exposure, no baked directional shadows or shiny highlights. All four edges should tile seamlessly with each other. No rocks larger than fine gravel, no cracks, no grass, no footprints, no field lines, no perspective or horizon, no objects, no text, no logos, no borders. Photo-real material surface detail at high resolution.
