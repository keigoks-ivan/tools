## 2. Per-game detail

### 2.1 Airline Manager 4 (now titled "Airline Manager - 2026" on iOS)
- Publisher/dev: Trophy Games (Steam page); iOS listing publisher shows Xombat ApS [W: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1641650/Airline_Manager_4/ ; https://apps.appfollow.io/ios/airline-manager-2026/1487669805?country=us]. Steam launch 23 Jun 2021, free-to-play [W Steam].
- Core loop: start with $10M and a ~$4M starter plane; buy planes, pay route fees, buy fuel and CO2 quotas [W: https://www.levelwinner.com/airline-manager-2023-guide-tips-tricks-strategies/ via search snippet]. Plane flies in real world duration and earns on arrival [W: reviewer at https://steamcommunity.com/id/asmodea666ancient/recommended?l=danish].
- Modes: EASY (simplified) or REALISM (runway length etc.) [W Steam].
- Features: 360+ aircraft, 3,600-4,000 airports, live flight tracking on interactive map, maintenance scheduling, seat configuration, staff, marketing, alliances, stock investments, leaderboards [W Steam; appfollow].
- Fuel/CO2: prices refresh every 30 min, buy low to store; this "adds strategic depth" per a reviewer [W steamcommunity link above]. Fuel efficiency in lbs/km compared by players (8 vs 15 lbs/km = 1.875x fuel cost) [W https://steamcommunity.com/app/1641650/discussions/0/5234891274081061723].
- Scores: Steam 71% positive of 1,769 total, recent 81% of 38 [W Steam]; iOS 4.7 stars, 5,093 ratings [W appfollow].
- Fun: casual check-in rhythm, balanced economy, player stock market, ~30h played in short sessions [W Steam reviewer].
- Tedium: cannot swap planes on a route, must buy extra aircraft [W appfollow]; players want seasonal profit swings and weather [W appfollow]; single profile per account [W reviewer]; community dismissive of suggestions [W appfollow].
- Visual: minimalist, "clearly designed for mobile" UI [W reviewer]. Flat live map with moving plane icons [M for icon detail].
- Monetisation: IAP; reviewer spent ~$8, felt no pressure, boosts "aren't required" [W reviewer]. Premium currency and ad-for-reward details not verified; [M] the game sells "Airline Cash" and offers ads for rewards.
- Realism: tells you fuel/CO2 cost, seats, price; no strong competitor-AI modelling because rivals are real players [M].

### 2.2 Fly Corp
- Dev KishMish Games, released 18 May 2023 on Steam, Windows+macOS, Very Positive 86% of 1,861 reviews; free demo [W https://store.steampowered.com/app/1372530/Fly_Corp/]. Later reported publisher HeroCraft and Switch/other-platform release Jan 2025; ~85% of 3,332 reviews on one platform [W search result https://ladiesgamers.com/fly-corp-review/ and search snippet].
- Core loop: open routes, buy/upgrade planes, raise airport capacity; ~200 territories, tens of thousands of real cities with real populations driving demand [W Steam].
- Passenger model: each passenger has a destination and takes layovers if no direct route [W Steam]. This is the hub-transfer lesson.
- Modes: Discover the World (new territory unlocks every 6 min, leaderboard), Challenges (preset scenarios), daily leaderboard scenario, free play, scenario editor and sharing [W Steam; ladiesgamers].
- Random events, good and bad (investment, disasters halting flights, a coffee-spill lawsuit) [W search snippet].
- Visual: minimalist dots and dotted lines; simple illustrations for events [W https://ladiesgamers.com/fly-corp-review/]. Comparison to Mini Metro [W same]. NOT a 3D globe in the sources I read; I could not verify the "globe" claim in your brief.
- Fun: management balance, realistic cities, mode variety [W ladiesgamers].
- Tedium: repeated events; online-only pop-ups for leaderboards; touch-only on Switch [W ladiesgamers].
- Progression feel: early game encourages rapid expansion, money gets tight later [W ladiesgamers].
- Monetisation: premium purchase plus DLC; no timers-to-pay [W Steam]. (Territory timer is a game-mode clock, not a paywall.)

### 2.3 AirTycoon 4 (ATO4 / "AirTycoon 4")
- Developer: TRADEGAME Lab Inc., South Korean studio; iOS launch June 2015, last update June 2019; $2.99 on App Store, iOS 8+, 245 MB [W https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id989733380 ; https://appgoblin.info/apps/989733380]. Not on Steam per my searches.
- Description: "authentic turn-based airline management simulation single player", based on Air Tycoon Online 2; up to 24 AI players, 150 real aircraft, 500 airports, 3D graphics, passenger and cargo routes, buy/sell used planes, upgradable planes and airports, no internet needed [W App Store + search results].
- Ratings: 3.9/5 from 117 (US) reviews [W App Store]; 3.95 from 286 ratings, ~14k installs, 246 MAU, no ads, revenue 100% IAP [W appgoblin].
- Player complaints: game ends in 2030 so late aircraft are missing; hard to close routes; landing mode mechanics; IAP currency does not carry across save files [W App Store]. Liked: strategic depth, long-term playability [W App Store].
- Developer tips on the page (good for teaching): keep leased planes below 30% of fleet, maximise aircraft utilisation, keep adjusting fares to occupancy [W App Store]. Sibling ATO2 text: raise fare at 100% occupancy, cut it below 70% [W https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id969924164].
- Hub building: ATO2 lets you buy or build airports, add a maintenance depot and VIP lounge at the hub [W ATO2 page]. ATO3 text: headquarters city chosen at start, branch offices possible [W https://www.bluestacks.com/apps/simulation/airtycoon-online-3-on-pc.html via search snippet].
- Cabin config: ATO2 says you adjust plane seats and cabin service quality [W ATO2 page]. Exact class split (economy/business/first) not confirmed.
- Landing Simulator mini-game, alliances, code-shares exist in ATO2 [W ATO2 page]. For AT4 "landing mode" is cited in complaints [W App Store].
- Slots / airport capacity: NOT confirmed in any page I could open (wiki pages blocked with HTTP 402). Treat as [M]/unknown.
- AI: for the earlier AirTycoon (a different game) a review called the AI sharp and interacting cleverly with the world [W http://www.appspy.com/review/3916/airtycoon-airline-management/]. Not verified for AT4.
- Scenarios/campaign: not found. Multiplayer: none in AT4 (single-player, offline); ATO2/ATO3 are the online versions [W].
- Visual style: "vivid and gorgeous 3D graphics" is the marketing line [W]. An older AirTycoon review calls the look "bland" and menu-driven, with slide-bar controls [W appspy]. No source describes the map, colours or panel layout of AT4. Anything I tell you about screens is [M]: flat-ish world map with 3D aircraft models, menu-driven lists and slider bars, dense numeric panels.
- Monetisation in ATO2 (the online sibling): credit packs $4.99-$99.99 plus $9.99 Pro Channel; users complain credits run dry and buying is the only way [W ATO2 page]. AT4 has no ads, IAP only [W appgoblin].

## 3. Most transferable ideas

1. Passengers as visible dots waiting at airports, overflow = lost revenue (from Mini Metro and Fly Corp) - teaches capacity vs demand and spill.
2. Self-routing transfer passengers: a hub fills only when spokes connect (from Fly Corp) - teaches hub-and-spoke feeder traffic.
3. A fare slider with the live rule "raise at 100% full, cut under 70%" shown as a gauge (from AirTycoon 4 / ATO2) - teaches load factor vs yield and price elasticity.
4. Break-even load factor line drawn on every route's seat meter (synthesised; leased-share and utilisation tips from AirTycoon 4) - teaches break-even load factor.
5. Fuel price ticker with a "lock now / buy later" choice (from Airline Manager 4) - teaches fuel hedging; use game turns, not 30-min real timers.
6. Fleet utilisation bar per plane, idle hours shown in grey (from AirTycoon 4's utilisation tip and Airline Manager 4's flight tracking) - teaches utilisation.
7. Lease vs buy with a visible "lease share" warning beyond 30% (from AirTycoon 4's tip) - teaches leasing risk vs ownership.
8. Hub upgrade menu: lounge, maintenance depot, extra gates (from AirTycoon Online 2/3) - teaches hub economics and slots/capacity.
9. End-of-season pick-one-of-three upgrade/event card (from Mini Metro weekly choice and Fly Corp random events) - teaches trade-offs, with seasonality and shock cards (fuel spike, rival low-cost entry).
10. Scenario cards with fixed goals plus a daily seed, no timers to pay (from Fly Corp challenges/daily scenarios) - teaches that outcomes depend on conditions.
11. Easy vs Realism toggle (from Airline Manager 4) - lets novices skip runway-length and slot rules at first.

## 4. Avoid list
1. Real-time price/refresh timers that force check-ins (Airline Manager 4 fuel 30-minute refresh).
2. Credit/currency packs and a subscription that bypass progress (AirTycoon Online 2 credits; Pro Channel).
3. IAP currency that does not carry between saves (AirTycoon 4 complaint).
4. Hard end year that kills late content (AirTycoon 4: 2030).
5. Locked decisions you cannot undo, such as no plane swap on a route (Airline Manager 4) or hard-to-close routes (AirTycoon 4).
6. Repeating random events with no variety (Fly Corp).
7. Mandatory online pop-ups for leaderboards (Fly Corp, Switch).
8. Late-game monotony from numeric menus only (earlier AirTycoon review).

## 6. Addendum (coordinator check, 2026-10-05): AirTycoon series detail and corrections

Added after the user asked for AirTycoon 4 to be covered as thoroughly as the other two. Same labels: [W] = found this session, [W-snippet] = search-result text only (page itself blocked), [M] = memory.

- **AirTycoon 4 facts confirmed again** [W: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/airtycoon-4/id989733380]: turn-based, single player, offline; up to 24 AI rivals; 150 real aircraft, 500 airports; upgradable planes and airports; buy and sell used planes; passenger and cargo routes; "3D graphics and detailed world map"; maintenance depot and lounge only at hub airports (depot saves 30% maintenance cost, lounge raises business and first class passengers); timeline ends in 2030. US rating 3.9 from 117 ratings; IAP game-cash packs US$0.99 and US$2.99. Review complaints: end date too early, aircraft stop production without notice, route-closing bugs, IAP cash does not move between saves, the landing mini-game feels punitive.
- **Air Tycoon Online 2 (AirTycoon 4 is the offline version of it)** [W-snippet: namu.wiki pages on 에어타이쿤 온라인 2, fetched pages returned HTTP 403]: hub facilities bought with credits are fuel tank, maintenance shop and lounge; the start city gives 20 slots; you then buy the first plane and open the first route. Treat as unverified detail.
- **AirTycoon 5** [W: https://apps.appfollow.io/ios/airtycoon-5/1406253554?country=us ; search result text]: lists a 2D/3D map select, ~170 aircraft that unlock over time, slots that grow with airport level (request up to 25 at a time), takeovers of rival companies, per-city "name value" raised by advertising (branch offices at 50), detailed management reports. Rated 2.3 (97 reviews); complaints: restrictive hub system, crashes and lost progress, awkward close buttons, thin airport coverage, profit impossible late in the game.
- **Correction to the brief**: no source shows a 3D globe in AirTycoon 4. The 3D map is an AirTycoon 5 feature. Fly Corp's sources describe a flat map with dots and dotted lines, not a globe. The globe in both SKYGLAZE directions is our own choice, built from NASA imagery and the flight-sim art; it is not copied from either game.
- **What we take from AirTycoon 4/5 into direction B**: hub facilities with a stated effect (depot −30% maintenance, lounge more premium passengers, fuel tank to store fuel), terminal level that raises the slot cap, buy vs lease vs used aircraft, cabin class split, turn-based seasons, several AI rivals, the developer's own tips as in-game warnings (lease share under 30%, fares follow occupancy). What we leave out: hard end year, landing mini-game, paid cash, routes that are hard to close.
