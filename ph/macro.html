Bottom line總結 The Philippines is the mirror image of the ageing ASEAN economies. The population is still growing, real income per head has compounded faster in each successive decade — 2.84% in the 2000s, 3.03% in the 2010s, 4.91% from 2020 to 2025 — and the external balance sheet has been rebuilt from an 85.9%-of-GDP debt crisis in 1986 to 30.3% in 2025. None of the structural decline that defines Thailand is present here. Growth has slowed in 2026, to 2.8% in the first quarter and 2.3% in the second.

The problem is elsewhere, and it is specific. Bank lending against Metro Manila residential property broke in 2020 and has not been repaired. Approved mortgage counts in the capital fell from 21,925 in 2019 to 11,041 in 2025, a decline of 49.6%, while the rest of the country rose 15.8% over the same period. The second quarter of 2026 printed 2,967 in the capital, 11% above a year earlier and still under half the 2019 peak of 6,494. The past-due ratio on residential real-estate loans doubled from 5.74% at end-2019 to 12.20% at end-2020 and, six years later, is still 8.83% against a 2013–2017 baseline near 3.4%. Commercial-property delinquency is 2.81%, about a third of the residential ratio. The damage is concentrated in one place: residential credit in the capital.

Two things fill the gap and both are cash rather than credit. Remittances reached US$39.6 billion in 2025 and grew 2.3% in January–July 2026. Because the peso lost a third of its value against the dollar over thirteen years, the same dollars now buy far more Philippine property than they did. The second is foreign capital, which the 1987 Constitution confines to condominium units under a 40% collective ceiling. Neither channel runs through a loan officer, which is why the market has not cleared through price. The 2026 backdrop is less friendly to both: the BSP has raised its policy rate three times to 5.00%, September inflation is 7.2%, and the peso closed at a record low of 62.90 on 8 October. Those mechanics are tested against the transaction data in the companion Metro Manila Property Market Report. 菲律賓與東協的高齡化經濟體正好相反。人口仍在成長。實質人均所得的年均複合成長率逐個十年加快：2000 年代 2.84%、2010 年代 3.03%、2020 到 2025 年 4.91%。對外資產負債表也已重建，外債占 GDP 的比重從 1986 年債務危機時的 85.9%，降到 2025 年的 30.3%。界定泰國處境的各種結構性衰退，這裡都沒有。2026 年成長放慢，第一季 2.8%，第二季 2.3%。

問題在別處，而且範圍明確。針對大馬尼拉住宅的銀行貸款在 2020 年斷掉，之後沒有修復。首都的房貸核准件數從 2019 年的 21,925 件降到 2025 年的 11,041 件，減少 49.6%，同期全國其餘地區增加 15.8%。2026 年第二季首都核准 2,967 件，比一年前多 11%，仍不到 2019 年高點 6,494 件的一半。住宅不動產放款的逾期率，從 2019 年底的 5.74% 倍增到 2020 年底的 12.20%。六年後仍有 8.83%，2013 到 2017 年的基線約 3.4%。商用不動產放款的逾期率是 2.81%，約為住宅的三分之一。劣化集中在一個地方：首都的住宅授信。

填補缺口的有兩項來源，都是現金，不是貸款。匯款 2025 年達 396 億美元，2026 年 1 到 7 月又增加 2.3%。披索十三年來對美元貶值三分之一，同樣的美元現在能買到的菲律賓房地產多很多。第二項是外國資金，1987 年憲法把它限制在公寓單位，並受 40% 的集體上限約束。兩項資金都不經過放款人員，所以市場沒有靠價格出清。2026 年的環境對這兩項都不友善：菲律賓央行（BSP）三度升息到 5.00%，9 月通膨 7.2%，披索 10 月 8 日收 62.90，創收盤新低。這些機制與成交資料的對照，見《大馬尼拉住宅市場報告》。

Philippine headline inflation ran at 2.42% in February 2026, then 4.07% in March and 7.23% in April. It eased to 6.36% in June, 6.2% in July and 6.1% in August, then rose again to 7.2% in September. The BSP expects average headline inflation above its 4% tolerance ceiling in both 2026 and 2027. The 2022 precedent sets the expected duration2022 年的前例決定了持續多久

This is not the first negative real rate in the series. Inflation peaked at 8.68% in January 2023 and the real policy rate bottomed at −3.59% in June 2022. Part 3 · The Politics — Who Sets the Rules政治篇 · 規則由誰制定

In Thailand the foreign-ownership limit is a statute, so a parliament could move it. In the Philippines it is a constitutional article, so nothing short of amending the constitution can.在泰國，外資持有上限訂在法律，國會就能修改。在菲律賓，這個限制是憲法條文，不修憲就動不了。

The foreign-ownership limit is constitutional, which places it beyond both administration and legislation外資持有限制的位階在憲法，行政與立法都動不了

The 1987 Constitution, Article XII, Sections 7 and 81987 年憲法第十二條第 7、8 項

Section 7 prohibits the transfer of private land to anyone not qualified to acquire public land, that is, to any individual who is not a Philippine citizen or any corporation that is not at least 60% Filipino-owned, with succession the only exception. Section 8 creates a narrow further exception for natural-born citizens who have lost their citizenship. The restriction on land is therefore in the constitutional text, not in a statute.第 7 項禁止把私有土地移轉給沒有資格取得公地的人，也就是不具菲律賓籍的個人，或菲律賓籍持股不到 60% 的法人，繼承是唯一例外。第 8 項為喪失國籍的天生公民另設一項範圍很窄的例外。土地限制因此寫在憲法本文，不在法律。

The Condominium Act, RA 4726, Section 5公寓法（RA 4726）第 5 條

The 40% ceiling operates at the level of the condominium corporation that holds the common areas, not at the level of the individual unit transaction. That legal structure is why a foreign buyer can hold a condominium certificate of title at all. Section 5 makes any transfer that would take the corporation past the ceiling void, not merely refusable.40% 上限作用在持有共有部分的公寓公司層級，不在個別單位交易層級。這個法律結構，讓外國買方有可能持有公寓所有權狀（CCT）。第 5 條規定，會使公司外資比例超過上限的移轉一律無效，不只是可以拒絕。

How the 40% is counted, RA 4726 Section 6(c)40% 的計算基礎，RA 4726 第 6(c) 條

The statutory default is one equal share per unit, that is, a headcount of units rather than a floor-area weighting. This page treats the statutory default as verified and the practice as unverified, because no case-by-case check of master deeds was performed.法定預設是每單位一等分，按戶數計，不按樓地板面積加權。該條明文允許主契約另訂，一般理解以樓地板面積加權是常見的業界做法。本頁把法定預設列為已驗證，把實務做法列為未驗證，因為沒有逐案查核主契約。

Two separate long-lease regimes兩套互不相同的長期租地制度

Presidential Decree 471 of 1974 allows a general foreign lessee 25 years renewable for 25, fifty in total, for any purpose. Republic Act 7652 of 1993, as amended by Republic Act 12252 of September 2025, allows an aggregate lease of up to ninety-nine years, but only for approved and registered investments in industrial estates, export processing, agro-industry or tourism projects; residential use is not on the list. An individual buying a home falls under the first, not the second, and the two are frequently conflated.1974 年第 471 號總統令（PD 471）允許一般外國承租人租用25 年，可續約 25 年，合計 50 年，不限用途。1993 年投資人租賃法（RA 7652）經 2025 年 9 月的 RA 12252 修正後，租期合計最長 99 年，但只適用於工業區、加工出口、農工企業或觀光專案中，經核准並登記的投資，住宅用途不在範圍內。個人買房適用前者，不適用後者，兩者常被混為一談。

The practical consequence is that the entire liberalisation channel available in Thailand does not exist here. A Thai government could raise the condominium quota to 75% with an act of parliament, which is why the proposal recurs there and why every valuation that assumed it has been wrong. In the Philippines the 40% ceiling on condominium corporations exists because the corporation holds land, and the 60% Filipino floor on land-holding corporations is in Article XII. Raising the condominium ceiling requires a constitutional amendment, not a bill. For an investor the correct posture is therefore not to monitor legislative sentiment but to price the current article and treat any change as outside the investable horizon.實務結果是：泰國有的整條自由化管道，這裡不存在。泰國政府可以用國會立法把公寓配額提高到 75%，所以這個提案在當地反覆出現，所有把它當假設的估值也都被證明錯了。在菲律賓，公寓公司的 40% 上限之所以存在，是因為公司持有土地，而持有土地的法人要有 60% 菲律賓籍持股，這條規定在第十二條。提高公寓上限要修憲，不是立法。所以對投資人，正確的立場不是追蹤立法動向，而是用現行憲法條文定價，並把任何變動視為可投資期間以外的事。

Three tradeable political rules三條可交易的政治規律

One — price the constitutional article, not the reform debate. The condominium ceiling is downstream of Article XII. Charter change has been discussed in Philippine politics for decades without altering the economic provisions. A valuation that assumes a higher foreign quota is assuming a constitutional amendment, and should be labelled as such rather than as policy risk.其一，以憲法條文定價，不以改革討論定價。公寓上限是第十二條的下游規定。修憲在菲律賓政治中已討論數十年，經濟條款從未變動。把更高的外資配額放進假設的估值，其實是在假設一次修憲，應該照實標示，不要列為政策風險。

Two — the executive can remove a tenant class faster than any market can reprice. Executive Order 74 was signed on 5 November 2024 with a compliance deadline of 31 December 2024. The variables that moved this market between 2019 and 2026 were the approval count and the delinquency ratio, neither of which is set by policy. The conclusions live in the interactions關鍵結論在交互作用裡

  • E enters twice with opposite signs. A weaker peso raises R × E for the domestic remittance buyer and lowers the dollar value of the same asset for the foreign holder. The Answer — Where, What, When結論篇 · 區位、產品、時點

Locations: the capital is where the credit broke, and it is also the only place a foreigner can buy區位：授信中斷的地方就是首都，也是外國人唯一能買的地方

The geographic split in this dataset is unusually clean. Mortgage approvals outside NCR rose 15.8% between 2019 and 2025 while NCR fell 49.6%; national population compounded at 1.55% a year over the last census interval while NCR managed 0.93% and lost share. On both the credit and the demographic reading, the growth is in the provinces. But the constitutional structure sends foreign capital in the opposite direction. A foreign buyer may hold a condominium unit and may not hold land, and condominium supply outside the capital is thin, so the product that is legally available and the geography that is economically growing do not overlap. The correct posture is not to resolve that contradiction but to state it: a foreign position in Philippine residential property is a position in NCR condominium, and NCR condominium is the segment whose domestic financing channel is running at half its 2019 volume. Anyone who is not constrained by the ownership rules — a Filipino citizen or a former natural-born citizen under Article XII Section 8 — faces a materially different opportunity set from the one this page describes.本資料集的地理分野很清楚。2019 到 2025 年，首都區以外的房貸核准件數增加 15.8%，首都區減少 49.6%。最近一個普查間隔內，全國人口年均複合成長 1.55%，首都區是 0.93%，占比下降。無論從信貸端或人口端看，成長都在地方。但憲法結構把外國資金導向相反的方向。外國買方可以持有公寓單位，不能持有土地，首都以外的公寓供給很薄。所以法律上買得到的產品，和經濟上在成長的地理，兩者不重疊。正確的立場不是解決這個矛盾，而是把它講清楚：外資在菲律賓住宅的部位，就是首都區公寓的部位，而首都區公寓正是本地融資管道量能只剩 2019 年一半不到的那一塊。不受持有規則約束的人，也就是菲律賓公民，或第十二條第 8 項所指的原天生公民，面對的機會，和本頁描述的有實質差異。

Products: rank by whether the exit buyer needs a mortgage產品：依出場買方是否需要房貸排序

The ranking principle in this market is not quality and not location. It is whether the eventual resale requires a bank to approve someone. A unit that will be resold to a remittance-funded cash buyer sits behind a channel that grew 111% in peso terms between 2013 and 2025 and has fallen in only one of the twenty-one years held, by 0.82% in 2020. A unit that will be resold to a mortgage applicant sits behind a channel that halved in the capital and carries an 8.83% past-due ratio. The same building can contain both. Average household size gives the practical filter: 3.83 persons in NCR and 3.35 in Makati, against 4.12 nationally, which is the demographic case for the smaller unit rather than the family-sized one. On leasehold structures, the applicable regime for an individual is Presidential Decree 471 at twenty-five years renewable for twenty-five, not the investor lease under RA 7652, which as amended runs to ninety-nine years, and the two are routinely conflated in marketing material. Verifying which regime a specific arrangement falls under is a legal question that precedes any pricing question.這個市場的排序原則，不是品質，也不是區位，而是未來轉售時要不要銀行批准某個人。轉售給靠匯款買房的現金買方的單位，背後的管道在 2013 到 2025 年以披索計成長 111%，21 年資料中只有 2020 年減少一次，幅度 0.82%。轉售給房貸申請人的單位，背後的管道在首都腰斬，逾期率是 8.83%。同一棟大樓可以同時包含兩者。平均家戶規模提供實務上的篩選：首都區 3.83 人、馬卡蒂 3.35 人，全國 4.12 人，這是偏向小坪數、不偏向家庭房型的人口論據。租賃制度方面，適用於個人的是 PD 471，25 年，可續約 25 年，不是 RA 7652 的投資人租賃（修正後最長 99 年），行銷資料常把兩者混用。確認特定安排適用哪一套制度是法律問題，順序在任何定價問題之前。

What to watch觀察重點

  • NCR approved mortgage counts, quarterly.首都區房貸核准件數，季頻。 The single highest-information series in this market. The latest reading is 2,967 (2Q26). 本市場資訊量最高的單一序列。連續四季高於 4,000 件，模型要改寫。跌破 2,000 件，壓力情境就提前。最新讀數 2,967 件（2026Q2）。
  • Residential past-due ratio versus commercial.住宅逾期率對照商用類。 The gap is the whole diagnosis: 8.83% against 2.81% in 2026Q1. Convergence in either direction is the signal. 兩者的差距就是全部診斷：2026Q1 為 8.83% 對 2.81%。任一方向的收斂，都是訊號。
  • Headline CPI against the policy rate.CPI 年增對照政策利率。 The market responds to the real rate. September CPI of 7.2% against a policy rate of 5.00% gives a real rate of −2.20%, as deep as April.   • Monthly personal remittances.月頻個人匯款。 Nineteen increases in twenty annual changes; the one decline was 0.82% in 2020. January–July 2026 is up 2.3%.   • PHP per USD and the BIS real effective rate together.披索兌美元與 BIS 實質有效匯率併同觀察。 A weaker peso with a stable real effective rate is an inflation differential.
