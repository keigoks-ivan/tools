Bottom line總結 A foreign buyer in Manila is not choosing among the properties on the market. The 1987 Constitution removes land from the choice set entirely, and what remains is one product — the condominium unit — under a statutory 40% foreign cap that operates at the level of the condominium corporation, with no central register that would let anyone check how much of that cap a given building has already used.

Inside that narrow channel the record is weaker than the headline suggests. The BIS index for Makati rose 219% in nominal terms between the first quarter of 2008 and the first quarter of 2026, and 70% after inflation. Measured from 2013, the first year with a peso exchange-rate series in this dataset, the same index returns +152% in nominal pesos, +66% after Philippine inflation, and +74% in nominal US dollars, because the peso fell 31% against the dollar over the period.

The demand channel that produces the official price index is contracting. NCR recorded 2,967 bank-financed residential purchases in the second quarter of 2026 against 6,494 in the fourth quarter of 2019; over the same period the rest of the country moved from 7,025 to 6,688, having peaked at 8,758 two quarters earlier. Residential real-estate loans have carried a past-due ratio between 8.8% and 12.9% since 2021, against 2.2% to 3.0% for commercial. And the rent line, which is the entire investment case in a market with no capital-gains engine, has lost to inflation: the NCR actual-rentals index is up 24.0% since January 2018 while NCR headline consumer prices are up 34.5%. In real terms rent fell from 2021 to 2024 and has been flat since the start of 2025. The BSP has raised its policy rate three times since April, to 5.00%, and the peso closed at a record low of 62.90 on 8 October.

The exit is governed by a rule most buyers never see at purchase. The BSP Manual of Regulations on Foreign Exchange Transactions classifies the acquisition of a condominium unit as foreign direct investment and requires direct registration with the central bank within one year; registration is the precondition for repatriating capital through the banking system's foreign exchange resources. The structural background is set out in the companion Philippines Macro × Property analysis. 外國買方在馬尼拉能選的標的，遠少於市場上的全部房屋。1987 年憲法把土地完全排除在外，外國人只能買公寓。公寓受公寓法 40% 外資上限約束。這個上限算在持有大樓共有部分的公寓公司層級。沒有任何中央登記，可以查出單棟大樓已用掉多少額度。

在這條窄通道裡，實際報酬比表面數字弱。國際清算銀行（BIS）的馬卡蒂住宅價格指數，2008 年第一季到 2026 年第一季名目上漲 219%，扣掉通膨後上漲 70%。從 2013 年起算，同一指數以名目披索計上漲 152%，扣掉菲律賓通膨後上漲 66%，以名目美元計上漲 74%。同期披索兌美元貶值 31%。

官方價格指數背後的買方正在減少。首都區 2026 年第二季核准 2,967 件住宅房貸，2019 年第四季是 6,494 件。全國其餘地區同期從 7,025 件變成 6,688 件，高點是 2025 年第四季的 8,758 件。住宅放款的逾期率 2021 年以來落在 8.8% 到 12.9%，商用放款落在 2.2% 到 3.0%。租金也沒有跟上物價。首都區實付租金指數自 2018 年 1 月上漲 24.0%，同期首都區物價上漲 34.5%。扣掉物價後，租金從 2021 年降到 2024 年，2025 年初以來持平。菲律賓央行（BSP）4 月以來三度升息，政策利率到 5.00%。披索 10 月 8 日收 62.90，創收盤新低。

出場受一項多數買方在承購時沒聽過的規定約束。央行的《外匯交易法規手冊》把買公寓視為外人直接投資，要求一年內直接向央行登記。有登記，日後才能透過銀行把資金匯出。結構背景見《菲律賓總經 × 房產》。

  • Deflated by consumer prices the same series went from 107.8 to 183.4, a gain of 70.0%. Compounded that is about 3.0% a year in real terms, before transaction costs, holding taxes and vacancy. Over the thirteen years to the first quarter of 2026 the Makati index returned +152.1% in nominal pesos, +65.6% after Philippine consumer prices, and +73.6% in nominal US dollars, against a peso that lost 31.2% against the dollar over the same window.   • The RPPI is built on acquisition cost, the contract price reported by banks in the quarterly Residential Real Estate Loans return. It is a transaction-price index, not an appraisal or an asking-price index, which puts it in the top tier of price evidence available for this market.RPPI 以成交成本（acquisition cost）編製，也就是銀行在季報《住宅不動產放款》申報的合約成交價。它是成交價指數，不是鑑價，也不是掛牌價。就這個市場能取得的價格證據來說，它是最高的一層。
  • Its coverage is also its limit. Cash purchases, non-bank transactions and socialized housing are outside the sample entirely. In a market where developers extend their own instalment terms at scale, the index reports on the segment that banks financed, not on the market.它的涵蓋範圍同時也是限制。現金交易、非銀行交易和社會住宅完全不在樣本內。開發商大量自己提供分期付款的市場，這個指數只反映銀行承作的那一段，不反映整個市場。
  • The quarterly path is volatile in a way that matters for reading it. NCR ran 149.5, 148.3, 145.4, 160.5 and then 157.3 across the five quarters to 2Q26. Single-quarter moves of eight to ten index points appear repeatedly across all three geographies, so quarter-on-quarter changes carry little information and the official year-on-year rate is the more usable series.季度路徑的波動大，判讀時要留意。首都區到 2026Q2 的最近五季是 149.5、148.3、145.4、160.5、157.3。三個地理層級都反覆出現單季 8 到 10 個指數點的變動。所以季增率幾乎沒有資訊，官方公布的年增率比較好用。

A quarter earlier the order was the opposite (NCR +3.5%, the rest of the country +5.7%). Cumulatively the two geographies are close: NCR is up 63.3% from the first quarter of 2019 and the rest of the country 64.5%. Two price indices, and one of them is discontinued兩個價格指數，其中一個已停用

Before 2025 the BSP published the RREPI, computed from the appraised value per square metre of new building properties. The RPPI that replaced it is computed from acquisition cost and covers new, pre-owned and foreclosed units. The BSP metadata states the difference in terms. The two indices also use different base periods, 1Q2014=100 against 2019=100. They cannot be spliced into one series, and any long-run Philippine house-price chart that appears to run from 2016 to today on a single line has done exactly that.2025 年之前，央行發布的是 RREPI，以新建物件每平方公尺的鑑價編製。接替它的 RPPI 以成交成本編製，涵蓋新成屋、中古屋和法拍屋。央行的指數說明文件明文寫出這個差異。兩個指數的基期也不同，分別是 2014Q1＝100 與 2019＝100。兩者不能接成一條序列。任何看似從 2016 年連續畫到今天的菲律賓長期房價圖，都已經做了這種接合。

A second boundary matters as much. The NCR index aggregates the entire National Capital Region, most of whose stock a foreign buyer can neither purchase nor would want to. It is a proxy for the segment a foreigner actually faces; it is not that segment. No official sub-city index exists — the BSP hedonic model uses city dummies as internal controls and does not publish them.第二個界線同樣重要。首都區指數涵蓋整個首都區，其中大部分存量外國買方買不了，也不會想買。它只是外國人實際面對的那一塊的代理值，並非那一塊本身。官方沒有次城市層級的指數。央行的特徵價格模型把城市當作內部控制變數，沒有對外公布。

Put together, a ten-year holder is underwriting three things at once.
