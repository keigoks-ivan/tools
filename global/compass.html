Three instruments, one page三套儀器，一頁讀 A house market asks three questions that one number can't answer at once — where prices are headed, whether the market is healthy, and whether it is flashing the specific warning signs that have, historically, preceded a real loss. This page keeps them on three separate instruments. The Compass orders direction from momentum and credit — a description of the past; a later attempt to validate that ordering as a forward call failed its own pre-registered gates. The Report Card diagnoses condition — valuation, population, supply — describing health without pretending to time it. The Risk Gauge is the one instrument here with a tested forward claim: a frozen, pre-registered test found that Starved-plus-supply-glut quarters carry real, elevated odds of a loss or a bottom-quartile outcome, in the markets that have ever had one to learn from. Three jobs, three tools, never blended into a single score.一個房市同時問三個問題，而一個數字無法一次回答——價格接下來往哪走、這個市場健不健康、以及它是否亮起那種過去曾預示真實虧損的特定警示訊號。這頁把三者放在三套分開的儀器上。羅盤只用動能與信貸為方向排序——這是對過去的描述；後續一次想把這個排序驗證為前向預測的嘗試，未能通過自己預先凍結的門檻。健檢卡診斷體質——估值、人口、供給——描述健康狀況，而不假裝拿它來抓時機。風險警報是這裡唯一一套通過前向驗證的儀器：一次凍結、預先設定門檻的測試發現，斷炊疊加供給過剩的季度，在曾經出現過虧損可供學習的市場中，確實帶有真實、明顯偏高的虧損或落入最差四分之一表現的機率。三種工作、三套工具，絕不合併成單一分數。

The Risk Gauge — a Validated Downside Warning風險警報 · 已驗證的下檔警示

A third instrument, kept separate from the compass and the report card: it answers one narrow, pre-registered question — is the market flashing the specific combination (no price momentum, credit still contracting, and a live supply glut) that has, historically, preceded either a 3-year nominal loss or a bottom-quartile outcome? The classification rule and the pass/fail bars were frozen before this test ran (scripts/compass_v3_protocol.md), and on the four frozen markets it passed. Pooled across the markets that qualify for each claim, the odds ratio comes out to 13.5× for an absolute loss and 16.1× for a bottom-quartile outcome.第三套儀器，與羅盤和體檢表分開設計：它只回答一個窄、且已預先凍結的問題——市場現在是否亮起那個特定組合（房價動能消失、信貸持續收縮、且供給過剩警示正在亮燈），而這個組合過去曾預示三年期名目虧損，或落入最差四分之一表現？分類規則與通過／失敗門檻，都在這次測試跑之前就已凍結（scripts/compass_v3_protocol.md），且於凍結的四個市場通過驗證。把符合資格的市場合併計算，絕對虧損的勝算比為13.5倍，落入最差四分之一的勝算比為16.1倍。

The Compass — Momentum × Credit羅盤 · 動能 × 信貸

The horizontal axis is momentum — the recent trend in house prices; the vertical axis is credit impulse — whether mortgage and household credit is accelerating or slowing. Crossing them gives four cells: Uptrend·Fuelled (prices rising, credit accelerating), Uptrend·Draining (prices rising but credit fading), Downtrend·Reflating (prices falling but credit turning up), and Downtrend·Starved (prices falling, credit still tightening). The glowing dot marks the latest quarter; a supply-glut warning lights up separately when vacancy or completions run loose relative to the recent norm (trailing z ≤ −1), since a glut can make even a fuelled uptrend risky.橫軸是動能——房價近期的趨勢；縱軸是信貸脈衝——房貸與家庭信貸正在加速還是放緩。兩軸交叉出四格：上升·助燃（房價漲、信貸加速）、上升·抽乾（房價漲但信貸轉弱）、下降·回溫（房價跌但信貸開始回升）、下降·斷糧（房價跌、信貸仍在收緊）。發光的點是最新一季；供給過剩警示會另外亮起——當空置率或完工量相對近期常態轉為寬鬆（滾動 z ≤ −1）時，即使是助燃中的上升格也可能變得危險。

The Report Card — Health, Not Prediction體檢表 · 診斷體質，不預測

Valuation, population growth and supply sit here as separate, transparent gauges — each one shows only whether that single force looks strained or comfortable right now. They are deliberately never averaged into one score and never used to predict where prices go next; that job belongs to the compass. Valuation and supply are standardised over the market's whole history, so they read position versus that market's own past — not a cross-country absolute (a market that is always expensive, like Taiwan near 9× income, reads mid-range against itself). Population instead reads an absolute growth direction on a fixed scale, not standardised against history — the same growth rate reads the same regardless of that market's own past.估值、人口成長與供給在這裡各是一個獨立、透明的儀表——每一個只顯示該力量目前偏緊繃還是偏寬鬆，僅此而已。它們刻意不被平均成一個分數，也不用來預測房價接下來會怎麼走；那是羅盤的工作。估值與供給以該市場整段歷史標準化，讀的是相對該市場自身過去的位置——不是跨國絕對值（一個一直很貴的市場，如台灣約 9 倍所得，相對自己會落在中段）。人口則是固定尺度的絕對成長方向讀數，不是相對歷史標準化——不論該市場過去長什麼樣子，同樣的成長率讀數都一樣。

Why It's Built This Way為什麼這樣設計

The split isn't a stylistic choice — it's what the data forces. Test each force on its own against three-year forward house-price returns across the US, Taiwan, Malaysia and Japan, and momentum is the only one that works everywhere: quarters following price uptrends beat quarters following downtrends by 7 to 14 points, in every market, no exceptions. Valuation fails the same test — it mean-reverts the way textbooks promise in cyclical markets, but in Japan it reversed by 18.9 points, because post-1990 cheapness there was structural decay, not a bargain waiting to close. That is exactly why valuation can't sit on the forecast axis: it describes condition, and condition points in different directions in different markets. Blend it into one “cycle” score and you answer two questions at once — where prices go next, and whether the market is expensive — and get both muddy. Kept apart, each instrument can be judged on its own terms. The Compass's four-cell ordering — Fuelled beating Starved — holds in the US, Malaysia and Japan; it breaks in Taiwan, but that's a sample problem, not a method one: 101 quarters of a nearly one-directional bull run means almost every “downtrend” quarter was simply followed by the next leg up. Momentum is also what finally reads Japan right — the one market that had a real crash to get right.這樣拆開不是風格選擇，是資料逼出來的結果。把每一種力量單獨拿去對美國、台灣、馬來西亞、日本的三年前向房價報酬做檢驗，動能是唯一在所有市場都有效的一項：房價處於上升趨勢之後的季度，報酬比處於下降趨勢之後的季度高出七至十四個百分點，四個市場、無一例外。估值撐不過同一套檢驗——在有循環性的市場裡它照教科書均值回歸，但在日本反轉了十八點九個百分點，因為 1990 年後的便宜是結構性衰敗，不是等待兌現的划算。這正是估值不能放上預測軸的原因：它描述的是體質，而體質在不同市場指向不同方向。把它攪進一個「週期」分數，等於同時回答兩個問題——價格接下來往哪走、以及市場貴不貴——結果兩個都答得含糊。拆開來，每套儀器才能各自被檢驗。羅盤的四格排序——燃料格贏過飢餓格——在美國、馬來西亞、日本都成立；在台灣不成立，但這是樣本問題，不是方法問題：一百零一季幾乎單向的多頭，意味著「下降趨勢」的季度後面幾乎都接著下一段漲勢。也是動能，終於讓日本這個唯一真正崩盤過的市場被讀對。

Three reference frames sit on this page, and each answers a different question. The compass is trailing and cyclical — momentum and credit impulse — and answers where prices are likely headed next. The self-history gauges are expanding, measuring valuation, population and supply against a market's own multi-decade record, and answer whether the market looks strained relative to its own past. The cross-country ranks are neither: they measure a market against the other 11 right now, and answer whether it's actually cheap or expensive in absolute terms. The US and Taiwan show why one frame alone misleads — the US reads expensive against its own history (z=-3.0) but only middling against the world (composite +0.30), while Taiwan reads neutral against its own history (z≈0) yet is the most expensive of the four against the world (composite -0.93, worst yield of all 12 markets). Use all three together, not one in isolation.這個頁面有三個參照座標，各自回答不同問題。羅盤是落後、順週期的——動能與信貸脈衝——回答「價格接下來可能往哪走」。健檢卡的自身歷史量表是擴張型的，把估值、人口與供給拿去對比該市場自己數十年的紀錄，回答「相對自己的過去，這個市場現在算不算緊繃」。跨國排名則兩者皆非：它把一個市場放進當下其他11個市場中比較，回答「就絕對水準而言，這個市場到底貴不貴」。美國與台灣正好說明只用單一座標會誤導：美國相對自身歷史顯得昂貴（z=-3.0），但相對世界卻只是中段（綜合分數+0.30）；台灣相對自身歷史持平（z≈0），但相對世界卻是這四個市場中最貴的一個（綜合分數-0.93，租金報酬率為全部12個市場中最差）。三者要合起來看，不能只取其一。

What This Does and Does Not Claim承諾什麼、不承諾什麼

  • The risk gauge is real, but thinly tested.
