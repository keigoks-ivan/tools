Can these four numbers be true at the same time?這個市場的四個數字，能不能同時是真的？

Rent, price, new construction and existing stock are locked together by definitions, not by estimated correlations. Put a market's numbers in below and this page works out whether they add up — and if they do not, which one has to move.租金、價格、新建量與存量，彼此的關係是定義出來的，不是估計出來的。把一個市場的數字填進去，這頁會算出它們對不對得起來，對不起來的話，哪一個必須動。

What this page answers這頁在回答什麼 This page answers one question: can a market's four numbers be true at the same time? Rent, price, new construction and stock are tied together by a mathematical relationship. If price is high but rent is low, and the market is still building fast, those three things cannot all be right at once. This page works out which one has to give.

It does not predict where prices go. It only tells you which number is under strain.這頁在回答一個問題：這個市場的四個數字，能不能同時是真的？租金、價格、新建量、存量，這四個數字彼此有數學上的約束。如果房價很高但租金很低，同時還一直在蓋，那這三件事不可能同時合理。這頁幫你算出哪一件事說不通。

它不預測漲跌。它只告訴你哪個數字必須動。

How to read the chart圖怎麼看

The axes run left and down on purpose. Think of it as a four-way intersection — each corner handles one relationship.軸往左、往下走是故意的。想成一個十字路口，四個角落各管一件事。

Read it clockwise only. Growth pushes rent up, rent pushes price up, price gets developers building, building grows the stock, and the bigger stock presses rent back down. Reading it backwards has no meaning. Do not take the stock number and back it into a rent forecast, or take completions and back them into a price forecast — that is the single most common way people misread this diagram. The dashed rectangle in the chart below closes when all four corners agree with each other; that is what "self-consistent" means here.只能順時針讀。成長推升租金，租金推升價格，價格帶動開發商動工，動工讓存量變大，變大的存量把租金壓回去。逆著讀沒有意義。不要拿存量倒推租金、拿新建量倒推價格——這是這張圖最常見的誤讀方式。下面圖裡的虛線矩形，四個角接得起來就是「自洽」的意思。
The four cells' formal names and identities (for checking the math)四個象限的正式代號與恆等式（給想核對算法的人看）

Each corner has a name and an equation, and the rest of this page refers to them by these labels. NW capitalises rent into a price, P = R / i. NE clears the space market, D(R, X) = S. SW is the build decision, C = f(P, K), with P < K ⇒ C = 0. SE rolls the stock forward, ΔS = C − δS, so the steady state is S* = C / δ. Nothing below assumes any market is sitting exactly at that steady state. Most are not, and that is the point of running the test.每個角落都有一個代號與一條方程式，這頁後面都用這些代號來指稱。NW 把租金資本化成價格，P = R / i。NE 是空間市場出清，D(R, X) = S。SW 是開發決策，C = f(P, K)，且 P < K ⇒ C = 0。SE 讓存量往前滾，ΔS = C − δS，穩態為 S* = C / δ。以下沒有任何一處假設某個市場正好落在那個穩態上。大多數市場不會，而這正是要跑這項檢定的原因。

The rectangle矩形

The four corners, and the shape they draw四個角，還有它們連出來的形狀

Price runs left, stock runs right, rent runs up, completions run down. The dashed rectangle joins your four numbers. The more it looks like a proper rectangle, the more the market's numbers agree with each other.價格向左、存量向右、租金向上、新建量向下。虛線矩形連起你輸入的四個數字。矩形長得越端正，代表這個市場的數字彼此越合得來。

Each half-axis has its own scale. Price is in currency per unit, rent in currency per unit per year, stock and completions in units, four different magnitudes. Lengths are normalised to each half-axis's own maximum, exactly as in the original DiPasquale-Wheaton figure. Do not compare a length on one axis with a length on another; only the direction of movement and the shape carry meaning. Hover any corner for the real numbers.每個半軸各自有自己的尺度。P 是每戶幣值、R 是每戶每年幣值、S 與 C 是戶數，四者量級不同。長度是各自對該半軸的最大值正規化，這正是 DiPasquale-Wheaton 原圖的畫法。不可拿一個軸上的長度去比另一個軸上的長度；只有移動方向與形狀變化有意義。滑鼠移到任一個角上可以看到真實數字。

What the four cells say四格怎麼說

Each cell backs one number out of the identity and checks it against something independent. A check mark means it holds up; a warning means there is tension; an ✗ means it does not add up — and either way, the cell names what has to move.每一格從恆等式反推出一個數字，再跟一個獨立的東西對比。打勾代表撐得住；警示代表有張力；打叉代表對不起來——不管哪一種，那一格都會指名什麼東西必須動。

The ✓ in cell 1 is fragile: try breaking it yourself第 1 格的那個 ✓ 很脆弱：自己動手試試看

It is built on gross rent (a 5.2% gross yield), but the identity wants net rent: after management fees, tax and vacancy. Malaysia does not publish a net-rent series at any geography.它算的是毛租金（5.2% 毛收益率），但恆等式要的是淨租金：扣掉管理費、稅、空置之後的數字。馬來西亞在任何地理層級都沒有淨租金的官方數據。

If net rent runs at roughly seventy percent of gross, the yield drops from 5.2% to about 3.6%, and the implied rent growth this price needs jumps from +0.55%/yr to about +2.11%/yr. That lands almost exactly on the 2.088%/yr rent has actually delivered since 2010. The comfortable cushion this cell was showing disappears. The colour on this page may not cross its own display threshold, but the substance already has — and the display threshold is only this page's choice, not part of the frozen method.如果淨租金大約只有毛租金的七成，收益率會從 5.2% 掉到約 3.6%，這個價格所需要的隱含租金成長會從 +0.55%／年跳到約 +2.11%／年。幾乎正好貼上 2010 年以來租金實際交出的 2.088%／年。這一格原本看起來的舒適空間就消失了。這頁的顏色不一定會跨過自己設的門檻，但實質已經變了——而門檻本來就只是這頁自己的選擇，不屬於凍結的方法論。

ρ and δ carry the same weakness. Their sliders in the panel above each have a push-to-extreme button of their own. Use them, and treat any conclusion that flips as not standing.ρ 與 δ 也背著同樣的弱點。上面控制面板裡它們的滑桿旁邊都有各自的「拉到極端」按鈕。按下去，會翻的結論就不算數。

The four legs are not one product, either: price and rent are KL condos, stock and completions are every residential type, landed housing included. More on that below the sourcing table.四條腿也不是同一個產品：價格與租金是 KL 公寓，存量與完工量是含有地住宅在內的全部住宅型別。詳見下面資料來源表格之後的說明。

What you can't do with the four cells above上面四格不能拿來做的事

  • Never turn this into a price target or an annual return. Nothing here converts into "prices will fall X%" or "expect Y% a year". The moment a result gets turned into a number like that, it has stopped being this framework.不能把結果換算成價格目標或年化報酬。這裡的任何輸出都不會變成「價格會跌 X%」或「預期年化 Y%」。一旦被換算成那種數字，它就已經不是這個框架了。
  • Never call a turning point from one snapshot. One reading of the four cells cannot tell you a top or a bottom. This framework has no sense of timing at all.不能用單一次的四格讀數宣告轉折。一次快照判斷不了頭部或底部。這個框架對時點完全沒有感覺。
  • ⚠ and ✗ say what has to move, never when. "Rent has to give" is a direction. "Rent gives next year" is not something this page can tell you.⚠ 與 ✗ 只說什麼必須動，不說何時動。「租金必須讓」是方向；「租金明年就會讓」不是這頁說得出來的事。
  • The three colours are a display choice made by this page, picked for readability. They are not part of the frozen method itself, which defines the tests and the diagnoses but never the cut-points.三種顏色是本頁自己選的顯示方式，為了好讀。它們不屬於凍結的方法論本身：方法論定義了檢定與診斷，但沒有定義切點在哪。

How to actually use this你會怎麼用它

  1. See a project or a market and want to know if the story holds up. Put its numbers in and find the cell that disagrees with the other three: that cell is the direction to go dig into.看到一個建案或一個市場，想知道那套說法站不站得住。把數字填進去，找出跟其他三格不合的那一格：那一格就是要去查的方向。
  2. Push the assumption sliders (ρ, δ, K) to their extremes and watch whether the verdict survives. A conclusion that flips inside a reasonable range does not stand.把假設參數（ρ、δ、K）拉到極端，看結論撐不撐得住。在合理範圍內會翻的結論，就不成立。
  3. Use it to stop a "prices here are going up" story in its tracks. This page tells you exactly how fast rent would need to grow for that story to hold. One look at history tells you whether that number is realistic.拿它擋掉「這裡房價會漲」的說法。這頁能算出那個說法要成立，租金得每年漲幾個百分比。歷史數字一擺出來，就知道那個故事站不站得住。

The four legs are not one product. Price and rent are KL condos; stock and completions are every residential type, landed housing included. The methodology rules landed housing out of the rent test entirely, because landed rent cannot be observed at all. What this does not say這句話沒有說的事

It does not say prices will fall back. Compression of i can be driven by the risk-free rate, by the risk premium, or by expected growth, and the identity cannot separate them, because ρ is an assumption parameter with no observation behind it. The status of this finding is: here is where the tension in the NW quadrant sits. It is not a forecast, and it carries no date.它沒有說價格會回跌。i 的壓縮可以由無風險利率、風險溢酬或預期成長任一項驅動，恆等式分不出是哪一項，因為 ρ 是背後沒有任何觀察值的假設參數。這條結論的地位是：NW 象限的張力在這裡。它不是預測，也不帶時點。

The Asian modification亞洲修改

The original NE identity writes D(R, X) = S. That embeds an assumption: every unit of stock enters the space market. It holds for US commercial real estate, where an empty office is a pure loss and the owner goes looking for a tenant. It does not hold in Kuala Lumpur, Bangkok or Dubai. Units get built, get sold, and then sit. The buyer bought an asset, not space. Those units are counted in the SE quadrant and never enter the NE rent-setting process. The rectangle leaks here.原始的 NE 恆等式寫 D(R, X) = S。這隱含一個假設：每一單位存量都會進入空間市場。在美國商用不動產成立：空著的辦公室對業主純粹是損失，一定去找租客。在吉隆坡、曼谷、杜拜不成立。單位蓋了、賣了、然後空著。買家買的不是空間，是資產。這些單位在 SE 象限計入存量，卻從未進入 NE 的租金決定過程。矩形在這裡漏水。

The fifth equation is the release valve: ΔS_effective = ΔS_total − ΔS_overhang − ΔS_shadow, with ΔS_shadow = −Λ(h, g^e) · S_shadow. Λ, the release rate, rises with holding cost h and falls with expected appreciation g^e. This is what explains why KL, Bangkok and Dubai carry years of overhang without rents collapsing — not strong demand, but the fact that those units are not in the NE denominator at all. They are suspended supply. When holding cost rises (mortgage rates, a vacancy tax, a change in rental income tax) or when the appreciation story stops working, Λ rises and the suspended stock pours into S_effective. That is where the real downside for rent lives, and its relationship to price is counter-intuitive.第五式是釋放閥：ΔS_effective = ΔS_total − ΔS_overhang − ΔS_shadow，其中 ΔS_shadow = −Λ(h, g^e) · S_shadow。Λ 是釋放率，隨持有成本 h 遞增、隨預期漲幅 g^e 遞減。這解釋了為什麼吉隆坡、曼谷、杜拜餘屋高企多年而租金沒有崩——原因不是需求強，而是那些單位根本不在 NE 象限的分母裡。它們是懸而未落的供給。一旦持有成本上升（房貸利率、空屋稅、租金所得稅制改變）或漲幅預期熄火，Λ 上升，懸置存量就會倒進 S_effective。那才是租金真正的下行風險點，而且它跟價格的關係是反直覺的。

And the shadow layer cannot be measured in Malaysia: so this stays qualitative而陰影層在馬來西亞量不到：所以這一段只能是定性的

Five candidate proxies were evaluated and closed on 2026-08-02: utility zero-consumption (the utility company publishes account counts by tariff class with no consumption bands, and its account total exceeds peninsular residential stock by 51%, a different population), CCC against assessment tax (assessment is levied on valuation and is blind to occupancy; the vacancy allowance provision selects owners who tried and failed to let, which is the complement of the shadow layer), tenancy registration (Malaysia has no mandatory regime, so the premise fails), and the OpenDOSM state living-quarters series (which returns negative vacancy rates for KL 2017–2019 and has an 11.3pp break at 2019→2020). One source timed out and is recorded as unresolved rather than as confirmed-absent. Consequence: the Asian modification is a mechanism and a single anchor, not a quantified layer. The only observation is the KL 2020 census — 92,429 vacant units (14.6%), of which 18,036 are holiday or transit dwellings; the true range 18,036–92,429 is too wide to discriminate, and its denominator is the census population, which must not be divided into NAPIC stock.五個候選代理已於 2026-08-02 全數評估並結案：公用事業零用量（電力公司只公佈按關稅類別的帳戶總數，無用量分級，且帳戶數比半島住宅存量多 51%，母體根本不同）、CCC 對評估稅（評估稅按估值課徵、對占用狀態失明；空置減免條款篩出來的是已努力出租而未果的業主，正好是陰影層的補集）、租賃登記（馬來西亞無強制登記制度，前提不成立）、OpenDOSM 州別居住單位序列（KL 2017–2019 算出負空置率，2019→2020 有 11.3 個百分點的斷點）。另有一個來源連線逾時，記為未結而非「已確認無資料」。後果：亞洲修改降級為定性機制加單點錨，不是可量化的分層。唯一的觀察值是吉隆坡 2020 年普查——空置 92,429 戶（14.6%），其中可對應的度假屋與過境屋 18,036 戶；真值區間 18,036～92,429 寬到沒有判別力，而且它的分母是普查母體，不得拿去除 NAPIC 存量。

Honest limits誠實邊界

Recorded as they are, not softened. The first four are the ones that decide whether a reading from this page is worth anything.照登，不修飾。前四條決定這頁的一次讀數到底值不值錢。

1 · Blind to timing1 · 轉折時點失明

This is a comparative-statics framework. It says which side the new equilibrium is on and which variable has to move to get there. It does not say how long, or whether the market overshoots on the way. Turning-point detection stays outsourced to tripwires — four blind tests caught this failure mode twice.這是比較靜態框架。它說新均衡在哪一邊、哪個變數必須動才到得了，不說要走多久、也不說路上會不會超調。轉折偵測仍然外包給絆線——四場盲測裡這個失效模式現形過兩次。

2 · Landed housing is out, entirely2 · 有地住宅完全不適用

In an owner-occupier market the rental sector covers only part of the stock, so the NE test only works on rental-active products: KL high-rise, serviced apartments. Landed housing does not qualify: its R cannot be observed, and applying the test anyway means fabricating it. This is a product-level boundary, not a detail to wave through.自住為主的市場裡，租賃只覆蓋部分存量，因此 NE 檢定只在租賃活躍的產品有效：吉隆坡高層公寓、服務式公寓。有地住宅不合格：它的 R 無法觀察，硬套等於捏造。這是產品層的適用邊界，不是可以揮手帶過的細節。

3 · δ and ρ are assumptions3 · δ 與 ρ 是假設參數

Neither has an official series in Malaysia. Every conclusion must carry a sensitivity band, and if a conclusion flips inside the plausible band for these parameters, that conclusion does not stand. g* is worse than an assumption-dependent number — it is the residual of the identity, so it absorbs the entire error in ρ. Use it to ask "is this g* plausible", never as a projected growth rate.兩者在馬來西亞都沒有官方序列。任何結論都必須附敏感度區間，而且若結論在這兩個參數的合理帶內會翻轉，該結論即不成立。g* 比「依賴假設的數字」更糟——它是恆等式的殘差，會把 ρ 的誤差全部吸收。它只能用來問「這個 g* 合理嗎」，永遠不能當預測的成長率引用。

4 · Test 3's denominator is knowingly too big4 · 檢定 3 的分母明知偏大

Because S_shadow cannot be measured, the denominator falls back to S_total − S_overhang. The bias direction is fixed and must travel with the number: a denominator that is too big means implied demand growth is systematically understated, which means the fallback version of test 3 reads optimistic. This sentence follows that number everywhere.因為 S_shadow 量不到，分母退回 S_total − S_overhang。偏誤方向是固定的，而且必須跟著數字走：分母偏大 ⇒ 隱含需求成長被系統性低估 ⇒ 退回版的檢定 3 偏樂觀。這句話必須永遠跟著那個數字。

5 · The SE identity does not hold on NAPIC data, and the failure has a fixed sign5 · SE 恆等式在 NAPIC 資料上不成立，且方向固定

ΔS = C − δS with δ ≥ 0 requires ΔS < C. In the data, ΔS exceeds C by 0.6–1.3% of stock every year, and the gap is positive in 64 of 71 non-break quarters. Four hypotheses were tested and falsified. The reason is in NAPIC's own Technical Notes: the survey "has not reached a total population count", so the stock series is an expanding survey frame that absorbs old units newly brought into scope, while completions count only units issued a certificate within the existing scope this quarter. Two columns, two coverage definitions — a definitional problem, not a data error. NAPIC does not publish the adjustment breakdown, so the cumulative 436,237 units cannot be split by channel and the magnitude is recorded as unknown. This is why δ* is reported as a bracket and never as a point.ΔS = C − δS 在 δ ≥ 0 之下要求 ΔS 小於 C。實際資料裡，ΔS 每年比 C 多出存量的 0.6–1.3%，非斷點季 71 季中有 64 季為正。四個假設逐一證偽。真正原因寫在 NAPIC 自己的 Technical Notes：調查「has not reached a total population count」，所以存量序列是一個還在擴張的調查底冊，會吸收本來就存在、這季才納入調查範圍的舊單位；完工量則只計既有範圍內當季發證者。兩欄、兩套涵蓋定義——這是定義問題不是資料錯誤。NAPIC 不公布調整項分解，累計 436,237 戶無法拆成各管道佔比，量級照實記為不明。這就是 δ* 只能報區間、永遠不能報點估的原因。

The remaining recorded limits, unabridged.其餘已記錄的邊界，未刪減。

  • NE's demand function mixes consumption and investment demand. That weakness is in the original DiPasquale-Wheaton; the shadow-stock split only patches the supply-side leak. Foreign buyers and residency-linked purchase demand remain blended into D(·). Unresolved.NE 的需求函數混了消費需求與投資需求。這是 DiPasquale-Wheaton 原版就有的弱點；陰影存量的拆解只補了供給側的漏水。外籍買家與居留連動的購買需求仍然混在 D(·) 裡。未解。
  • The institutional layer has not landed. Plot-ratio rules, buildable land stock and commuting accessibility are all unsourced for KL. The SW quadrant therefore carries a price-cost relationship and no supply-elasticity dimension — which is precisely the cell that a prior six-test round judged not scoreable.制度層尚未落地。容積制度、可建地存量、通勤可及性三項在吉隆坡全部無來源。因此 SW 象限只有價格—成本關係，沒有制度彈性維度——而那正是先前六個測試判定為無法評分的那一格。
  • Counter-clockwise is unreadable. Repeated here because it is the misuse that keeps happening.逆時針不可讀。在此重申，因為這是不斷發生的誤用。
  • The rent index cannot date a turn. CPI 041 prices the stock of continuing tenancies: across 197 month-on-month steps it moved only 68 times, 66 of them in the second month of a quarter. It is a quarterly series wearing monthly clothes, with 66 effective observations and zero redundancy. It also reprices only as contracts renew, so it is structurally slower than a transaction price index — pairing it against MHPI to date a lead or lag measures the instrument, not the market.租金指數無法判定轉折時點。CPI 041 量的是續約中的租約存量：197 次月變動只動了 68 次，其中 66 次落在季度的第二個月。它是季頻序列穿著月頻的衣服，有效觀察 66 個、零冗餘。它也只隨換約才逐批反映市場租金，因此結構上比成交價指數慢——拿它去對 MHPI 判定領先或落後，量到的是儀器不是市場。
  • A frozen proposition already known to be unscoreable. Screening the states for "δ* at or above 3% for three consecutive years" returns only two states on either measure, too few for the rank test the ledger specifies. The threshold will not be loosened to manufacture sample size; at the scoring date it will be recorded as "sample too small, not scoreable".一條已知無法評分的凍結命題。以「δ* 連續 3 年 ≥3%」掃各州，兩個口徑都只篩出 2 州，遠不足以做帳本指定的排序檢定。門檻不會為了湊樣本量而放寬；評分日將照實記為「樣本不足、無法評分」。
