Bottom line總結 Turkish property has been priced by one variable for five years, and that variable is the real policy rate. Between November 2021 and June 2023 the central bank cut into accelerating inflation; the effective funding cost less consumer price inflation reached −74 percentage points in November 2022. Real house prices rose 168% in the four years to the second quarter of 2023. The stance then reversed to a peak policy rate of 50.00% in March 2024, the real rate crossed into positive territory in September 2024, and real prices have fallen in ten of the eleven quarters since the 2023 peak. The correction has run entirely through inflation: the largest nominal fall between half-yearly readings over the whole sixteen-year series is 1.1%. Since June, inflation has fallen three months in a row, to 29.73% in September, with the policy rate held at 37.00% on 10 September: the real policy rate is +7.3 points.

The second consequence of that inflation is structural rather than cyclical. Household credit fell from 19.4% of GDP in the third quarter of 2013 to 9.4% in 2024, and not by repayment: the nominal stock rose 5.7 times between the first quarter of 2022 and the fourth quarter of 2025. Mortgaged sales were 40.0% of Turkish house transactions in 2013 and 10.8% in 2024. They recovered to 14.1% in 2025 and to 19.9% in January to August 2026, just under the 20% line this page uses to test the argument. The credit channel is narrower than in most markets but it is no longer closed, and it is set out transaction by transaction in the companion Istanbul Property Market Report.

The binding constraint is external. Reserves excluding gold, measured in the IMF's special drawing rights (SDR), fell from SDR 61.1bn in January 2026 to SDR 36.3bn in March, the month the central bank suspended its one-week repo auctions (1 March) amid market volatility that press reports tie to the US-Israeli war with Iran. They rebuilt to SDR 50.8bn by August, still SDR 10.2bn below January, while the real effective exchange rate rose from 104.9 in January to 109.4 in July. Defending an appreciating real lira on a reserve stock that has not been refilled is the configuration that broke in 2018 and again in 2021. The March fall shows how fast that stock can move, and the August level shows how much of it has come back. 土耳其房價這五年主要跟著實質政策利率走。2021 年 11 月到 2023 年 6 月，央行在通膨加速時持續降息，有效資金成本扣掉通膨後，2022 年 11 月低到 −74 個百分點。到 2023 年第二季為止的四年，實質房價漲了 168%。政策方向其後反轉，政策利率 2024 年 3 月升到 50.00% 的高點，實質利率 2024 年 9 月轉正。從 2023 年的高點算起，十一季裡有十季實質房價下跌。這次修正完全靠通膨完成：十六年的序列中，名目房價在兩次半年讀數之間最大的跌幅只有 1.1%。6 月以後通膨連降三個月，9 月為 29.73%。央行 9 月 10 日維持利率 37.00%，實質政策利率為 +7.3 個百分點。

The distinction, stated so it can be checked三者的區別，可供查核

The one-week repo rate is the announced policy rate and is what the central bank's own decision table records: 37.00% since 23 January 2026. The average cost of funding is what the central bank actually charged the banking system: it equals the announced rate in calm periods, and sits above it when the bank funds off the upper band. On 1 March 2026 the central bank suspended its one-week repo auctions and funded at the 40% overnight lending rate, so the cost was 39.82% in March and 40.00% from April to July 2026, three points above the announced rate. Auctions resumed on 24 August at 37%, and the funding cost returned to the announced rate (TCMB press release 2026-35). The OECD immediate rate is the overnight borrowing rate, not the policy rate, and reads 35.50%. The three coincide at 50.00, 50.00 and 47.00 in mid-2024, which is what identifies them.一週期附買回利率是公告的政策利率，央行決議表記載的就是它：2026 年 1 月 23 日起為 37.00%。平均資金成本是央行實際向銀行收取的利率。平穩時期兩者相等，央行改由利率走廊上緣供給資金時，平均資金成本就高於公告利率。2026 年 3 月 1 日，央行暫停一週期附買回標售，改以 40% 的隔夜借出利率供給資金，平均資金成本因此在 3 月為 39.82%，4 到 7 月為 40.00%，比公告利率高 3 個百分點。8 月 24 日恢復標售，利率 37%，資金成本回到公告利率（TCMB 第 2026-35 號公告）。OECD 即期利率是隔夜借款利率，不是政策利率，讀數為 35.50%。三者在 2024 年年中分別為 50.00、50.00 和 47.00，可據此對照三條序列。

Seventy years of Turkish inflation七十年的土耳其通膨

Türkiye has recorded double-digit January inflation in the large majority of the years for which the series exists, including a reading of 125.9% in January 1995 and 101.6% in January 1998. The conclusions live in the interactions關鍵結論在交互作用

  • Real funding rate × inflation-hedge demand. These are the two largest terms and they work against each other. A positive real rate gives a household a lira-denominated alternative to housing for the first time since 2019, so the hedging motive weakens exactly when the discount rate rises.   • Real exchange rate × external reserves. The real appreciation that removed the foreign-buyer discount is being financed from the reserve stock. Close but not met: the share was 20.4% in the first half of 2026, 19.3% in July and 17.4% in August, and household credit was 10.1% of GDP.家庭信用占 GDP 的比重回升超過 15%，或房貸占成交的比重連續四季維持在 20% 以上。這推翻論點二：信用管道重新運作，標準的利率對價格架構重新適用。目前接近但沒有成立：2026 年上半年占比為 20.4%，7 月為 19.3%，8 月為 17.4%，家庭信用占 GDP 為 10.1%。
  • Reserves excluding gold rebuild above SDR 60bn while the real effective exchange rate holds above 105 for two consecutive quarters. This breaks argument three: the real appreciation would be financed by inflows rather than by the reserve stock, and the external constraint would not be binding. Not met: reserves were SDR 50.8bn in August.外匯存底（不含黃金）回升到 600 億 SDR 以上，且實質有效匯率連續兩季維持在 105 以上。這推翻論點三：實質升值由資金流入支撐，不是靠消耗存底，對外約束不成立。目前沒有成立：8 月存底為 508 億 SDR。

Falsified to the downside if若出現以下，向下證偽

  • The announced policy rate is cut below the prevailing inflation rate. This restores the 2021 configuration, and both previous instances were followed by a currency event within twelve months. It is the single highest-information observation on this page. The rate is 37.00% against September inflation of 29.73%.公告政策利率降到當期通膨率以下。這會回到 2021 年的配置，前兩次發生後，十二個月內都出現貨幣事件。這是本頁資訊含量最高的單一觀測值。目前利率為 37.00%，9 月通膨為 29.73%。
  • Reserves excluding gold fall below SDR 35bn, or the lira depreciates more than 10% in a month. The external constraint would have bound. The Answer — Where, What, When結論篇 · 區位、產品、時點

Where區位

Not İstanbul on price momentum. The city's nominal index has risen 206% since December 2022 against Ankara's 301% (to June 2026), a gap of 95 percentage points in three and a half years, and its real index has fallen 18.6% from the January 2023 reading against a national 8.9%. In August 2026 İstanbul's nominal annual growth of 26.3% was the highest of the three large cities (Ankara 24.8%). İstanbul is the right market for rent, tenant depth and liquidity; it was the wrong one for capital growth in 2023, 2024 and 2025, and the relative position has only begun to change in 2026.不要因為價格動能選伊斯坦堡。該市名目指數自 2022 年 12 月起（算到 2026 年 6 月）漲了 206%，安卡拉漲了 301%，三年半差 95 個百分點。伊斯坦堡實質指數自 2023 年 1 月下跌 18.6%，全國為 8.9%。2026 年 8 月，伊斯坦堡名目年增 26.3%，是三大城市最高，安卡拉為 24.8%。伊斯坦堡適合租金、租客深度與流動性。2023 到 2025 年它不適合資本利得，2026 年的相對位置才剛開始改變。

What產品

Completed stock with a verifiable letting record, bought on the rent line. The gross yield has expanded from 4.28% at the 2022 price peak to 6.09% in the second quarter of 2026, and that expansion is the entire investment case. Off-plan and instalment structures carry the lira inflation risk the buyer believes the property is hedging, and they deliver into the 2027 to 2029 permit pipeline.選已完工、有可查證出租紀錄的物件，以租金承作。毛收益率從 2022 年房價高點的 4.28% 擴大到 2026 年第二季的 6.09%，投資論述整個建立在這個擴大上。預售和分期付款要承擔買方以為房產在對沖的里拉通膨風險，交屋時間又落在 2027 至 2029 年的建照供給區間。

When時點

The decision does not turn on the price index. It turns on the reserve series and the announced policy rate, both published and both moving before price does. A foreign buyer is additionally buying at a real exchange rate about 40% above its January 2022 low, so the currency entry point is among the least favourable in five years even where the property entry point is not.判斷不取決於房價指數，而取決於存底序列和公告政策利率。兩者都是公開資料，也都比價格先動。外國買家另外要面對進場時實質匯率比 2022 年 1 月低點高約 40%，所以即使房產進場時點尚可，貨幣進場時點仍是五年來最不利的之一。

What would change the answer什麼會改變這個答案

A step depreciation of the lira. It would restore the foreign-buyer discount that closed between January 2022 and July 2026, and it would do so at the same moment it forced the real rate negative and restarted the inflation-hedge bid. That single event flips three of the four terms in the equation at once, and the reserve series is where it becomes visible first.里拉一次性貶值。它會恢復 2022 年 1 月到 2026 年 7 月之間消失的外資折讓，同時迫使實質利率轉負、重啟通膨避險買盤。單一事件同時翻轉式中四項裡的三項，存底序列是它最先顯現的地方。

What this page concludes本頁的結論

Türkiye is the only market on this site where monetary policy alone explains most of the property price series, and it is the only one where the credit channel has been narrowed by inflation rather than by regulation or by preference. The real funding rate moved 82 percentage points in two years, and the real house price index moved with it in both directions. An investor who tracks one series here should track that one.土耳其是本站唯一一個，貨幣政策單獨就能解釋房價序列大部分變動的市場，也是唯一一個信用管道被通膨（不是法規或偏好）縮小的市場。實質資金利率兩年內移動 82 個百分點，實質房價指數兩個方向都跟著動。在這個市場只追蹤一條序列的人，應該追蹤這一條。

The record does not support underwriting real capital growth from here. Real prices are 18.6% below their January 2023 reading in İstanbul and have fallen in ten of the last eleven quarters, entirely through inflation rather than through a nominal fall. Against that, real output per head rose 29% between 2020 and 2025, which is a genuine domestic income base and the strongest argument against extrapolating the decline. The return available now is the rent, at a gross yield that has expanded 181 basis points from the 2022 trough, and the mechanism behind that expansion is a lapsed statute rather than a market judgement.歷史紀錄不支持從現在起把實質資本利得放進承作假設。伊斯坦堡實質房價比 2023 年 1 月低 18.6%，十一季中有十季下跌，全部經由通膨完成，名目沒有下跌。另一方面，實質人均產出 2020 到 2025 年上升 29%，是真實的本地所得基礎，也是反對把跌勢外推的最有力論據。現在拿得到的報酬是租金，毛收益率比 2022 年谷底擴大 181 個基點，擴大的原因是一項已經到期的法律規定，不是市場判斷。

The question that decides the next three years is not the price index. It is whether the reserve stock can continue to finance a real appreciation of the size seen since January 2022. March 2026 showed that stock can fall SDR 17.1bn in a month (SDR 53.4bn in February, SDR 36.3bn in March). That question is answered month by month in a published series, and the transaction-level consequences of each answer are set out in the companion Istanbul Property Market Report.決定未來三年的問題不是房價指數，而是存底能不能繼續支撐 2022 年 1 月以來規模的實質升值。2026 年 3 月顯示，存底可以在一個月內減少 171 億 SDR（2 月 534 億，3 月 363 億）。這個問題每月都有公開序列給出答案，各種答案在成交層面的後果，見對照的《伊斯坦堡住宅市場報告》。
