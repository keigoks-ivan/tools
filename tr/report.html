Bottom line總結 Credit is a minority channel in the İstanbul housing market, and it is only now widening. Mortgaged transactions were 10.8% of Turkish house sales in 2024 against 40.0% in 2013, and 13.4% in İstanbul against 45.8%. They recovered to 14.1% in 2025 and to 19.9% in January to August 2026, when mortgage sales rose 27.6% on the year while total sales fell 6.8%. The nominal housing loan rate sits between 41.4% and 43.7% (weekly readings, late July 2026). Household credit fell from 19.4% of GDP in the third quarter of 2013 to 9.4% in 2024, and not by repayment: the nominal stock rose 5.7 times between the first quarter of 2022 and the fourth quarter of 2025. Inflation voided the instrument, and about four in five Turkish house purchases still clear on cash, on inheritance or on a developer instalment plan.

This is the third distinct credit mechanism documented on this site, and the three are worth stating together. Bangkok is a market where the bank will not lend, with rejection rates of 40% to 50% on applications that were made. Dubai is a market that does not need to borrow, with 86% of purchases settling in cash by choice. İstanbul is a market where the instrument exists and inflation has made it a minority channel. The three produce different price behaviour, different downside mechanics and different early-warning series, and confusing them is the most common error in cross-market property analysis.

The return has inverted. Rebased to the 2023 average, the national new-tenant rent index stands at 336 against a house price index of 237 (August 2026); real prices are 18.6% below their January 2023 reading in İstanbul while real rents more than doubled between 2020 and 2025. The İstanbul gross yield went from 4.28% at the 2022 price peak to 6.09% in the second quarter of 2026. The mechanism is a lapsed statute: the 25% renewal cap introduced by Law 7409 in June 2022 and extended by Law 7456 expired on 1 July 2024 with no third extension, while new lettings ran near 80% a year. In August 2026 İstanbul new-tenant rents were still up 34.5% on the year against house prices up 26.3%. The macro conditions that produced all of this are set out in the companion Türkiye Macro × Property analysis. 伊斯坦堡住宅市場的房貸只占少數，而且到 2026 年才開始回升。2024 年房貸成交占土耳其房屋成交的 10.8%，2013 年為 40.0%。伊斯坦堡 2024 年為 13.4%，2013 年為 45.8%。全國占比 2025 年回到 14.1%，2026 年 1 至 8 月為 19.9%。同期房貸成交件數年增 27.6%，總成交年減 6.8%。名目住宅貸款利率介於 41.4% 到 43.7%（2026 年 7 月下旬週頻讀數）。家庭信用占 GDP（國內生產毛額）的比重，從 2013 年第三季的 19.4% 降到 2024 年的 9.4%。這不是還款的結果，名目餘額在 2022 年第一季到 2025 年第四季之間增為 5.7 倍。通膨讓房貸失去作用，現在仍有約五分之四的購屋，用現金、繼承或建商分期完成。

這是本站記載的第三種信用型態，三種放在一起看最清楚。曼谷是銀行不放款的市場，已提出的申請有 40% 到 50% 被退件。杜拜是不需要借款的市場，86% 的購屋者自己選擇付現。伊斯坦堡有房貸這個工具，但通膨把它壓成少數人才用的管道。三者的價格走法、下跌機制和預警指標都不同。把它們混為一談，是跨市場房產分析最常見的錯誤。

報酬來源已經翻轉。以 2023 年平均為 100，2026 年 8 月全國新租租金指數為 336，房價指數為 237。伊斯坦堡實質房價比 2023 年 1 月低 18.6%，實質租金在 2020 到 2025 年之間增加一倍以上。伊斯坦堡毛收益率從 2022 年房價高點的 4.28%，升到 2026 年第二季的 6.09%。推動這個變化的是一條已到期的法律。2022 年 6 月第 7409 號法律設下 25% 的續約調漲上限，第 7456 號法律延長一年，到 2024 年 7 月 1 日到期，沒有第三次延長。上限生效期間，新出租的租金年增接近 80%。2026 年 8 月，伊斯坦堡新租租金仍年增 34.5%，房價年增 26.3%。這些變化背後的總體條件，見對照的《土耳其總經 × 房產》。

Two facts have to be held together. Real İstanbul prices compounded at 4.9% a year across the full sixteen years, which is a genuine real return and is the strongest argument against extrapolating the current decline. Over the last three and a half years they have compounded at −5.8% a year. The market did not lose money over the long run; it repriced violently upward between 2019 and 2023 on a real funding rate that reached −74 percentage points, and it has been giving part of that back ever since the rate turned. We regard the sixteen-year record as the relevant one for a ten-year holding period and the three-year record as the relevant one for an entry decision made now.兩件事要放在一起看。伊斯坦堡實質房價在完整十六年間，年化成長 4.9%。這是真實的實質報酬，也是反對把目前跌勢一路外推的最有力論據。最近三年半，年化變動是 −5.8%。這個市場長期沒有虧損。2019 到 2023 年，實質資金利率低到 −74 個百分點，房價劇烈重新定價向上。利率轉向後，房價開始回吐其中一部分。十年期持有，看十六年的紀錄。現在要不要進場，看最近三年的紀錄。

What this report concludes本報告的結論

İstanbul is the third distinct credit mechanism documented on this site and the only one in which the instrument was narrowed by the price level rather than by a bank or by a preference. Mortgaged transactions were 10.8% of the Turkish market in 2024 against 40.0% in 2013 and 19.9% in January to August 2026, and household credit halved as a share of GDP while its nominal stock rose 5.7 times. The consequences are specific and they are not intuitive: there is no forced-seller channel, the nominal price index barely falls, and a real correction of 17.9% has run for eleven quarters without producing a single quarter of nominal decline.伊斯坦堡是本站記載的第三種信用型態，也是唯一一個由物價水準，而不是銀行或個人偏好，把房貸壓成少數的市場。房貸成交占土耳其市場的比重，2024 年為 10.8%，2013 年為 40.0%，2026 年 1 至 8 月回到 19.9%。家庭信用占 GDP 的比重減半，名目餘額卻增為 5.7 倍。後果很具體，也不直覺。沒有被迫賣出的管道，名目房價指數幾乎不會下跌，17.9% 的實質修正走了十一季，沒有一季名目下跌。

The access rule is the one variable in this market whose effect can be measured cleanly, and it has been measured twice in opposite directions. The September 2018 loosening took monthly foreign sales from 4,159 to 6,134; the June 2022 tightening took İstanbul from 4,452 to 1,274 in a single month. The return has inverted, and the mechanism is a lapsed statute rather than a market judgement. Real İstanbul rents and prices both peaked in 2023; since then real prices have fallen to 147.5 and real rents have risen to 206.5 on a 2018 = 100 base, and the gross yield has expanded from 4.28% to 6.09%. The 25% renewal cap expired on 1 July 2024 with no third extension while new lettings ran near 80% a year, and that wedge is now largely spent as new-let growth has decelerated to 35.4% (34.5% in August 2026).
