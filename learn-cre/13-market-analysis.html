Tokyo's office vacancy rate sits at 0.7%. Chicago's sits at 26.8%. Both numbers describe the same asset class — multi-tenant office buildings — measured the same way, in the same quarter. The gap between them is not noise or a measurement quirk; it is two different markets in two different structural positions in their supply-and-demand cycle, and a large part of what separates a disciplined market analyst from someone reciting a national cap-rate average is being able to explain exactly why that 26-point gap exists and which side of it a given submarket is closer to. 1. Demand analysis by asset type

一、依資產類型拆解需求

"Demand" is not one thing. Each asset type is filled by a different economic activity, and pulling the wrong headline statistic — a plausible-sounding one, often reported right next to the correct one in the same government release — produces a demand forecast that is confidently wrong. The four sections below walk through what actually drives space absorption for office, multifamily, retail, and industrial, and where analysts most often substitute the convenient number for the correct one.

「需求」不是單一一件事。每種資產類型是被不同的經濟活動填滿的，而抓錯了頭條統計數字——一個聽起來很合理、常常就出現在同一份政府統計發布裡、緊挨著正確數字旁邊的數字——會做出一份自信滿滿卻是錯的需求預測。以下四節逐一拆解辦公室、住宅、零售與工業空間真正的空間去化驅動力，以及分析師最常在哪裡用方便取得的數字取代了正確的數字。

Office: office-using employment, not total employment

辦公室：辦公室使用就業，而非總就業

A metro can post strong headline job growth while its office market keeps deteriorating, because most job categories never sit in an office building. The number that actually drives office demand is office-using employmentThe subset of total employment in job categories that typically occupy conventional office space — principally financial activities, professional and business services, information/tech, and legal services. It excludes retail, leisure and hospitality, healthcare delivery, construction, and manufacturing employment, which occupy retail, medical, industrial, or field-based space instead.總就業中，通常會佔用傳統辦公空間的職類子集合——主要是金融活動、專業與商業服務、資訊科技與法律服務。不包含零售、休閒與住宿、醫療照護、營建與製造業就業，這些職類佔用的是零售、醫療、工業或現場型空間。: finance, professional and business services, information/technology, and legal, roughly. A city adding warehouse jobs, restaurant jobs, and hospital jobs can show a healthy unemployment rate and payroll count while its downtown office towers keep bleeding tenants, because none of those job categories rent office suites.

一個都會區可以在總體就業數字上表現亮眼，辦公室市場卻持續惡化，因為多數職類本來就不會坐在辦公大樓裡。真正驅動辦公室需求的數字是辦公室使用就業The subset of total employment in job categories that typically occupy conventional office space — principally financial activities, professional and business services, information/tech, and legal services. It excludes retail, leisure and hospitality, healthcare delivery, construction, and manufacturing employment, which occupy retail, medical, industrial, or field-based space instead.總就業中，通常會佔用傳統辦公空間的職類子集合——主要是金融活動、專業與商業服務、資訊科技與法律服務。不包含零售、休閒與住宿、醫療照護、營建與製造業就業，這些職類佔用的是零售、醫療、工業或現場型空間。——大致上是金融、專業與商業服務、資訊科技，以及法律這幾類。一個城市可能新增了倉儲、餐飲與醫院的工作機會，失業率與薪資統計都很健康，市中心的辦公大樓卻持續流失租戶，因為這些職類根本不租辦公室套房。

To translate a job-growth forecast into a square-footage demand forecast, an analyst multiplies projected office-using job growth by an assumed square-feet-per-employee ratio. That ratio is a rough, market-specific rule of thumb — commonly cited figures have historically clustered somewhere in the 150–250 sf-per-employee range depending on industry mix and building class — and it is not a fixed constant. It has been trending down for two structural reasons: open-plan densification that started well before the pandemic, and post-pandemic hybrid-work schedules that let a company sign for fewer seats than it has headcount. Any demand forecast that plugs in a static, decade-old sf-per-employee ratio will overstate the office space a given amount of job growth actually pulls through.

要把就業成長預測換算成坪數需求預測，分析師會把預估的辦公室使用就業成長，乘上一個假設的「每位員工平方英尺數」比例。這個比例只是一個粗略、因市場而異的經驗法則——常見引用的數字歷史上大致落在每員工 150 至 250 平方英尺之間，隨產業組合與大樓等級而異——而且它不是一個固定常數。它一直在往下走，有兩個結構性原因：早在疫情之前就開始的開放式辦公空間密集化，以及疫情後的混合辦公排程，讓公司可以簽下比實際員工人數更少的座位數。任何套用一個靜態、十年前比例的需求預測，都會高估某一定量的就業成長實際能拉動多少辦公空間。

⚠️
Pitfall:常見陷阱： Citing a metro's total nonfarm payroll growth as evidence of office-market strength. A market can add 3% total jobs and still lose office-using employment if the growth is concentrated in leisure, hospitality, healthcare, and warehousing — check the office-using subtotal specifically before treating a headline jobs number as bullish for office. 拿一個都會區的總非農就業成長率，當成辦公室市場強勁的證據。如果成長集中在休閒、住宿、醫療與倉儲業，一個市場的總就業成長 3%，辦公室使用就業卻可能反而在流失——在把頭條就業數字當成辦公室市場利多之前，一定要先個別檢查辦公室使用就業的子項目。

Multifamily: population growth is not household formation

住宅：人口成長不等於家戶形成

Apartment demand is driven by units, not people, and the number of units a given population needs depends on the household formation rateThe rate at which new, separate households (people living independently, whether alone, as a couple, or with roommates who each maintain their own household) are formed within a population, distinct from the population growth rate itself.在一群人口中，新的獨立家戶（無論獨居、成雙居住，或與室友同住但各自維持獨立家戶）形成的速度，與人口成長率本身是兩件不同的事。 — a distinct number from the population growth rate itself. Population can grow while household formation stalls: the clearest example is adult children delaying moving out and staying in a parent's household longer, whether from high rents, student debt, or weak entry-level job markets. A metro can add residents on net migration while the actual count of new, separate households — the number that matters for apartment absorption — grows much more slowly, because a chunk of the population growth is landing inside existing households rather than forming new ones.

公寓需求是被戶數而不是人數驅動的，而一定人口所需要的戶數，取決於家戶形成率The rate at which new, separate households (people living independently, whether alone, as a couple, or with roommates who each maintain their own household) are formed within a population, distinct from the population growth rate itself.在一群人口中，新的獨立家戶（無論獨居、成雙居住，或與室友同住但各自維持獨立家戶）形成的速度，與人口成長率本身是兩件不同的事。——這是一個跟人口成長率本身不同的數字。人口可以成長，但家戶形成卻停滯：最明顯的例子就是成年子女延後搬出、在父母家戶裡待得更久，原因可能是高房租、學貸，或疲弱的新鮮人就業市場。一個都會區可能靠淨遷入淨增加了居民，但真正新增、獨立的家戶數——這才是公寓去化真正在意的數字——成長得慢很多，因為有一部分人口成長是落進了既有家戶裡，而不是形成新的家戶。

A second layer sits on top of household formation: the renter-versus-owner tenure split within newly formed households. A market where mortgage rates and home prices push a larger share of new households toward renting (rather than buying) sees apartment demand run ahead of household formation alone; a market where affordability improves and ownership becomes more attainable sees the opposite — new households forming, but a smaller share of them renting. Reading multifamily demand correctly means checking both layers: how many new households are forming, and what share of them will rent.

在家戶形成之上還有第二層：新形成家戶中，租屋與購屋的比例分布。如果房貸利率與房價把更大比例的新家戶推向租屋（而非購屋），公寓需求成長就會跑在單純的家戶形成之前；如果可負擔性改善、購屋變得更容易入手，就會出現相反情況——新家戶照樣在形成，但租屋的比例變小。正確判讀住宅需求代表兩層都要查：有多少新家戶正在形成，以及其中有多少比例會選擇租屋。

⚠️
Pitfall:常見陷阱： Modeling apartment demand directly off a population-growth forecast without checking the household-formation rate behind it. A metro can look like a strong renter-demand story on population alone while the actual pace of new, separate households forming — the number that fills units — is running well below it. 直接用人口成長預測來建模公寓需求，卻沒有查核背後的家戶形成率。單看人口，一個都會區可能看起來像是強勁的租屋需求故事，但真正在形成、能填滿戶數的新獨立家戶速度，實際上遠低於人口成長速度。

Retail: trade area, drive-time, and gravity

零售：商圈、車程分析與重力模型

Retail demand is local in a way office and industrial demand are not: a shopping center draws customers from a defined trade areaThe geographic area from which a retail property draws the majority of its customers, typically segmented into primary (closest, highest-spend-capture), secondary, and tertiary rings or drive-time bands.一個零售物件吸引大多數顧客所來自的地理範圍，通常再細分為主要（最近、消費捕捉率最高）、次要與再次要的環圈或車程分帶。, and the analysis question is how to define that area accurately. The crude method is a simple radius ring — draw a 3-mile or 5-mile circle around the site and count the population and income inside it. The more accurate method is drive-time analysisDefining a trade area by actual travel time along the real road network (e.g. a 10-minute drive-time polygon) rather than a straight-line radius, so it naturally accounts for highways, bridges, rivers, and other barriers that shape how people actually reach a site.用實際道路網絡上的真實車程時間（例如 10 分鐘車程多邊形）而非直線半徑來定義商圈，因此能自然反映高速公路、橋樑、河流等實際影響人們如何抵達該地點的地理屏障。 — mapping how far a customer can actually drive in, say, 10 minutes along the real road network, rather than as the crow flies.

零售需求具有一種辦公室與工業需求所沒有的在地性：一座購物中心的顧客來自一個界定明確的商圈The geographic area from which a retail property draws the majority of its customers, typically segmented into primary (closest, highest-spend-capture), secondary, and tertiary rings or drive-time bands.一個零售物件吸引大多數顧客所來自的地理範圍，通常再細分為主要（最近、消費捕捉率最高）、次要與再次要的環圈或車程分帶。，而分析的問題在於如何精確定義這個範圍。粗略的方法是簡單的半徑環圈——在基地周圍畫一個 3 英里或 5 英里的圓，再算出圈內的人口與所得。更精確的方法是車程分析Defining a trade area by actual travel time along the real road network (e.g. a 10-minute drive-time polygon) rather than a straight-line radius, so it naturally accounts for highways, bridges, rivers, and other barriers that shape how people actually reach a site.用實際道路網絡上的真實車程時間（例如 10 分鐘車程多邊形）而非直線半徑來定義商圈，因此能自然反映高速公路、橋樑、河流等實際影響人們如何抵達該地點的地理屏障。——測繪顧客實際能在例如 10 分鐘內、沿著真實道路網絡開到多遠，而不是直線距離。

The reason drive-time wins is that a radius ring treats geography as if it were a featureless plane. It counts households on the far side of a river with no nearby bridge as equally reachable as households a block away. It ignores a competing shopping center two miles down the highway that intercepts most of the traffic before it reaches the subject site. Drive-time polygons pick all of that up automatically because they are built on the actual road network and can route around barriers — which is why serious retail underwriting uses drive-time as the real analysis and treats the radius ring as a first-pass screening shortcut, useful for a five-minute gut check but not for a final trade-area definition. Once the trade area is defined correctly, the next inputs are the trade area's aggregate household count and per-household spending on the retail category in question (translated from regional consumer-spending survey data), set against the square footage of competing retail already capturing that spending — the same logic as an office sf/employee ratio, just applied to dollars of spend per household instead of square feet per job.

車程分析之所以更準，是因為半徑環圈把地理當成一個沒有任何特徵的平面來處理。它會把河對岸、附近沒有橋樑可過的家戶，跟一街之隔的家戶視為同樣容易抵達。它也會忽略掉高速公路兩英里外一座正在攔截大部分車流、讓車流到不了標的基地的競爭型購物中心。車程多邊形能自動抓到這一切，因為它是建立在真實道路網絡上、能繞開屏障——這正是為什麼認真的零售核算會把車程分析當成真正的分析工具，而把半徑環圈當成初步篩選的捷徑，適合拿來做五分鐘的直覺檢查，但不適合當成最終的商圈定義。一旦商圈正確定義好，接下來的輸入就是商圈內的家戶總數，以及每戶在該零售類別上的消費支出（從區域消費支出調查資料換算而來），再對照已經在瓜分那筆支出的競爭零售面積——邏輯跟辦公室的每員工平方英尺比例一樣，只是套用在「每戶消費金額」而非「每份工作平方英尺」上。

Industrial and logistics: proximity, access, and last-mile economics

工業與物流：鄰近性、通行條件與最後一哩經濟學

Industrial demand is driven by the physics and economics of moving goods, and three location factors dominate: proximity to ports for import-heavy tenants, direct access to the interstate/highway network for anything trucked regionally, and — increasingly, the largest single driver of new industrial development — last-mile logisticsThe final leg of a delivery, from a local distribution facility to the end customer's door. Because delivery-time promises (same-day, next-day) are a fixed constraint, last-mile facilities must sit close to the population they serve regardless of land cost, which is why they cluster in dense metros even where land is far cheaper an hour away.配送流程的最後一段：從在地配送設施到終端顧客家門口。因為送貨時效承諾（當日達、隔日達）是固定限制，最後一哩設施必須緊鄰其服務的人口，無論土地成本高低，這也是為什麼即使一小時車程外土地便宜得多，這類設施仍會群聚在人口密集的都會區。 positioning near dense population centers.

工業需求是由貨物移動的物理與經濟學所驅動的，其中三個區位因素最為主導：對進口導向承租戶而言的港口鄰近性、對區域卡車運輸而言的州際公路／高速公路直接通行條件，以及——如今日益成為新增工業開發最大單一驅動力的——最後一哩物流The final leg of a delivery, from a local distribution facility to the end customer's door. Because delivery-time promises (same-day, next-day) are a fixed constraint, last-mile facilities must sit close to the population they serve regardless of land cost, which is why they cluster in dense metros even where land is far cheaper an hour away.配送流程的最後一段：從在地配送設施到終端顧客家門口。因為送貨時效承諾（當日達、隔日達）是固定限制，最後一哩設施必須緊鄰其服務的人口，無論土地成本高低，這也是為什麼即使一小時車程外土地便宜得多，這類設施仍會群聚在人口密集的都會區。——貼近人口密集區域的定位。

The reason last-mile facilities cluster near dense population regardless of cheaper land an hour away is pure delivery-time economics: a same-day or next-day delivery promise is a hard time budget, and every extra mile between the facility and the customer eats into that budget. Land a warehouse forty minutes further out might save meaningfully on rent per square foot, but if that extra distance breaks the delivery-time promise for a large share of the customer base, the cheaper land is not actually usable for that function — it just gets used for a different tier of the network (regional distribution, bulk storage) that doesn't carry a same-day constraint. This is also where the rail-served versus truck-only distinction matters: a rail-served industrial building can receive full unit trains of bulk freight at a materially lower cost per ton-mile than an equivalent truck-only facility, which matters enormously for manufacturers and bulk-commodity distributors, but is largely irrelevant to a last-mile parcel-delivery tenant, whose whole model runs on trucks and vans making short local hops. The right industrial submarket depends entirely on which of these tenant profiles the analysis is underwriting for.

最後一哩設施之所以會群聚在人口密集區、而不是選一小時車程外更便宜的土地，純粹是送貨時效的經濟學：當日達或隔日達的承諾是一個硬性的時間預算，設施與顧客之間每多一英里，都在消耗這個預算。把倉庫設在再往外四十分鐘的地方，每平方英尺租金或許能省下不少，但如果那多出來的距離讓一大部分顧客群的送貨時效承諾破功，這塊便宜的土地其實對那個功能來說並不真正可用——它只會被用在網絡裡不需要當日達限制的另一個層級（區域配送、大宗倉儲）。這也是為什麼「有無鐵路服務」與「純卡車運輸」的區分很重要：一座有鐵路服務的工業建築，能以顯著更低的每噸英里成本接收整列大宗貨運火車，這對製造業者與大宗商品配送業者來說極為重要，但對一個最後一哩包裹配送承租戶來說幾乎無關緊要，因為它的整套模式都靠卡車與貨車做短程在地接駁。正確的工業子市場選擇，完全取決於分析要核算的是哪一種承租戶輪廓。

2. Supply analysis: the pipeline math

二、供給分析：管線的算法

Supply moves through a predictable funnel: a developer files for permitsGovernment approvals filed before construction can legally begin. The deeper point behind this arithmetic is that supply is, by a wide margin, the most forecastable variable in real estate. Construction takes years from permit to delivery — long enough that a disciplined analyst can see most of a supply wave coming well before it hits the market, simply by pulling permit and under-construction data. Demand, by contrast, is genuinely hard to forecast: it depends on the future path of employment, interest rates, and consumer behavior, none of which is knowable with precision years out. And yet supply is also the variable most commonly ignored at cycle peaks — not because the data isn't available, but for a behavioral reason. Near the top of a cycle, rent growth has usually been strong for several consecutive years, and sponsors underwriting a new deal tend to extrapolate that recent, visible, emotionally salient trend forward, rather than pulling up the permit data that would show a large wave of new supply about to hit the same submarket. Recency bias on rent growth is a stronger pull on judgment than a spreadsheet column of permits filed eighteen months ago, even though the permit data is the more reliable forecast of the two.

這套算法背後更深一層的重點是：供給在房地產裡是遙遙領先、最容易被預測的變數。從許可到交屋，營建工程要花上好幾年——時間長到一個有紀律的分析師只要調出許可與興建中資料，就能在供給浪潮真正打到市場之前，看到它大部分的樣貌。相對地，需求真的很難預測：它取決於就業、利率與消費者行為未來的走向，這些在好幾年前都不可能被精確得知。然而供給也恰恰是週期高點時最常被忽略的變數——不是因為資料拿不到，而是有一個行為上的原因。在週期接近高點時，租金成長通常已經連續好幾年強勁，而正在核算新交易的發起人，傾向把這個近期、看得到、情緒上有感的趨勢往後外推，而不是去調出許可資料——那份資料會顯示一大波新供給即將打到同一個子市場。租金成長的近因偏誤，對判斷的拉力遠強過一欄十八個月前申請的建照許可資料，儘管許可資料其實是兩者之中更可靠的預測依據。

🎙 From the Trenches 來自實戰

The Sun Belt multifamily supply glut of 2023–2024 is one of the most widely documented recent examples of exactly this pattern. Low interest rates and strong in-migration into Sun Belt metros through 2021 and into 2022 pushed rent growth to some of the strongest levels those markets had seen in years, and developers responded the way the pipeline math predicts: a large wave of permits and construction starts followed. Because apartment construction takes roughly two years from groundbreaking to delivery, that wave landed on the same markets in 2023 and 2024 — right as the migration and rent-growth tailwind that triggered it in the first place was cooling. The result, widely reported across the industry's data providers, was a sharp deceleration in rent growth and a rise in concessions across several fast-growing Sun Belt metros, concentrated almost entirely in the submarkets that had absorbed the heaviest share of new deliveries. None of this required unusual foresight to see coming — the permit data was public well before the deliveries hit.

2023 至 2024 年間陽光地帶（Sun Belt）住宅市場的供給過剩，是這個模式近期最廣為人知的紀錄之一。2021 年到 2022 年間，低利率與強勁的淨遷入把陽光地帶多個都會區的租金成長推到多年來最強勁的水準，而開發商的反應也正如管線算法所預測的：緊接著出現一大波建照許可與動工申請。由於公寓興建從動工到交屋大約要花兩年時間，這一波供給浪潮正好在 2023 年與 2024 年打到同一批市場——恰好是當初觸發這波移民與租金成長順風的力道正在降溫的時候。結果，業界多家資料供應商都廣泛報導了這件事：多個高速成長的陽光地帶都會區出現租金成長急速放緩、租讓誘因上升，而且幾乎全數集中在那些吸收了最大比例新交屋量的子市場。這一切完全不需要什麼異於常人的先見之明才看得出來——許可資料在交屋量打到市場之前，早就是公開的。

3. Submarket selection: path of growth vs. established core

三、子市場選擇：成長路徑對比成熟核心區

Once demand and supply are understood at the metro level, the practical decision is which submarket within that metro to target, and the classic framework splits into two poles. An established core submarket already has the infrastructure, amenities, and tenant base in place — the risk is mostly priced in, competition for deals is intense, and basis (price per unit or per square foot) tends to run at a premium. A path-of-growth submarket is betting on where demand is headed rather than where it already sits — typically an area with improving infrastructure, rezoning momentum, or spillover demand from an adjacent, more expensive core, where basis is lower precisely because the market hasn't fully priced in the growth yet.

一旦在都會區層級理解了需求與供給，接下來實務上的決策就是要鎖定該都會區裡的哪一個子市場，而經典的框架分成兩極。成熟核心區子市場已經具備基礎建設、生活機能與承租戶基礎——風險大多已反映在價格裡，交易競爭激烈，而基準單價（每戶或每平方英尺價格）往往偏高。成長路徑子市場則是在賭需求未來的走向，而不是它現在所在的位置——通常是基礎建設正在改善、重劃有動能，或從鄰近、更昂貴的核心區溢出需求的地區，其基準單價之所以較低，正是因為市場還沒把成長完全反映進價格裡。

Transit-driven submarket transformation is one of the most consistently repeated patterns across global real estate markets: cities that have opened new rail or metro lines have, across many documented studies and market cycles, seen land and commercial values near the new stations rise measurably faster than comparable areas without transit access, with the effect typically strongest within a short walk of the station and fading with distance. The pattern holds across very different cities, transit technologies, and decades, which is part of why it's treated as one of the more reliable, non-speculative theses in submarket selection — it isn't a bet on a single project's execution, it's a bet on a well-documented general mechanism. The caveat submarket analysts learn quickly is timing: the value lift tends to price in well before a station actually opens, once funding is secured and the alignment is finalized, so buying "on the transit thesis" after ribbon-cutting day is usually buying after most of the easy repricing has already happened.

4. Market-level tightness metrics

四、市場緊度指標

Vacancy rate is the most quoted number in market analysis and also the most misleading one when read in isolation, because it is a stock measure — a snapshot of the current ratio of empty to total space — while the forces that will move it forward are flow measures. Reading a market's real-time trajectory correctly means looking past the single vacancy number to three things: the relationship between absorption and deliveries, the trend in vacancy rather than its level, and effective rent rather than headline asking rent.

空置率是市場分析裡最常被引用的數字，但單獨拿它來看時也是最容易誤導的一個，因為它是一個存量指標——目前空置與總空間比例的一張快照——而真正將推動它未來走向的力量，是流量指標。要正確判讀一個市場的即時軌跡，必須看穿單一的空置率數字，改看三件事：淨去化量與交屋量的關係、空置率的趨勢而非其水準，以及有效租金而非表價租金。

This reads cleaner than the vacancy level alone because vacancy is a lagging stock number that only reports where things already stand, while absorption-versus-deliveries reports which direction things are actively moving right now, in the same period being measured.

It is the cleanest real-time read on demand because it nets out both sides of tenant movement in one number.一段期間內已佔用空間的淨變化——新承租的空間減去退租的空間。因為它把租戶進出雙向的動態淨算成一個數字，是判讀當下需求最乾淨的指標。與同期的交屋量相比，是判讀緊度最乾淨的即時指標：當去化量超過交屋量時，市場正在緊縮——已佔用空間的成長速度快過總存量，所以空置率其實已經機械性地在下降，即使空置率這個數字本身還沒追上去、還沒顯示出來。當交屋量超過去化量時，情況正好相反，即使當下的空置率水準看起來還算舒適。這比單看空置率水準更乾淨，因為空置率是一個落後的存量數字，只回報目前已經走到哪裡，而去化量對比交屋量，回報的是同一段被測量的期間內，事情正朝哪個方向積極移動。

Vacancy trend vs. vacancy level

空置率趨勢對比空置率水準

Two markets can both sit at 8% vacancy and be in completely different positions in their cycle. A market at 8% and falling from 12% a year ago is tightening — landlords are gaining leverage, concessions are likely shrinking, and rent growth is probably accelerating. A market at 8% and rising from 5% a year ago is loosening — even though the current level is identical, the trajectory says landlords are losing leverage and concessions are probably growing. An analyst or underwriter who reads only the current 8% and treats both markets the same is missing the entire story; the trend line, not the snapshot, tells you which direction pricing power is headed.

兩個市場可能都停在 8% 的空置率，卻處於週期裡截然不同的位置。一個市場現在 8%、去年是 12% 一路降下來，這是在緊縮——房東正在取得議價優勢，租讓誘因很可能在縮小，租金成長很可能正在加速。另一個市場現在 8%、去年是 5% 一路升上來，這是在鬆動——儘管目前的水準完全相同，走勢卻顯示房東的議價優勢正在流失，租讓誘因很可能正在擴大。一個只看當下 8% 這個數字、把兩個市場一視同仁的分析師或核算人員，會漏掉整個故事；告訴你定價力量正朝哪個方向走的，是趨勢線，而不是那張快照。

Effective rent vs. asking rent

A market can report flat or even slightly positive asking-rent growth while its real economics are deteriorating, because landlords facing weaker demand hold the headline number steady (a stated rent cut looks bad in a broker's marketing package and can be read by other tenants as landlord weakness) and instead quietly widen the concession package: more months of free rent, a bigger TI allowance. The mechanics: a tenant signing a five-year lease at a headline rent that looks unchanged from last year, but with two extra months of free rent and $10 more per square foot in TI than last year's comparable deal, is paying a materially lower effective rent — the deterioration is real, it's just been moved from the number everyone quotes into the fine print of the lease abstract.

Tokyo's extreme tightness reflects, on the supply side, genuine land scarcity plus a slow, permit-gated development process that keeps new office deliveries structurally limited relative to demand, and on the demand side a large, durable base of office-using employment concentrated in a geographically constrained core. 🎙 From the Trenches 來自實戰

Tokyo and Chicago office vacancy sitting at 0.7% and 26.8% respectively, in the same asset class in the same reporting period, is worth sitting with for a moment rather than treating as a footnote. It is a reminder that "the office market" is not a single global story with local variations — it is dozens of genuinely independent local markets that happen to share an asset-class label, each shaped by its own supply constraints, its own demand base, and its own building-stock age profile. A capital allocator reading a national or global office cap-rate average and concluding anything useful about a specific submarket from it is making close to the same mistake as reading a national jobs number and concluding something about office-using employment specifically: the aggregate number is real, but it isn't the number that determines the outcome on the ground.

東京與芝加哥的辦公室空置率，在同一資產類別、同一報告期間，分別是 0.7% 與 26.8%，這件事值得花點時間好好體會，而不是當成一個附註帶過。它提醒我們，「辦公室市場」不是一個帶著在地變化的全球單一故事——而是數十個真正各自獨立的在地市場，只是剛好共用同一個資產類別標籤，各自由自己的供給限制、自己的需求基礎，以及自己的建築存量年齡結構所塑造。一個資金配置者讀著全國或全球的辦公室資本化率平均值，就對某個特定子市場下結論，跟讀著全國就業數字，就對辦公室使用就業本身下結論，犯的幾乎是同一種錯誤：加總數字是真實的，但它不是決定實地結果的那個數字。

7. Data source hierarchy: broker research, government stats, and REIT disclosures

七、資料來源的可信度層級：經紀商研究、政府統計與 REIT 揭露

A practitioner without a Wall Street research subscription has three realistic, freely or cheaply accessible source categories, and each has a different tradeoff between speed, bias, and granularity. Reading all three together, and knowing which to trust when they disagree, is a skill on its own.

一個沒有華爾街研究訂閱的實務工作者，實際上能取得的免費或低成本資料來源有三大類，各自在速度、偏誤與細緻度之間有不同的取捨。把三者一起讀、並且知道彼此矛盾時該信誰，本身就是一項技能。

Broker research — the quarterly market reports published free by the major global brokerage research shops (CBRE, JLL, Cushman & Wakefield, Colliers, and similar firms) — is fast, covers essentially every market the firm operates in, and is genuinely useful for a directional read: current vacancy, recent absorption, headline rent, and a qualitative narrative about the market's momentum. The tradeoff is that this research is produced by firms whose core business is transacting in the same markets they're reporting on, so the framing tends to run marketing-flavored and directionally optimistic — a market described as "resilient" or "well-positioned" in a brokerage report is a real data point, but it should be read with a discount for the fact that the firm publishing it benefits from transaction activity in that market.

經紀商研究——由全球主要經紀商研究部門（CBRE、JLL、Cushman & Wakefield、Colliers 等類似機構）免費發布的季度市場報告——速度快，涵蓋該機構有業務的幾乎每一個市場，對於方向性判讀確實有用：目前空置率、近期去化量、頭條租金，以及對市場動能的定性敘事。取捨在於，這些研究是由核心業務就是在同一批市場裡促成交易的機構所產出的，所以論述傾向帶有行銷色彩、方向上偏樂觀——經紀商報告裡把一個市場描述成「有韌性」或「處於有利位置」，是一個真實的數據點，但讀的時候應該打個折扣，因為發布這份報告的機構，本身就受惠於該市場的交易活動。

Government statistics — employment data (such as the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics' payroll surveys), census population and household data, and building-permit survey data published by national statistical agencies — are the slowest of the three to update and the least granular at the submarket level, but they carry no transactional bias whatsoever: no one publishing a permit survey benefits from a specific building getting built or not. This is the right source for the metro-level employment, population, and permit inputs that feed directly into the demand and supply frameworks earlier in this module.

政府統計——就業資料（例如美國勞工統計局的薪資調查）、普查人口與家戶資料，以及各國統計機構發布的建照許可調查資料——是這三者之中更新最慢、在子市場層級最不細緻的，但完全沒有交易面的偏誤：發布許可調查的機構不會因為某棟特定建築有蓋或沒蓋而受益。這是本課前面需求與供給框架所需要的都會區層級就業、人口與許可輸入資料的正確來源。

REIT public disclosures — annual and quarterly filings (10-Ks and 10-Qs) and earnings-call commentary from publicly listed real estate investment trusts — sit in a distinct category: same-store NOI and occupancy figures reported by a REIT describe a real, specific portfolio of buildings the REIT actually owns and operates, disclosed under securities-law accuracy obligations rather than marketing incentives. A REIT that misstates its same-store performance in a public filing faces securities-fraud exposure, not just a bad look, which is a materially stronger accuracy incentive than a brokerage marketing report faces. The tradeoff is coverage: a REIT's disclosures only describe the specific buildings and submarkets it happens to own, not the market as a whole, so they function as high-confidence "ground truth" spot-checks on specific submarkets rather than a comprehensive market read.

REIT 公開揭露——上市不動產投資信託的年度與季度申報文件（10-K 與 10-Q）以及財報電話會議上的說明——屬於一個獨立的類別：REIT 揭露的同店淨營運收益與出租率數字，描述的是一批 REIT 真正持有並營運的具體建築物，而且是在證券法的準確性義務下揭露的，而不是出於行銷誘因。一家 REIT 若在公開申報文件裡誤報同店表現，面對的是證券詐欺的法律風險，不只是名聲不好看，這是遠比經紀商行銷報告所面對更強的準確性誘因。取捨在於涵蓋範圍：一家 REIT 的揭露只描述它剛好持有的特定建築物與子市場，不是整個市場，所以它的功能是對特定子市場做高可信度的「地面真實」抽查，而不是全面性的市場判讀。
