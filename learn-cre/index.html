CRE Investing: From First Principles to the Closing Table

商用不動產投資課：從第一性原理到交割桌

Real buildings and real numbers first. Formulas second. Every module ends with a quiz and most start with a deal that actually happened.

先看真實建築物與真實數字，公式其次。每個模組結尾都有測驗，多數模組則以一筆真實發生過的交易開場。

Most commercial real estate education starts with formulas — cap rate, IRR, DSCR — presented as abstractions to memorize before ever looking at a real deal. That order breaks on contact with an actual acquisition, because every formula hides a judgment call (whose NOI, which quarter's rent roll, whose comps) that the formula itself never teaches anyone to make.

多數商用不動產教學從公式開始——資本化率、內部報酬率、償債保障比——要求在看到任何一筆真實交易之前先背下來。這個順序一接觸真實收購就會失靈，因為每個公式背後都藏著一個判斷——用誰的淨營運收益、哪一季的租金明細、誰的可比案例——而公式本身從不教人怎麼做這個判斷。

Real deals expose the judgment calls a formula hides: the gap between a broker's pro-forma NOI and a lender's underwritten NOI, why two "comparable" cap rates in the same submarket can sit 150 basis points apart, what a rent roll doesn't say. This course runs in the opposite order from most textbooks — a real building with real numbers first, the formula that explains it second.

真實交易會逼出公式藏起來的判斷：仲介的預估淨營運收益，跟放款方核保後的淨營運收益差在哪裡；同一個次市場裡兩個「可比」資本化率，為什麼能差到一百五十個基點；租金明細表沒寫出來的東西是什麼。本課程把多數教科書的順序倒過來——先看一棟有真實數字的真實建築物，再學解釋它的公式。

Retrieval beats re-reading. Every module closes with a short quiz — not to grade, but because forcing recall is what moves a concept from "read once" to "usable under pressure," the same pressure a closing table applies. The war-story boxes throughout are drawn from deals that lost real money, because the lessons a market teaches for free are usually the ones someone else already paid six figures to learn.

主動回想比重複閱讀有效。每個模組結尾都有一個小測驗——目的不是打分數，而是強迫回想，這才是把一個概念從「讀過一次」推進到「壓力下用得出來」的機制，而交割桌施加的正是這種壓力。課程中穿插的「來自實戰」案例框取材自真實虧錢的交易，因為市場免費教會你的教訓，通常是別人已經付出六位數代價換來的。

How This Course Teaches本課程的教學方法
Concrete before abstract具體先於抽象

Every concept opens with one real building's numbers, not a variable name.

每個概念都先從一棟真實建築物的數字談起，而不是先給一個變數名稱。

Worked examples逐步演算範例

Full calculations shown step by step, not just the final answer.

完整計算過程逐步呈現，而非只給最終答案。

Retrieval practice主動回想測驗

Short quizzes force recall instead of passive re-reading.

簡短測驗強迫主動回想，而非被動重讀。

Case-based learning案例式學習

Real deals — wins and losses — carry the lesson, not abstract rules.

真實交易——成功與失敗——承載教訓，而非抽象規則。

Interleaving交錯練習

Topics mix and reappear across modules rather than being learned once and dropped.

主題交錯穿插並在不同模組反覆出現，而非學完一塊就丟開。

Learn-by-doing calculators動手試算器

Sliders let you move one variable and watch the deal's numbers change in real time.

拉桿讓你調整單一變數，即時看到交易數字如何變化。
