The Rate Backdrop

利率背景

Every cap rate on the dashboard sits on top of a local risk-free rate, and as of August 2026 those risk-free rates span an enormous range: Switzerland 0.35%, China 1.68% (Shanghai and Beijing both price off the same national curve), Taiwan 1.94%, Singapore 2.26%, Japan 2.86%, Germany 3.14% (Berlin, Frankfurt, Munich), the U.S. 4.65% (and the Gulf, proxied off the USD curve given SAR/AED pegs and thin local sovereign-bond liquidity), the UK 4.96%, Mexico 9.14%, and Brazil 14.72%. A post-zero-interest-rate world means cap-rate anchoring is no longer a single global conversation — it's dozens of separate local conversations, each starting from a different risk-free baseline that itself has moved dramatically since the 2010s.

儀表板上的每一個資本化率，都建立在當地無風險利率之上，而截至 2026 年 8 月，這些無風險利率的區間非常寬：瑞士 0.35%、中國 1.68%（上海與北京採用同一條國家殖利率曲線）、台灣 1.94%、新加坡 2.26%、日本 2.86%、德國 3.14%（柏林、法蘭克福、慕尼黑）、美國 4.65%（波灣地區因沙幣／迪拉姆與美元掛鉤、加上當地主權債流動性稀薄，也以美元曲線代理）、英國 4.96%、墨西哥 9.14%，以及巴西 14.72%。一個走出零利率時代的世界，代表資本化率的定錨已經不再是單一的全球對話——而是幾十場各自獨立的在地對話，每一場都從一個自 2010 年代以來劇烈變動過的不同無風險基準出發。

The single clearest regime change inside that range is Japan. For most of the 2010s and into the early 2020s, the Bank of Japan held policy rates at or below zero and 10-year JGB yields hovered near zero — the widely documented backdrop that let Tokyo cap rates sit in the low-3% range and still look like a comfortable spread trade. As the BOJ has moved off negative and near-zero rates through the mid-2020s, that anchor has shifted materially: the 10-year JGB now sits around 2.86% in this dataset, a multi-percentage-point move from a near-zero base within a single decade. Every cap rate in Japan that was priced assuming the old anchor is, by construction, being re-tested against a new one.

這個區間裡最明確的一次「政權轉換」發生在日本。整個 2010 年代到 2020 年代初，日本央行的政策利率長期維持在零或負值，十年期日本公債（JGB）殖利率也貼近於零——這是廣泛紀錄在案的背景，讓東京的資本化率能停在 3% 出頭，看起來仍是一筆舒服的利差交易。隨著日銀在 2020 年代中期逐步走出負利率與近零利率政策，這個定錨已經出現實質變化：本資料集中，十年期 JGB 目前約在 2.86%，短短十年內從接近零的基準移動了好幾個百分點。任何當初以舊定錨定價的日本資本化率，如今都必然正在被拿去對照一個新的基準重新檢驗。

🎙 Live Case: This Cycle 這一輪週期的現在進行式

The Bank of Japan's exit from negative rates — the policy rate lifted off -0.1% in March 2024, followed by further hikes as inflation ran persistently above the BOJ's 2% target — is not a closed historical event; it's a policy path still unfolding as this page is written. It matters far beyond bond math, because Japan's near-zero-rate era is the single most important reason Tokyo cap rates could sit in the low-3% range for so long and still clear the market: with a risk-free rate near zero, even a thin nominal spread was, in relative terms, wide. Module 06 walks through the resulting arithmetic in detail — a roughly 24-basis-point spread between Tokyo's ~3.10% office cap rate and Japan's ~2.86% 10-year yield, the thinnest cushion of any major market in this dataset. Whether that cushion holds, widens, or gets tested by a further BOJ move is one of the most consequential open questions in global cap-rate anchoring right now — and unlike a historical case study, it hasn't finished playing out.

日本央行走出負利率——2024 年 3 月政策利率脫離 -0.1%，隨後又因通膨持續高於日銀 2% 目標而多次升息——並不是一段已經蓋棺論定的歷史事件；在本頁寫成的當下，這條政策路徑仍在進行中。它的重要性遠遠超出債券算術本身，因為日本這段近零利率的年代，正是東京資本化率能長期停在 3% 出頭、卻仍能順利成交的最主要原因：當無風險利率接近零時，即使名目利差很薄，相對而言也算寬裕。第 06 課詳細拆解過這道算術——東京約 3.10% 的辦公室資本化率，對照日本約 2.86% 的十年期殖利率，利差僅約 24 個基點，是本資料集所有主要市場中緩衝最薄的一個。這段緩衝究竟會撐住、回補加寬，還是被日銀下一步動作進一步壓縮，是目前全球資本化率定錨中最具影響力、也最懸而未決的問題之一——而且不同於一個歷史案例研究，它還沒有結束。

Sector Reads

各板塊現況

Office: the great bifurcation

辦公室：巨大的分化

Nowhere in the dataset is the split between markets starker than office. On one side sit the U.S. CBD-distressed gateways: Chicago's downtown office vacancy runs roughly 26.8%, San Francisco roughly 29.2%, Seattle 25.2%, Dallas 25.5%, Atlanta 24.9%, Houston 23.9% — a cluster of major metros where a quarter or more of Class A/B stock sits empty. The spreads those cap rates carry over their local (uniformly 4.65%) 10-year Treasury are correspondingly wide: San Francisco roughly +2.7 percentage points, Miami +2.35pp, Toronto +2.96pp, Montreal +3.12pp, Chicago +3.85pp. Per Module 06's lesson, that width is the market pricing real, specific vacancy risk — not a bargain sitting on the table.

在整份資料集裡，沒有哪個板塊的市場分化像辦公室這麼劇烈。一端是美國市中心的困境型門戶市場：芝加哥市中心辦公室空置率約 26.8%，舊金山約 29.2%，西雅圖 25.2%，達拉斯 25.5%，亞特蘭大 24.9%，休士頓 23.9%——這一群主要都會區，有四分之一以上的甲乙級辦公室存量閒置。這些資本化率相對於當地（統一為 4.65%）十年期公債的利差也相應偏寬：舊金山約正 2.7 個百分點，邁阿密 +2.35pp，多倫多 +2.96pp，蒙特婁 +3.12pp，芝加哥 +3.85pp。依照第 06 課的教訓，這個寬度是市場在為真實、具體的空置風險定價——不是留在桌上沒人撿的便宜貨。

On the other side sits APAC and the Gulf, where office vacancy is often the tightest on the planet: Tokyo 0.7%, Taipei 5.1%, Seoul 6.6%, Riyadh 0.5%, Abu Dhabi 0.1%. Europe reads in between — Amsterdam's office vacancy is a moderate 5.6%, Munich 8.8%, Frankfurt 11.1%, Paris 11.4%, with prime-scarcity stories layered on top of broader softness (Munich's logistics vacancy, discussed below, is essentially zero). The honest structural-vs-cyclical question this data can raise but not settle: is the U.S. office overhang a permanent, hybrid-work-driven repricing of demand per employee, or a cyclical overbuild-and-lease-rollover lag that resolves as the 2020-2022 vintage of leases resets at lower rents and gets absorbed? Cross-market comparison narrows the honest range of answers — comparably wealthy, comparably white-collar APAC and Gulf economies did not see anything close to a 25%+ office vacancy blowout, which argues at least part of the U.S. story is structural rather than purely cyclical — but the dataset alone cannot prove the split between the two forces, and treating either explanation as fully settled would be overreaching.

另一端則是亞太與波灣地區，那裡的辦公室空置率往往是全球最緊俏的：東京 0.7%，台北 5.1%，首爾 6.6%，利雅德 0.5%，阿布達比 0.1%。歐洲則介於兩者之間——阿姆斯特丹辦公室空置率屬中等的 5.6%，慕尼黑 8.8%，法蘭克福 11.1%，巴黎 11.4%，並在較普遍的疲軟之上疊加了「核心稀缺」的故事（下文會提到，慕尼黑的物流空置率幾乎是零）。這份數據能提出、但無法自行解答的誠實結構性 vs 週期性問題是：美國辦公室的過剩，究竟是混合辦公永久改變了「每位員工需要多少辦公空間」這個結構性重新定價，還是 2020 至 2022 年一波過度開發加上租約到期時滯的週期性問題，會隨著那一批租約陸續以更低租金續約、被市場逐步吸收而化解？跨市場比較能縮小合理答案的範圍——同樣富裕、同樣以白領為主的亞太與波灣經濟體，並未出現接近 25% 以上的辦公室空置率爆量，這說明美國的故事至少有一部分是結構性、而非純粹週期性的——但單憑這份資料集，無法證明兩股力量之間確切的分割比例，把任何一種解釋當成完全定案，都是過度推論。

🎙 Live Case: This Cycle 這一輪週期的現在進行式

The office-distress numbers above aren't abstract — they showed up directly on U.S. regional-bank balance sheets. Silicon Valley Bank and Signature Bank failed in March 2023, and in the stress-testing that followed, regulators and analysts repeatedly flagged mid-size U.S. banks' concentrated exposure to commercial real estate — office loans above all — as a slow-burning risk distinct from the deposit-run mechanics that took down SVB itself. Through 2023 and into 2024, the dominant pattern for maturing office loans at many lenders was "extend and pretend": modifying and extending loans rather than forcing sales into a market clearing at the wide, distressed spreads this page just described, on the bet that values would eventually recover before a forced reckoning. That bet was tested publicly in early 2024, when a major regional lender surprised markets with a sharply larger-than-expected loan-loss provision tied to its office and rent-regulated multifamily book, cut its dividend, and saw its stock fall by more than a third in a single trading session — a live reminder that Chicago's and San Francisco's wide spreads aren't just a pricing curiosity on a dashboard; they're a claim on bank balance sheets that hasn't fully worked its way through the system.

上面提到的辦公室困境數字，並不是抽象數據——它們直接反映在美國區域銀行的資產負債表上。矽谷銀行與 Signature Bank 於 2023 年 3 月倒閉，之後的壓力測試中，監管機關與分析師一再點名：美國中型銀行對商用不動產——尤其是辦公室貸款——的集中曝險，是一個與擠兌本身不同、正在緩慢延燒的風險。整個 2023 年到 2024 年初，許多放款機構處理到期辦公室貸款的主流做法是「展延裝睡」（extend and pretend）：修改並展延貸款，而不是把資產強制拋售進一個以本頁前述那種寬、困境型利差成交的市場，賭的是價值最終會回升，趕在被迫攤牌之前。這場賭注在 2024 年初被公開檢驗：一家主要區域銀行公布財報時，其與辦公室及租金管制多戶住宅相關的放款損失提列，遠超市場預期，隨後大砍股息，股價單日重挫超過三分之一——這是一個活生生的提醒：芝加哥與舊金山的寬利差，不只是儀表板上的一個定價趣聞，它們是銀行資產負債表上一筆尚未在體系裡完全走完流程的求償。

Logistics: normalizing off the e-commerce peak

物流：從電商高峰回歸常態

Logistics remains the institutional favorite sector by spread quality — a healthier, tighter distribution than office, with a median spread around +1.0pp and a narrower Q1–Q3 band of roughly +0.6pp to +1.4pp across the dataset's 35 tracked logistics markets. But "favorite" doesn't mean "still booming." Rent momentum has gone negative in several of the same gateway markets that drove the 2020-2022 e-commerce boom: NYC logistics rent is down roughly 10% year-over-year (steepest in Brooklyn), Los Angeles down roughly 7%, Toronto down roughly 5.4%, Paris down roughly 5%, Montreal down roughly 3.4%. Vacancy has climbed well past pandemic-era lows in a few markets too — Seoul logistics vacancy sits around 21.3%, Shanghai around 25.1%, Boston around 12.4%. At the same time, a handful of European industrial markets are essentially full: Munich logistics vacancy is about 0.1%, Frankfurt about 0.5%. Read together, this looks like sector-wide normalization off an unsustainable 2021-2022 peak rather than a demand collapse — the median spread hasn't blown out — but the gateway-market rent declines are real and worth tracking as a possible early hypersupply tell in exactly those submarkets.

以利差品質而言，物流仍是機構投資人最偏好的板塊——分布比辦公室健康、緊密，本資料集追蹤的 35 個物流市場中，中位數利差約為正 1.0 個百分點，Q1 到 Q3 的區間也較窄，約在 +0.6pp 到 +1.4pp 之間。但「最受偏好」不代表「還在狂飆」。租金動能已在幾個曾帶動 2020 至 2022 年電商榮景的門戶市場轉為負值：紐約物流租金年減約 10%（布魯克林跌幅最深），洛杉磯年減約 7%，多倫多年減約 5.4%，巴黎年減約 5%，蒙特婁年減約 3.4%。部分市場的空置率也已明顯攀升、遠超疫情期間的低點——首爾物流空置率約 21.3%，上海約 25.1%，波士頓約 12.4%。與此同時，少數歐洲工業市場幾乎滿租：慕尼黑物流空置率約 0.1%，法蘭克福約 0.5%。整體來看，這比較像整個板塊從 2021 至 2022 年不可持續的高峰回歸常態，而不是需求崩潰——中位數利差並未大幅擴張——但門戶市場的租金下滑是真實的，值得當成這些特定次市場潛在供給過剩的早期訊號來追蹤。

Retail: bifurcation continues

零售：分化持續

Retail shows the same pattern as office — extremes on both ends — but with fewer markets carrying a published cap rate (34 of 57). Prime scarcity stories dominate the tight end: London retail trades at a deeply negative spread (roughly -2.1pp, cap rate ~2.88% against a 4.96% gilt yield) on prime vacancy of just 2.2%, and Tokyo, Singapore, and Auckland all carry wide positive spreads on very different underlying stories. The wide end is a mix of genuinely oversupplied secondary retail (Manila retail vacancy ~13.1%, Auckland ~12.9%) and markets like Montreal, where a +3.5pp retail spread over a moderate 3.63% Canadian bond looks more like conservative underwriting on a smaller, thinner-traded market than acute distress. The lesson that carries over from office: a wide or narrow spread on its own is not a verdict — it's an invitation to ask what specific risk, or specific scarcity, the number is pricing.

零售呈現與辦公室相似的型態——兩端都出現極值——但擁有公開資本化率的市場較少（57 座城市中僅 34 座）。緊俏端以核心稀缺故事為主：倫敦零售的利差深度為負（約 -2.1pp，資本化率約 2.88%，對照 4.96% 的英國公債殖利率），核心零售空置率僅 2.2%；東京、新加坡與奧克蘭則各自基於截然不同的底層故事，都呈現寬幅正利差。寬幅端則混合了真正供給過剩的次級零售市場（馬尼拉零售空置率約 13.1%，奧克蘭約 12.9%），以及像蒙特婁這樣的市場——其零售利差高達 +3.5pp，對照相對溫和的 3.63% 加拿大公債殖利率，看起來比較像是對一個規模較小、成交較薄市場的保守估值，而非急性困境。從辦公室板塊延續下來的教訓是：利差寬或窄，本身不是定論——它是一個邀請，邀請你去問這個數字究竟在為哪種具體風險、或哪種具體稀缺定價。

The Regional Heat Map, in Words

區域現況地圖（文字版）

Grouping the 57 tracked cities by geography and macro regime, rather than reading each one in isolation, is where the dataset starts to tell a coherent story. Each cluster below is a read, not a rule — and each comes with what would change it.

把追蹤的 57 座城市按地理與總經制度分群、而不是逐一孤立解讀，正是這份資料集開始講出一個連貫故事的地方。以下每一群都是一個研判，不是一條規則——每一群也都附上會讓這個判斷改變的條件。

U.S. office-distressed gateways

美國辦公室困境型門戶市場

NYC, San Francisco, Chicago, Boston, Seattle, and Washington DC all carry office vacancy at or above the high-teens, several well past 25%, with wide spreads (SF +2.7pp, Chicago +3.85pp) pricing that distress directly. Manhattan is the exception-within-the-cluster worth noting: citywide vacancy is ~13.1%, but Midtown prime vacancy has fallen to roughly 2.2% — a flight-to-quality bifurcation happening inside a single metro, not just across metros. What would change this read: two or more consecutive quarters of positive net absorption citywide, or spreads compressing toward the office-market median (~+1.2pp) without a corresponding rate cut.

紐約、舊金山、芝加哥、波士頓、西雅圖與華盛頓特區，辦公室空置率都在接近或超過 20% 上下，數個已遠超 25%，寬幅利差（舊金山 +2.7pp，芝加哥 +3.85pp）直接為這種困境定價。曼哈頓值得注意的是這個群組內部的例外：全市空置率約 13.1%，但中城核心區的空置率已降至約 2.2%——這是發生在同一個都會區「內部」、而不只是跨都會區的「品質逃離」分化。會讓這個判斷改變的條件：連續兩季以上出現正的全市淨吸納，或利差在沒有對應降息的情況下，向辦公室市場中位數（約 +1.2pp）收斂。

U.S. Sunbelt — growth without absorption, yet

美國陽光帶——成長尚未轉化為吸納

Dallas (25.5% office vacancy, +3.1% rent), Houston (23.9%, +1.2%), and Atlanta (24.9%, +0.9%) are population- and job-growth markets whose office vacancy is, counterintuitively, as bad as or worse than the coastal gateways above — modest positive rent growth suggests demand hasn't collapsed, but it hasn't come close to absorbing the standing vacant stock either. Miami is the outlier that breaks the pattern: 12.5% office vacancy and +6.5% rent growth, a genuinely tighter market. What would change this read: Dallas/Houston/Atlanta vacancy turning down for two or more consecutive readings, which would confirm demographic growth is finally translating into office absorption rather than just headline job counts.

達拉斯（辦公室空置率 25.5%，租金 +3.1%）、休士頓（23.9%，+1.2%）與亞特蘭大（24.9%，+0.9%），都是人口與就業成長型市場，其辦公室空置率卻反直覺地與上述沿海門戶市場一樣糟、甚至更糟——溫和的正租金成長顯示需求並未崩潰，但也遠遠稱不上已經吸納完現有的閒置存量。邁阿密是打破這個型態的例外：辦公室空置率 12.5%，租金成長 +6.5%，是真正較緊俏的市場。會讓這個判斷改變的條件：達拉斯／休士頓／亞特蘭大的空置率連續兩次以上的讀數出現下降，這將證實人口成長終於開始轉化為辦公室吸納，而不只是頭條的就業人數。

Canada

加拿大

Toronto (14.4% office vacancy, +2.96pp spread), Vancouver (11.6%, +2.27pp), and Montreal (9.2%, +3.12pp) sit in a middle zone — visibly softer than pre-pandemic norms but nowhere near the 25%+ vacancy of the worst U.S. gateways, while logistics rent momentum has turned negative in both Toronto (-5.4%) and Montreal (-3.4%), echoing the broader North American logistics normalization. What would change this read: a further leg down in logistics rent alongside rising logistics vacancy would suggest the normalization is turning into genuine oversupply rather than a peak cooling off.

多倫多（辦公室空置率 14.4%，利差 +2.96pp）、溫哥華（11.6%，+2.27pp）與蒙特婁（9.2%，+3.12pp），落在一個中間地帶——明顯比疫情前的常態疲軟，但遠不及美國最糟門戶市場 25% 以上的空置率；而物流租金動能在多倫多（-5.4%）與蒙特婁（-3.4%）都已轉負，呼應了北美物流板塊更廣泛的正常化。會讓這個判斷改變的條件：若物流租金再進一步下探、同時物流空置率持續上升，將顯示這波正常化正在轉為真正的供給過剩，而不只是高峰後的降溫。

LatAm high-nominal

拉美：高名目殖利率

São Paulo (14.72% bond, office spread roughly -5.5pp, retail spread roughly -6.9pp) and Mexico City (9.14% bond, no published office cap rate, 17.6% vacancy) are the two clearest reminders that a high local bond yield is not automatically a "safe" comparison point — a large share of both countries' nominal yields compensates for currency and inflation risk, not a neutral risk-free baseline, exactly as Module 06 walks through for São Paulo specifically. What would change this read: a sustained fall in Brazil's or Mexico's 10-year yield toward developed-market levels would signal the currency/inflation premium easing, at which point office and retail spreads that look deeply negative today would need to be re-benchmarked from scratch.

聖保羅（公債殖利率 14.72%，辦公室利差約 -5.5pp，零售利差約 -6.9pp）與墨西哥市（公債殖利率 9.14%，未公開辦公室資本化率，空置率 17.6%），是最清楚提醒「當地公債殖利率高，不代表它就是一個『安全』的比較基準」的兩個例子——兩國名目殖利率中有相當大一部分，補償的是貨幣與通膨風險，而不是中性的無風險基準，這正是第 06 課針對聖保羅具體拆解過的道理。會讓這個判斷改變的條件：若巴西或墨西哥的十年期殖利率持續下滑、朝已開發市場水準靠攏，代表貨幣／通膨溢酬正在緩解，屆時今天看起來深度為負的辦公室與零售利差，都需要重新從頭校準基準。

Europe core

歐洲核心

London (office spread -1.08pp, retail -2.08pp, both driven by prime scarcity rather than broad tightness), Paris (11.4% office vacancy, logistics rent -5.0%), Amsterdam (5.6% office vacancy, comparatively tight), and Germany's big three (Berlin/Frankfurt/Munich office vacancy 8.6–11.1%, but logistics vacancy near-zero at 0.1–0.5%) collectively read as a market where headline office softness coexists with sharp, narrow scarcity — logistics scarcity in Germany, prime-only scarcity in London and Paris. What would change this read: German office vacancy climbing toward the low-to-mid teens would suggest the softness is broadening beyond secondary stock into the market's core.

倫敦（辦公室利差 -1.08pp，零售 -2.08pp，兩者都是核心稀缺驅動、而非全面緊俏）、巴黎（辦公室空置率 11.4%，物流租金 -5.0%）、阿姆斯特丹（辦公室空置率 5.6%，相對緊俏），以及德國三大城（柏林／法蘭克福／慕尼黑辦公室空置率 8.6% 到 11.1%，但物流空置率接近於零，介於 0.1% 到 0.5%），整體讀起來像是一個 headline 辦公室疲軟、卻與尖銳而狹窄的稀缺並存的市場——德國是物流稀缺，倫敦與巴黎則是僅限核心地段的稀缺。會讓這個判斷改變的條件：若德國辦公室空置率攀升到十幾％的中段，將顯示疲軟正從次級存量擴散到市場核心。

Nordics & CEE

北歐與中東歐

Stockholm (3.75% office cap, +0.78pp spread), Copenhagen, and Oslo read as stable, low-drama developed markets, while Warsaw (6.0% office cap, +8.5% rent growth, +0.18pp spread on a much higher 5.82% local bond) and Prague show the CEE pattern of much higher nominal cap rates and bond yields moving together, keeping spreads thin despite double-digit-adjacent rent growth in Warsaw's case. What would change this read: a further widening of Poland's or the Czech Republic's bond-cap rate gap from current levels would suggest CEE risk premia are being repriced independently of Western Europe.

斯德哥爾摩（辦公室資本化率 3.75%，利差 +0.78pp）、哥本哈根與奧斯陸，讀起來是穩定、少波瀾的已開發市場；而華沙（辦公室資本化率 6.0%，租金成長 +8.5%，利差僅 +0.18pp，對照高出許多的 5.82% 當地公債殖利率）與布拉格，則呈現中東歐典型的型態——名目資本化率與公債殖利率一起走高，即使像華沙這樣接近雙位數的租金成長，利差依然偏薄。會讓這個判斷改變的條件：若波蘭或捷克的公債與資本化率缺口從目前水準進一步擴大，將顯示中東歐的風險溢酬正獨立於西歐重新定價。

Japan — regime repricing

日本——政權式重新定價

Tokyo (3.10% office cap, 0.7% vacancy, ~24bp spread) and Osaka (4.10% cap, 3.0% vacancy, +1.24pp spread, +10% rent growth) sit at opposite ends of the country's own spread spectrum, but both share the same overhead question: what happens to a market's cap rate when the risk-free anchor it was priced against has moved several points in a decade. What would change this read: Osaka's spread compressing in lockstep with Tokyo's would suggest a systemic Japan-wide repricing rather than a Tokyo-specific trophy-asset liquidity premium holding cap rates artificially low.

東京（辦公室資本化率 3.10%，空置率 0.7%，利差約 24 個基點）與大阪（資本化率 4.10%，空置率 3.0%，利差 +1.24pp，租金成長 +10%），站在日本自身利差光譜的兩端，但兩者共享同一個懸而未決的大哉問：當一個市場的資本化率賴以定價的無風險利率定錨，十年內移動了好幾個百分點，這個市場的資本化率究竟會怎麼走？會讓這個判斷改變的條件：若大阪的利差與東京同步壓縮，將顯示這是一場系統性、全日本範圍的重新定價，而不只是東京特有的招牌資產流動性溢酬，人為壓低了資本化率。

Greater China — its own cycle

大中華——自成一格的週期

Shanghai (19.3% office vacancy) and Beijing (14.9% vacancy, office rent -8.0% YoY) sit against a China 10-year bond yield of 1.68% — one of the lowest in the entire dataset, widely read as a market signal of persistent growth and deflation concern rather than confidence. Hong Kong (3.6% office cap, 19.5% vacancy, ~+0.01pp spread) trades at a barely-positive spread despite vacancy nearly matching Shanghai's, a reminder that Hong Kong's currency board and capital-market depth keep it priced closer to a global gateway than to the mainland cycle next door. What would change this read: Beijing office rent stabilizing (no longer declining YoY) for two or more consecutive readings, or China's 10-year yield rising off its current multi-decade-low range, would both point toward the deleveraging cycle turning.

上海（辦公室空置率 19.3%）與北京（空置率 14.9%，辦公室租金年減 8.0%），對照的是中國十年期公債殖利率 1.68%——是本資料集中最低的水準之一，普遍被解讀為市場對持續成長趨緩與通縮疑慮的訊號，而非信心。香港（辦公室資本化率 3.6%，空置率 19.5%，利差僅約 +0.01pp）儘管空置率幾乎與上海相當，利差卻僅是微幅為正——這提醒我們，香港的聯繫匯率與資本市場深度，讓它的定價更貼近一個全球門戶市場，而不是隔壁的中國大陸週期。會讓這個判斷改變的條件：若北京辦公室租金連續兩次以上的讀數不再年減、趨於穩定，或中國十年期殖利率從目前數十年低點區間回升，兩者都將指向這一輪去槓桿週期正在轉向。

Southeast Asia — oversupply overhang

東南亞——供給過剩懸而未解

Bangkok (23.8% office vacancy, rent -0.7%), Jakarta (24.0%), Ho Chi Minh City (10.2%, rent -0.9%), Manila (19.4%, rent essentially flat), and Kuala Lumpur (21.8%, rent +1.7%) form a broadly oversupplied cluster where vacancy is high across the board and rent momentum is flat to negative in most of it — India's Mumbai (15.6% vacancy, rent +3.4%) reads somewhat better within the same broad supply-heavy emerging-Asia bucket. Singapore is the sharp exception: 3.3% office vacancy, +5.0% rent growth, +1.24pp spread — a genuinely tight market sitting inside a region defined by the opposite. What would change this read: two or more of the oversupplied SEA markets turning vacancy down simultaneously would suggest regional absorption is finally catching a multi-year supply wave, rather than one market's local story.

曼谷（辦公室空置率 23.8%，租金 -0.7%）、雅加達（24.0%）、胡志明市（10.2%，租金 -0.9%）、馬尼拉（19.4%，租金幾乎持平）與吉隆坡（21.8%，租金 +1.7%），共同組成一個廣泛供給過剩的群組——空置率全面偏高，租金動能在多數市場持平或轉負；印度孟買（空置率 15.6%，租金 +3.4%）在同一個供給沉重的新興亞洲大類裡，讀起來相對好一些。新加坡是鮮明的例外：辦公室空置率 3.3%，租金成長 +5.0%，利差 +1.24pp——在一個被反方向定義的區域裡，是一個貨真價實緊俏的市場。會讓這個判斷改變的條件：若東南亞供給過剩市場中有兩個以上同時出現空置率下降，將顯示區域吸納力終於追上了一波持續多年的供給浪潮，而不只是單一市場的個別故事。

ANZ

澳紐

Sydney (21.5% office vacancy, +6.6% rent), Melbourne (20.5%, +5.2%), and Auckland (15.8%) all run vacancy well above the region's historical norms despite decent rent growth in Sydney and Melbourne — a pattern of demand and pricing power coexisting with a large standing vacant stock. Brisbane is the exception: 7.7% vacancy, +11.7% rent growth, the tightest and best-momentum office market in the cluster. What would change this read: Sydney or Melbourne vacancy falling meaningfully below 18% would suggest the region's absorption is finally catching up with its post-2020 supply pipeline.

雪梨（辦公室空置率 21.5%，租金 +6.6%）、墨爾本（20.5%，+5.2%）與奧克蘭（15.8%），儘管雪梨與墨爾本的租金成長還算不錯，空置率卻都遠高於區域歷史常態——這是一種需求與定價能力，與大量閒置存量並存的型態。布里斯本是例外：空置率 7.7%，租金成長 +11.7%，是這個群組裡最緊俏、動能也最好的辦公室市場。會讓這個判斷改變的條件：若雪梨或墨爾本的空置率明顯降至 18% 以下，將顯示該區域的吸納力終於追上 2020 年後的供給管線。

Gulf — tight and expanding

波灣——緊俏且擴張中

Dubai (6.0% office vacancy, +14.1% rent), Riyadh (0.5% vacancy, +3.0% rent), and Abu Dhabi (0.1% vacancy, +11.7% office rent, +19.0% logistics rent) form the tightest cluster in the entire dataset by vacancy, with rent momentum running well ahead of most developed markets. But this reads as a market with essentially no slack left rather than one comfortably mid-cycle — Dubai's own +3.85pp office spread, discussed above, hints the cap-rate side is already pricing meaningful sovereign/liquidity risk even as occupancy fundamentals look pristine. What would change this read: any uptick in vacancy off these near-zero floors, or the first appearance of sublease space in broker reporting, would be the earliest tell that new supply is starting to catch up with the current expansion.

杜拜（辦公室空置率 6.0%，租金 +14.1%）、利雅德（空置率 0.5%，租金 +3.0%）與阿布達比（空置率 0.1%，辦公室租金 +11.7%，物流租金 +19.0%），以空置率而言，是整份資料集裡最緊俏的一個群組，租金動能也遠遠領先多數已開發市場。但這讀起來比較像一個幾乎沒有任何餘裕的市場，而不是一個舒服地處於週期中段的市場——杜拜自身 +3.85pp 的辦公室利差（前文已討論）暗示，即使出租率基本面看起來近乎完美，資本化率這一側其實已經在為相當程度的主權／流動性風險定價。會讓這個判斷改變的條件：只要空置率從這些接近於零的低點開始出現任何回升，或仲介報告首次出現轉租空間，都會是新供給開始追上目前擴張的最早訊號。

Spread Discipline

利差判讀紀律

Across the 43 of 57 tracked cities carrying a published office cap rate, the office spread distribution runs from roughly -5.5pp (São Paulo) to +3.85pp (Chicago and, separately, Dubai), with a first quartile around +0.3pp, a median around +1.2pp, and a third quartile around +2.0pp. Logistics (35 cities) runs a tighter band — Q1 ≈ +0.6pp, median ≈ +1.0pp, Q3 ≈ +1.4pp — and retail (34 cities) is wider again, Q1 ≈ +0.4pp, median ≈ +0.9pp, Q3 ≈ +1.5pp, with São Paulo's retail spread the single widest outlier on the downside at roughly -6.9pp. Only about four of the 43 office markets — São Paulo, Manila, London, and Seoul — trade at a genuinely negative spread, and each is a different story.

在追蹤的 57 座城市中，有 43 座公開了辦公室資本化率，其利差分布從約 -5.5pp（聖保羅）到約 +3.85pp（芝加哥，以及各自獨立的杜拜），第一四分位約 +0.3pp，中位數約 +1.2pp，第三四分位約 +2.0pp。物流（35 座城市）的區間較窄——Q1 約 +0.6pp，中位數約 +1.0pp，Q3 約 +1.4pp；零售（34 座城市）則再度較寬，Q1 約 +0.4pp，中位數約 +0.9pp，Q3 約 +1.5pp，聖保羅的零售利差是下側唯一的極端離群值，約 -6.9pp。在這 43 個辦公室市場中，只有約四個——聖保羅、馬尼拉、倫敦與首爾——的利差是真正為負，而且每一個背後都是不同的故事。

四個負或薄利差，四個不同的故事

🎙 Live Case: This Cycle 這一輪週期的現在進行式

Spread discipline runs into a second, related trap: not every printed cap rate updates at the same speed as the market it's supposed to represent. Starting in late 2022, Blackstone's non-traded Real Estate Income Trust (BREIT) — priced on appraisal-based net asset value rather than a public market quote — saw investor redemption requests exceed its stated monthly and quarterly repurchase limits for months at a stretch, forcing it to gate (partially fulfill) redemptions. Public REITs holding comparable assets were, over the same stretch, trading at meaningful discounts to their own net asset values as public markets repriced office and other CRE exposure faster and more visibly than appraisal-based marks did. Other large non-traded vehicles, including a comparable KKR fund, faced similar redemption pressure around the same period. The episode is a live, ongoing lesson in the same discipline this section teaches: a cap rate — whether it's a broker's printed number on a dashboard or an appraiser's mark feeding a fund's NAV — is a claim about where a market clears, and appraisal-based marks can lag the public price discovery that a liquid market provides, sometimes for a long stretch before the gap closes.

利差判讀紀律，還會撞上第二個相關的陷阱：並不是每一個公告的資本化率，更新速度都跟它理應代表的市場一樣快。從 2022 年底開始，黑石集團的非上市不動產收益信託（BREIT）——以估價師評估的淨資產價值計價，而非公開市場報價——連續數月遭遇贖回請求超過其公告的每月與每季贖回上限，被迫「閘門管制」（部分履行）贖回。同一段期間，持有可比資產的上市 REIT，因為公開市場對辦公室及其他 CRE 曝險重新定價的速度更快、也更明顯，其股價相對自身淨資產價值出現了顯著折價。其他大型非上市基金，包括一檔可比的 KKR 基金，在同一時期也面臨類似的贖回壓力。這起事件，是本節所教的同一項紀律一個活生生、仍在進行中的例證：一個資本化率——不論它是儀表板上仲介公告的數字，還是餵入基金淨值的估價師評估值——本質上都是「市場在哪裡出清」的一個主張，而以估價為基礎的評估值，可能落後於流動性市場所提供的公開價格發現，有時這個落差會持續很長一段時間才收斂。

The Cycle-Position Call

週期定位研判

Everything below is framed as 研判 — a research judgment made from the data available right now — not a prediction of fact. Each call uses Module 06's four-quadrant model (Recovery, Expansion, Hypersupply, Recession) and comes with 2–3 falsifiable markers: specific, checkable signals that, if they show up, mean this particular call was wrong.

以下所有內容，都以「研判」的方式框定——這是根據目前可取得的數據所做出的研究判斷——而不是對未來事實的預測。每一則判斷，都套用第 06 課的四象限模型（復甦期、擴張期、供給過剩期、衰退期），並搭配 2 到 3 個可證偽的觀察指標：具體、可查核的訊號，一旦出現，就代表這個特定判斷是錯的。

U.S. office: late Recession, edging toward Recovery — unconfirmed

美國辦公室：衰退期尾聲，正在邊緣試探復甦期——尚未確認

Vacancy near or above 25% across several gateway metros, combined with spreads wide enough to be pricing real distress (Chicago +3.85pp, SF +2.7pp), looks most consistent with late Recession — the phase where lagging data has fully caught up with the oversupply/demand-shift story, and where landlords are still the desperate party. Whether the market is starting to tip into Recovery (vacancy stopping its rise, absorption quietly turning positive before headline numbers confirm it) is exactly what this dataset's snapshot vacancy and spread numbers cannot directly show — recovery is defined by a change in direction, not a level, and a single reading can't show direction.

數個門戶都會區的空置率接近或超過 25%，加上利差已寬到足以為真實困境定價（芝加哥 +3.85pp，舊金山 +2.7pp），這最貼近衰退期尾聲——這個階段，落後數據已經完全追上供給過剩／需求轉移的故事，房東仍是急著求成交的一方。市場是否正開始傾向復甦期（空置率停止上升，吸納力在headline數字證實之前悄悄轉正），正是本資料集這份快照式的空置率與利差數字無法直接顯示的——復甦是由「方向的改變」所定義，而不是「水位」，單一次讀數無法顯示方向。

  • This call is wrong if: sublease space (not tracked in this dataset — check current CBRE/JLL/Cushman quarterly reports) keeps growing rather than shrinking, which would say the market is still deep in Recession, not edging toward Recovery.這個判斷會錯，如果：轉租空間（本資料集未追蹤——需查核最新的 CBRE／JLL／高力國際季報）持續成長而非萎縮，這將顯示市場仍深陷衰退期，而非正邊緣試探復甦期。
  • This call is wrong if: Chicago's or San Francisco's office spread widens further from today's already-elevated level without a corresponding local rate move — a further widening would say the market still hasn't finished pricing in the risk, not that it's stabilizing.這個判斷會錯，如果：芝加哥或舊金山的辦公室利差，在沒有對應的當地利率變動下，從今天已經偏高的水準進一步擴大——進一步擴大將顯示市場尚未定價完風險，而不是正在趨穩。
  • This call is wrong if: construction starts pick up meaningfully in any of these metros — cranes reappearing during still-record-high vacancy would be a red flag of premature, narrative-driven building, not evidence the cycle has turned.這個判斷會錯，如果：這些都會區中，任何一個的動工量出現顯著回升——在空置率仍處歷史高檔時，工地吊車重新出現，會是敘事驅動、過早動工的紅旗，而不是週期已經轉向的證據。

Logistics: mid-cycle normalization

物流：週期中段的正常化

A healthy median spread (~+1.0pp, tighter Q1–Q3 band than office) alongside negative rent momentum concentrated in a handful of former boom gateways (NYC -10%, LA -7%, Toronto -5.4%, Paris -5%) reads as mid-cycle normalization — cooling off an unsustainable 2021-2022 peak — rather than a sector-wide turn toward Hypersupply or Recession.

健康的中位數利差（約 +1.0pp，Q1 到 Q3 的區間也比辦公室緊密），加上負租金動能集中在少數幾個曾經榮景的門戶市場（紐約 -10%，洛杉磯 -7%，多倫多 -5.4%，巴黎 -5%），讀起來像是週期中段的正常化——從 2021 到 2022 年不可持續的高峰降溫——而不是整個板塊轉向供給過剩期或衰退期。

  • This call is wrong if: the median logistics spread across the dataset widens materially past its current Q3 (~+1.4pp), which would say markets are re-rating for structural oversupply, not just normalizing off a peak.這個判斷會錯，如果：資料集中的物流利差中位數，明顯超過目前的 Q3（約 +1.4pp）持續擴大，這將顯示市場正在為結構性供給過剩重新定價，而不只是從高峰降溫。
  • This call is wrong if: vacancy in Seoul (21.3%) or Shanghai (25.1%) logistics keeps climbing rather than stabilizing over the next several readings.這個判斷會錯，如果：首爾（21.3%）或上海（25.1%）的物流空置率，在接下來幾次讀數中持續上升而非趨於穩定。

Gulf: Expansion, with no visible slack left

波灣：擴張期，且已無明顯餘裕

Sub-1% vacancy in Riyadh and Abu Dhabi, plus double-digit rent growth across the cluster, is a textbook Expansion-quadrant read — but Expansion is also, per Module 06, the phase where the next cycle's oversupply gets planted, via construction decisions this dataset cannot directly observe.

利雅德與阿布達比不到 1% 的空置率，加上整個群組雙位數的租金成長，是教科書等級的擴張期讀數——但根據第 06 課，擴張期同時也是下一輪供給過剩種子被種下的階段，透過的是這份資料集無法直接觀察到的動工決策。

  • This call is wrong if: vacancy in Riyadh or Abu Dhabi ticks up off these near-zero floors — even a small move off 0.1–0.5% would be an early tell of new supply catching up.這個判斷會錯，如果：利雅德或阿布達比的空置率，從目前接近於零的低點開始回升——即使只是從 0.1% 到 0.5% 小幅移動，也會是新供給正在追上的早期訊號。
  • This call is wrong if: rent momentum across the cluster decelerates sharply from the current +11% to +19% range toward single digits, suggesting the tightest phase has already passed.這個判斷會錯，如果：整個群組的租金動能，從目前 +11% 到 +19% 的區間快速降溫到個位數，代表最緊俏的階段已經過去。

Japan: a regime-shift special case, not a standard quadrant

日本：政權轉換的特殊個案，不是標準四象限

Tokyo's combination of the tightest vacancy in the dataset (0.7%) with the thinnest spread cushion (~24bp) doesn't map cleanly onto Recovery/Expansion/Hypersupply/Recession — those quadrants describe a supply-demand cycle, and Tokyo's supply-demand fundamentals look like late Expansion or even a structurally tight steady state. The live risk is a separate axis entirely: the risk-free anchor itself resetting under a cap rate that has almost no room left to absorb it.

東京同時擁有本資料集最緊俏的空置率（0.7%）與最薄的利差緩衝（約 24 個基點），這個組合無法乾脆地套進復甦期／擴張期／供給過剩期／衰退期——這四個象限描述的是供需週期，而東京的供需基本面看起來像是擴張期尾聲、甚至是結構性緊俏的穩態。真正活生生的風險，其實在一條完全不同的軸線上：無風險利率定錨本身正在重設，而資本化率幾乎已沒有空間去吸收這個變化。

  • This call is wrong if: Tokyo's cap rate rises over the next several dashboard refreshes without a corresponding fall in demand — that would say the spread cushion is being restored through price, resolving the imbalance this section flags.這個判斷會錯，如果：東京的資本化率在接下來幾次儀表板更新中上升，而需求並未相應下降——這將顯示利差緩衝正透過價格重新回補，化解本節所指出的失衡。
  • This call is wrong if: the BOJ pauses or reverses its normalization path, which would remove the pressure this call depends on entirely.這個判斷會錯，如果：日銀暫停或逆轉其政策正常化路徑，這將完全移除這個判斷所依賴的壓力來源。

China: its own deleveraging cycle, decoupled from the rest of APAC

中國：自成一格的去槓桿週期，與亞太其他地區脫鉤

Beijing's -8.0% office rent YoY alongside a 14.9% vacancy rate, and Shanghai's 19.3% vacancy, against a China 10-year yield of just 1.68% (widely read as a low-growth/deflation-concern signal), read as Recession — but a China-specific one, decoupled from the tightness visible in Tokyo, Taipei, Seoul, and Singapore elsewhere in APAC. This is not a regional cycle; it's a distinct national deleveraging story.

北京辦公室租金年減 8.0%、空置率 14.9%，加上上海空置率 19.3%，對照中國十年期殖利率僅 1.68%（普遍被解讀為低成長／通縮疑慮訊號），讀起來像是衰退期——但這是一個中國特有的衰退期，與亞太其他地區在東京、台北、首爾、新加坡所見的緊俏脫鉤。這不是一個區域性週期；而是一個獨立的國家級去槓桿故事。

  • This call is wrong if: Beijing office rent stops declining (turns flat or positive YoY) for two or more consecutive readings.這個判斷會錯，如果：北京辦公室租金連續兩次以上的讀數，不再年減（轉為持平或年增）。
  • This call is wrong if: China's 10-year yield rises meaningfully off its current sub-1.7% range, signaling reflation expectations are taking hold.這個判斷會錯，如果：中國十年期殖利率從目前低於 1.7% 的區間明顯回升，代表再通膨預期正在成形。

What This Means for a Learner

對學習者的意義

If you're evaluating a specific deal right now, this page is a starting lens, not a substitute for underwriting it yourself. A few direct pointers: evaluating a U.S. office deal — re-read Module 06's cycle mechanics and Module 04's valuation methods before letting this page's cycle call bias your own numbers. Comparing cap rates across two countries — re-read the spread-discipline section above and Module 06's São Paulo/Tokyo/Chicago cases before treating a headline spread number as comparable across currencies. Sizing a Gulf or APAC allocation — treat the falsifiable markers in the cycle-position-call section as your literal watch-list, not this page's framing as a standing conclusion.

如果你正在評估一筆具體的交易，本頁是一個起手的視角，不能取代你自己親手做的估值評估。幾個直接的指引：評估美國辦公室交易——先重讀第 06 課的週期機制與第 04 課的估值方法，再讓本頁的週期判斷影響你自己的數字。跨國比較資本化率——先重讀上方的利差判讀紀律段落，以及第 06 課的聖保羅／東京／芝加哥案例，再把一個headline利差數字當成可以跨貨幣直接比較的東西。評估波灣或亞太的配置規模——把週期定位研判段落裡的可證偽觀察指標，當成你自己真正要盯的清單，而不是把本頁的框架當成一個恆常不變的結論。
