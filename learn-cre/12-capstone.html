🎙 From the Trenches 來自實戰

In 2006, Tishman Speyer and BlackRock paid $5.4 billion for Manhattan's Stuyvesant Town–Peter Cooper Village, underwriting to a pro-forma that assumed thousands of rent-stabilized apartments would convert to market rate far faster than New York rent law actually allowed. Actual NOI never came close to tracking that projected path. By 2010 the venture defaulted on its debt and handed the property back to its lenders — one of the largest real estate losses of that cycle, built on the same mechanism Task 2 below asks you to spot: a projected NOI that leaned on income that had not been legally secured yet.

2006 年，Tishman Speyer 與 BlackRock 以 54 億美元買下曼哈頓的 Stuyvesant Town–Peter Cooper Village，核保時採用的預估值假設數千戶租金管制公寓能比紐約租金法實際允許的速度更快轉為市場租金。實際淨營運收益從未接近那條預測路徑。到了 2010 年，這筆投資對其債務違約，將物件交還給放款方——那個週期裡最大的不動產虧損案例之一，其構成機制正是下方任務二要你抓出來的那一種：預估的淨營運收益，倚賴的是尚未經過法律程序確保的收入。

Why This Order

為什麼是這個順序

The eight tasks below are not eight unrelated questions — they run in the order a working underwriter actually runs them, and each one gates the next. NOI has to be pinned to actuals (Task 1) before a pro-forma gap even means anything (Task 2). Once the gap is named, it has to be repriced into a cap rate (Task 3) and stress-tested against the loan the deal is actually financed with (Task 4) before it is safe to look past income entirely to lease structure (Task 5) and exit assumptions (Task 6). Only once all of that is on the table does a decision (Task 7) mean anything more than a guess — and the synthesis (Task 8) exists because real risk rarely arrives as one clean problem at a time.

下面八個任務不是八個互不相干的問題——它們是照一個實際在做核保的人真正會走的順序排列，前一題會卡住後一題。淨營運收益必須先釘在實際數字上（任務一），預估值的落差才有意義可言（任務二）。一旦落差被指認出來，就得重新反映進資本化率（任務三），並用這筆交易真正的融資條件做壓力測試（任務四），之後才能放心地把視角從收入本身，移到租約結構（任務五）與出場假設（任務六）。所有這些都攤在桌上之後，一個決定（任務七）才有意義、而不只是用猜的——而綜合判斷（任務八）之所以存在，是因為真實風險很少一次只丟出一個乾淨的問題。

This mirrors the arc of Modules 02 through 11: NOINet Operating Income — rental and other property income minus operating expenses, before debt service and capital expenditure.淨營運收益——租金與其他物業收入，扣除營運費用後、尚未扣除還款與資本支出前的金額。 discipline from reading a deal (Module 03), valuation logic from the three-way framework (Module 04), the debt mechanics of amortization and balloon terms (Module 05), the cap-rate-expansion math that hits every exit regardless of property performance (Module 06), and the deal-structuring instinct that turns a broken number into a negotiated fix rather than a dead deal (Module 09). The capstone does not introduce new formulas. It tests whether the formulas can be run in the right order, under time pressure, on numbers that were never designed to be easy.

這呼應了第 02 到 11 課的整個脈絡：來自「看懂一個 Deal」（第 03 課）的淨營運收益紀律、來自估值三法框架（第 04 課）的估值邏輯、攤還與氣球到期的債務機制（第 05 課）、無論物件表現如何都會打到每一次出場的資本化率擴張數學（第 06 課）、以及把一個算不過關的數字變成可談判的解方、而非直接宣告交易死亡的架構直覺（第 09 課）。這場畢業考沒有引入任何新公式，它測試的是：在時間壓力下、面對從一開始就不打算讓人輕鬆算過的數字，能不能把這些公式用對的順序跑一遍。

One caution before starting: none of the eight tasks below can be solved by pattern-matching a keyword in the question to a formula. Two of the eight are pure arithmetic, three ask what a correct-looking number is hiding, and the last three ask for a judgment call that a spreadsheet cannot make on its own.

開始之前先提醒一點：下面八個任務裡，沒有一個能靠把題目裡的關鍵字對應到某個公式就解出來。八題裡有兩題是純算術，三題問的是一個看起來正確的數字背後藏了什麼，最後三題則需要試算表自己做不出來的判斷。

Worked Example逐步演算範例
  1. 1 Start with Gross Potential Rent from the rent roll: $1,439,250. 從租金明細表的毛潛在租金開始：$1,439,250。
  2. 2 Add expense reimbursements/CAM recoveries: $1,439,250 + $186,000 = $1,625,250. 加上費用回收／共用區域維護費回收：$1,439,250 + $186,000 = $1,625,250。
  3. 3 Subtract actual T-12 vacancy and credit loss: $1,625,250 − $64,000 = $1,561,250. 減去 T-12 實際空置與呆帳損失：$1,625,250 − $64,000 = $1,561,250。

$1,196,600 is now the fixed anchor for the rest of this exercise. Every later task either compares something to it, discounts something back from it, or grows something forward from it — which is exactly why it had to be pinned down first, before any comparison to the marketed number could mean anything.

用任務一算出的實際 T-12 淨營運收益，在同一個 2,345 萬美元開價下重新算出進場資本化率，並跟這筆交易行銷用的 5.75% 相比。

Worked Example逐步演算範例
  1. 1 Actual T-12 NOI (from Task 1): $1,196,600. 實際 T-12 淨營運收益（來自任務一）：$1,196,600。
  2. 2 Asking price: $23,450,000. 開價：$23,450,000。
  3. 3 Going-in cap rate = NOI ÷ price: $1,196,600 ÷ $23,450,000 = 5.10%. 進場資本化率＝淨營運收益÷價格：$1,196,600 ÷ $23,450,000 = 5.10%。
  4. 4 Compare to the marketed rate: $1,348,150 (pro-forma NOI) ÷ $23,450,000 = 5.75%. 與行銷資本化率相比：1,348,150 美元（預估淨營運收益）÷ 23,450,000 美元 ＝ 5.75%。
  5. 5 The 65bp gap (5.75% marketed vs. 5.10% actual) is the direct fingerprint of the pro-forma inflation identified in Task 2. 這 65 個基點的落差（行銷 5.75% 對比實際 5.10%）正是任務二找出的預估值灌水的直接指紋。

A cap rate answers whether the price is fair for the income. It says nothing about whether the loan the buyer is actually using to close can be serviced by that same income — which is a separate test, run on a different threshold, with a different failure mode.

資本化率回答的是：這個價格對這筆收益來說算不算公道。它完全沒有回答另一件事：買方實際用來成交的這筆貸款，能不能靠同一筆收益償還——這是另一項測試，用不同的門檻，會用不同的方式失敗。

TASK 4 OF 8 任務四／共八個任務

Compute DSCR at the Quoted Loan

用報價貸款算出償債保障比率

The loan quote — 65% LTV, 6.25%, 30-year amortization, 5-year term — is the same structure flagged in Module 05: a 30-year amortization schedule paired with a 5-year balloon that forces a refinancing event at year five, not year thirty. Before that refinancing question even arises, the loan has to clear its DSCR covenant today. Run the covenant test on both NOI figures.

報價貸款條件——LTV 65%、利率 6.25%、30 年攤還、5 年期——正是第 05 課點名過的結構：30 年攤還排程搭配 5 年期氣球到期，會在第五年、而非第三十年就逼出一次再融資事件。但在談到那個再融資問題之前，這筆貸款今天就得先過償債保障比率條款這一關。分別用兩個淨營運收益數字做一次條款測試。

Worked Example逐步演算範例
  1. 1 Loan amount = 65% × $23,450,000 = $15,242,500. 貸款金額＝65% × $23,450,000 ＝ $15,242,500。
  2. 2 Monthly rate = 6.25% ÷ 12 ≈ 0.5208%; amortization term n = 360 months. 月利率＝6.25% ÷ 12 ≈ 0.5208%；攤還期數 n ＝ 360 個月。
  3. 3 Standard amortizing-loan payment formula gives an estimated monthly payment of roughly $93,850. 用標準攤還貸款還款公式算出，估計每月還款額約為 $93,850。
  4. 4 Annual debt service ≈ $93,850 × 12 ≈ $1,126,200. 年度還款額 ≈ $93,850 × 12 ≈ $1,126,200。
  5. 5 DSCR on marketed pro-forma NOI: $1,348,150 ÷ $1,126,200 ≈ 1.20x — appears to clear a typical covenant. 用行銷用預估淨營運收益算的 DSCR：$1,348,150 ÷ $1,126,200 ≈ 1.20 倍——看起來剛好過常見條款門檻。
  6. 6 DSCR on actual T-12 NOI: $1,196,600 ÷ $1,126,200 ≈ 1.06x — fails a typical 1.20x–1.25x covenant. 用實際 T-12 淨營運收益算的 DSCR：$1,196,600 ÷ $1,126,200 ≈ 1.06 倍——過不了常見的 1.20 至 1.25 倍條款門檻。

In practice, a DSCR shortfall like this one rarely just kills a deal outright — a lender who sees 1.06x against a 1.20x requirement typically responds by sizing the loan down to whatever proceeds actual NOI can support, rather than lending against the marketed number and hoping the pro-forma catches up. A smaller loan means more buyer equity is required to close at the same price, which is exactly the kind of gap Task 7 asks how to close.

實務上，像這樣的 DSCR 缺口很少直接讓一筆交易破局——放款方看到 1.06 倍對上 1.20 倍的要求，通常的反應是把貸款金額縮減到實際淨營運收益所能支撐的規模，而不是照行銷數字放款、再期待預估值日後追上來。貸款金額變小，代表買方要用更多股權才能以同樣的價格成交，而這正是任務七要問的、該怎麼補上的缺口。

TASK 5 OF 8 任務五／共八個任務

WALT and the Rollover Cliff

加權平均到期年限與到期懸崖

WALT compresses eight lease expirations into a single rent-weighted number. A single number is easy to read — and easy to be misled by. Compute it, then look at what it is averaging over.

加權平均到期年限（WALT）把八份租約的到期日壓縮成單一個租金加權數字。單一數字很好讀——也很容易誤導人。先算出這個數字，再看看它到底在對什麼東西取平均。

Worked Example逐步演算範例
  1. 1 WALT = sum of (years remaining × annual rent) for every tenant, divided by total annual rent. 加權平均到期年限＝每個承租戶（剩餘年數 × 年租金）加總後，除以總年租金。
  2. 2 Summing all eight tenants' (years remaining × annual rent) products gives roughly $4,347,600. 把八個承租戶的（剩餘年數 × 年租金）加總，約得出 $4,347,600。
  3. 3 Divide by total annual rent: $4,347,600 ÷ $1,439,250 ≈ 3.02 years — the WALT that looks acceptable in isolation. 除以總年租金：$4,347,600 ÷ $1,439,250 ≈ 3.02 年——單獨看起來相當可以接受的 WALT。
  4. 4 But three office tenants — Cascade ($288,000), Evergreen ($208,000), and Harbor ($100,000) — all expire in 2027, within roughly an 18-month window of each other: $596,000 combined. 但三個辦公室承租戶——Cascade（288,000 美元）、Evergreen（208,000 美元）、Harbor（100,000 美元）——全都在 2027 年到期，彼此相距約 18 個月：合計 $596,000。
  5. 5 $596,000 ÷ $1,439,250 ≈ 41.4% of in-place rent clustered in that one window — the concentration a 3.0-year average does not show. $596,000 ÷ $1,439,250 ≈ 41.4% 的在收租金集中在那一個窗口內——這正是 3.0 年平均值沒有顯示出來的集中度。
🎙 From the Trenches 來自實戰

Westfield San Francisco Centre carried anchor leases from Nordstrom and other department stores that had underpinned its income for years. When Nordstrom announced its departure in 2022 amid falling downtown foot traffic, and other tenants followed, the mall's owner, Unibail-Rodamco-Westfield, stopped payments on the property's loan and handed the keys back to its lenders in 2023. The mechanism was not one bad quarter — it was concentrated tenant risk meeting a loan that needed steady income to service, the same combination Task 8 below asks about.

Westfield San Francisco Centre 多年來的收入，靠的是 Nordstrom 與其他百貨公司主力承租戶撐起的租約。2022 年，隨著市中心人流下滑，Nordstrom 宣布撤出，其他承租戶也陸續跟進，該購物中心的業主 Unibail-Rodamco-Westfield 於 2023 年停止繳付物件貸款，並將鑰匙交還給放款方。這個機制不是單一個壞季度造成的——而是集中的承租戶風險，撞上一筆需要穩定收入才能還款的貸款，正是下方任務八要問的同一種組合。

TASK 6 OF 8 任務六／共八個任務

Stress the Exit Cap

為出場資本化率做壓力測試

Nothing about the rent roll or the loan has to change for this deal to lose value at exit — only the market's appetite for risk has to shift. Hold the five-year NOI growth path fixed and test what a 100 basis point move in the exit cap rate alone does to the number the buyer walks away with at sale.

這筆交易在出場時要虧掉價值，並不需要租金明細表或貸款本身出任何變化——只要市場的風險胃納改變就夠了。固定五年的淨營運收益成長路徑，測試單單出場資本化率移動 100 個基點，會對買方賣出時拿到的數字造成什麼影響。

Worked Example逐步演算範例
  1. 1 Actual T-12 NOI base: $1,196,600. 實際 T-12 淨營運收益基期：$1,196,600。
  2. 2 Grow at roughly 3%/year for a 5-year hold to get forward Year-6 NOI: $1,196,600 × (1.03)^5 ≈ $1,387,000. 以每年約 3% 成長、持有五年，得出前瞻第六年淨營運收益：$1,196,600 × (1.03)^5 ≈ $1,387,000。
  3. 3 Exit value at an unchanged 5.75% cap: $1,387,000 ÷ 0.0575 ≈ $24,120,000. 若出場資本化率維持 5.75% 不變：$1,387,000 ÷ 0.0575 ≈ $24,120,000。
  4. 4 Exit value at a stressed 6.75% cap (+100bp, per the cap-rate-expansion math from Module 06): $1,387,000 ÷ 0.0675 ≈ $20,550,000. 若出場資本化率壓力測試至 6.75%（+100 個基點，依第 06 課的資本化率擴張數學）：$1,387,000 ÷ 0.0675 ≈ $20,550,000。
  5. 5 Value reduction from the cap move alone: ($24,120,000 − $20,550,000) ÷ $24,120,000 ≈ 14.8%, with the underlying NOI forecast unchanged. 單單資本化率移動造成的價值減損：($24,120,000 − $20,550,000) ÷ $24,120,000 ≈ 14.8%，且背後的淨營運收益預測完全沒有改變。
⚠️
Pitfall:常見陷阱： It is tempting to assume the cap rate a buyer purchases at is the cap rate a buyer will sell at five years later. Module 06 covered why that assumption breaks whenever the rate cycle turns — and a hold period long enough to let NOI grow 3% a year is also long enough to sit through at least one full turn of that cycle. 很容易假設買方買進時的資本化率，就是五年後賣出時的資本化率。第 06 課解釋過，只要利率週期一轉向，這個假設就會失靈——而一個長到足以讓淨營運收益每年成長 3% 的持有期，同樣也長到足以撐過至少一整輪的週期轉向。
TASK 7 OF 8 任務七／共八個任務

Decide: Price, Restructure, or Walk

決定：續行、重新架構，還是退場

The math is now on the table: a marketed NOI running about 12.7 percent hot, a DSCR that clears on the pro-forma and fails on actuals, and an exit that loses about 14.8 percent of its value on a 100bp cap move alone. None of that automatically means the deal is dead — it means it needs a decision, not a reflex.

現在數字都攤在桌上了：行銷用的淨營運收益虛高約 12.7%，DSCR 用預估值算過關、用實際數字算不過關，出場價值光是資本化率移動 100 個基點就要蒸發約 14.8%。這些都不代表這筆交易一定該放棄——而是代表它需要一個經過判斷的決定，而不是一個反射動作。

A renegotiated price or a restructured deal is only a good decision if it is aimed at the right risk. Task 8 asks whether the DSCR problem and the rollover cliff are actually two separate risks to price for, or one risk wearing two names.

重新議價或重新架構交易，只有在瞄準正確的風險時才算是好決定。任務八要問的是：DSCR 問題和到期懸崖，究竟是兩個各自需要單獨定價的風險，還是同一個風險用了兩個不同的名字。

TASK 8 OF 8 任務八／共八個任務

Synthesis: The Biggest Risk in This Deal

綜合判斷：這筆交易最大的風險

Every prior task tested one thread in isolation. This last one asks what happens when the two sharpest threads — the financing math from Task 4 and the leasing math from Task 5 — are read together instead of separately. A real underwriting memo does not present risks as a numbered list; it has to say, in one sentence, what actually keeps a lender or an investment committee up at night.

前面每個任務都只單獨測試一條線索。最後這一題要問的是：當任務四的融資數字，和任務五的租賃數字，放在一起讀而不是分開讀時，會發生什麼事。一份真正的核保備忘錄不會把風險列成編號清單就交差；它必須用一句話說清楚，究竟是什麼真正會讓放款方或投資委員會夜不成眠。

Before moving on, it is worth being able to state each of the eight answers above out loud, in one sentence, without looking back at the worked examples. That is the actual test this capstone is administering — not whether the arithmetic can be followed once, but whether the conclusions stick.

在往下走之前，值得檢查自己能不能不回頭看逐步演算範例，就用一句話把上面八個答案都講出來。這才是這場畢業考真正在測試的東西——不是能不能跟著算一遍，而是這些結論有沒有真的留下來。
