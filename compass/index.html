    How a stage is set階段怎麼判斷

    Each market is judged on one series only: the real house price index, which is the price index after removing consumer price inflation. Two numbers are taken from it.每個市場只用一條序列判斷：實質房價指數，也就是扣掉消費者物價之後的房價指數。從裡面算兩個量。

    • Trend: the change versus four quarters earlier, which is the real house price growth over the past year.趨勢：本季對四季前的漲跌，也就是過去一年的實質房價年增率。
    • Position: how far this quarter sits below the highest point since the series began. In a quarter that sets a new high, the distance is zero.位置：本季離「這個市場有資料以來的最高點」多遠。創下新高的那一季，距離是 0。

    The two numbers place the market in one of six cells. The cells do not overlap and cover every case.兩個量對照下表，落進六格之一。六格互不重疊，涵蓋所有情況。

    Stage階段Distance below the high離高點Real growth over a year年增率
    Peak, rising高點續漲Within 5%5% 以內Up 2% or more年增 2% 以上
    Peak, stalling高檔停滯Within 5%5% 以內Up less than 2% (a small fall counts)年增不到 2%（含小跌）
    Pulling back回檔中5% to 15% below低於高點 5% 到 15%Down 2% or more年減 2% 以上
    Deep correction深度修正15% or more below低於高點 15% 以上Down 2% or more年減 2% 以上

    A value exactly on a threshold goes to the "within" or "or more" side. The first 20 quarters of each series are not classified, because the high point means little that early. "Quarters in the stage" counts consecutive quarters in the same stage. The thresholds were fixed before any result was seen and have not been changed. Credit, interest rates, supply and valuation play no part in the stage; they are background only.恰好等於門檻的數值，歸入「以內」或「以上」那一側。每條序列開始後的前 20 季不判斷階段，因為那麼早的最高點沒有意義。「持續幾季」是連續處在同一階段的季數。門檻在看到任何結果之前就定好，之後沒有改過。信貸、利率、供給、估值不參與判斷，只當背景資料。

    How the stages were tested怎麼檢定

    Two claims were written down before the data were downloaded. Only the 43 test markets count towards pass or fail: every other BIS country with at least 80 quarters of data, picked by rule and not by hand. The United States, Japan, Australia, Malaysia, Thailand and Greece were looked at while the rules were being designed, so they are reported but do not count.兩個假設在抓資料之前就先寫定。只有 43 個檢定市場的結果算通過與否：BIS 其餘國家裡至少有 80 季資料的，名單依規則產生，不是手挑。美國、日本、澳洲、馬來西亞、泰國、希臘在設計規則時看過，只報告結果，不算通過。

    Test one: do the three years after a rising stage beat those after a falling stage? It compares "Peak, rising" plus "Recovering" with "Pulling back" plus "Deep correction", on real price change over the next three years. It uses the whole history of each of the 43 markets. For each market the median of the two groups is taken, then the gap. Of the 38 markets with at least 8 quarters in both groups, 34 had the rising group ahead, and the median gap was 13.3 percentage points. Redrawing the markets 10,000 times puts 90% of the results between 9.8 and 17.8 points, a range that does not touch zero (a confidence interval). The pass rule, set in advance, was that two thirds of markets favour the rising group and the range excludes zero. Both held.檢定一：上漲階段之後三年，是不是比下跌階段好？比的是「高點續漲＋修復上漲」和「回檔中＋深度修正」之後三年的實質房價漲跌。用的是 43 個市場的全部歷史：每個市場各算兩組的中位數，再看差距。兩組各有至少 8 季的有 38 個市場，其中 34 個是上漲組較好，差距的中位數是 13.3 個百分點。把市場重抽 1 萬次，90% 的結果落在 9.8 到 17.8 個百分點之間，沒有碰到 0（這個範圍叫信賴區間）。事先訂的通過條件是三分之二以上的市場上漲組較好，而且這個範圍不含 0，兩條都達到。

    Using only quarters from 2010 onward, 20 of 24 markets favoured the rising group, with a gap of 9.3 points and a range of 2.8 to 14.9. The result holds. It also holds under all four alternative threshold settings (trend at 1% and 3%, position at 3% and 10%, position at 10% and 20%). In the 6 markets seen during design, 5 point the same way, with a gap of 10.8 points.只看起點在 2010 年以後的季度，24 個市場裡有 20 個上漲組較好，差距 9.3 個百分點，範圍是 2.8 到 14.9，仍然成立。四種門檻改法（趨勢改 1% 和 3%，位置改 3%／10% 和 10%／20%）結果也都成立。設計時看過的 6 個市場裡，5 個方向相同，差距 10.8 個百分點。

    Test two: after the rise stops near the high, are the next three years more likely to end lower? It compares "Peak, stalling" plus "Pulling back" with "Peak, rising", on the share of quarters in which real prices were lower three years later. It uses the whole history of the 32 markets with at least 8 quarters in each group and at least one fall. The pooled odds ratio, which is the odds of a fall (chance of a fall divided by chance of no fall) in the first group divided by the same odds in the second, is 2.56, and 27 of the 32 markets point the same way. The pass rule was at least 2, with at least 60% of markets in the same direction. Both held, so the full sample passes.檢定二：漲勢在高點附近停下之後，三年後房價下跌的機會是不是比較高？比的是「高檔停滯＋回檔中」和「高點續漲」，看三年後實質房價比當時低的季數占多少。用的是兩組各有至少 8 季、而且至少有 1 季下跌的 32 個市場的全部歷史。合併的勝算比是 2.56：前一組的下跌勝算（下跌的機率除以不下跌的機率），除以後一組同樣的勝算。32 個市場裡有 27 個方向相同。事先訂的通過條件是至少 2，而且 60% 以上的市場方向相同，兩條都達到，全樣本通過。

    The result is not stable. Using only quarters from 2010 onward, the odds ratio falls to 1.42 and only 8 of 16 markets point the same way, so it does not pass. With the position thresholds set at 3% and 10%, the odds ratio is 1.95 and it does not pass; the other three settings pass. In the 6 markets seen during design the odds ratio is 1.56, which does not pass. The second claim therefore rests on data from before 2010.這個結果不穩。只看起點在 2010 年以後的季度，勝算比降到 1.42，16 個市場裡只有 8 個方向相同，沒有通過。位置門檻換成 3%／10% 時，勝算比是 1.95，沒有通過；其他三種改法通過。設計時看過的 6 個市場，勝算比 1.56，沒有通過。所以檢定二只靠 2010 年以前的資料撐住。

    What is not promised不承諾什麼

    • No forecast for any single market. The numbers are the pooled past record of 43 markets. They are not an expectation for one market.不預測個別市場。這裡的數字是 43 個市場過去合併的紀錄，不是某一個市場的預期。
    • Quarters overlap. Each three-year window overlaps its neighbours, so adjacent quarters describe the same stretch of price moves and the number of quarters is not the number of independent observations. The ranges above are therefore drawn by resampling whole markets, not single quarters.季數互相重疊。三年的區間彼此重疊，相鄰的季度講的是同一段漲跌，所以季數不等於獨立的觀察次數。上面的範圍因此是以整個市場為單位重抽出來的，不是把每一季當成獨立樣本。
    • The data are one to two quarters late. The latest BIS figures usually run one to two quarters behind today. The stage shown is the stage of the latest quarter available, not of today.資料晚一到兩季。BIS 的最新一期通常比今天晚一到兩季。頁面顯示的是最新一期的階段，不是今天的。
    • No forward result yet. The stage of each market in its latest quarter has been saved and frozen. When the data for the first quarter of 2029 arrive, each will be compared with the actual three-year change. If rising stages have not done better than falling stages, the statement above that they do is withdrawn.前瞻驗證還沒有結果。各市場最新一期的階段已經存檔凍結，等 2029 年第一季的資料出來，再逐一對照實際的三年漲跌。若上漲階段沒有比下跌階段好，上面「上漲階段比下跌階段好」的說法就收回。
    • Two sources differ. Taiwan is not in the BIS database. It uses the Sinyi house price index divided by the consumer price index, calculated here, and is not part of the test. The BIS database no longer carries the UAE, so the version archived by FRED is used, which stops at the first quarter of 2024.兩個市場的來源不同。台灣不在 BIS 資料庫，用信義房價指數除以消費者物價指數自算，不進檢定。BIS 現行資料庫已經沒有阿聯，改用 FRED 保存的版本，停在 2024 年第一季。
