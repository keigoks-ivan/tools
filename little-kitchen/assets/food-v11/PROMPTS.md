# Food states — v11

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Each of the three grids is one food-state sprite-atlas asset; the beaten egg and broth are separate sprites. Transparent alpha is preserved. Existing whole-food photos and recipe-v9 scrambled-egg curds are reused. Final project assets are `cut.webp`, `pan.webp`, `pot.webp`, `beaten.webp` and `broth.webp`; `frames.json` stores the measured atlas regions. WebP encoding preserves the generated alpha without background removal.
