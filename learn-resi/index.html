Residential Property Investing: From First Home to Portfolio

住宅不動產投資課：從第一間房到資產組合

Real listings and real numbers first. Formulas second. Every module ends with a quiz and most start with a deal that actually happened.

先看真實物件與真實數字，公式其次。每個模組結尾都有測驗，多數模組則以一筆真實發生過的交易開場。

Housing is the asset class almost everyone touches and most people analyze the least. A first home is usually the largest single purchase most buyers ever make, financed with the most leverage they will ever use, decided in weeks under emotional pressure — and rarely underwritten with the same rigor an institutional buyer applies to a commercial building. That gap between how much is at stake and how little analysis goes into it is where this course starts.

住宅是幾乎每個人都會接觸、卻是多數人分析得最少的資產類別。第一間房通常是多數買家一生中金額最大的單筆購買，用上一生中最高的槓桿融資，卻常常在幾週內在情緒壓力下做出決定——很少有人用機構買家分析商用大樓那種嚴謹程度去核算它。這個「賭注很大、分析卻很少」的落差，正是本課程的起點。

This course runs deals-first, same as the numbers a real closing forces on you: what a listing's asking price doesn't tell you, why two "comparable" units three floors apart can sell 15% apart, what a mortgage amortization table actually does to your equity in year one versus year fifteen. Formulas — loan-to-value, debt service, cap rate, cash-on-cash — show up only after a real transaction has already made the judgment call visible.

本課程以真實交易為起點，就像一次真正的成交會逼你面對的那些數字：一筆物件的開價沒告訴你什麼、同一棟樓相差三層的兩戶「可比」單位為什麼能差到 15%、房貸攤還表在第一年與第十五年對你的權益到底做了什麼。貸款成數、償債能力、資本化率、現金報酬率這些公式，都要等一筆真實交易先把判斷點攤在眼前之後才登場。

Retrieval beats re-reading. Every module closes with a short quiz — not to grade, but because forcing recall is what moves a concept from "read once" to "usable under pressure," the same pressure a bidding war or a financing deadline applies. The war-story boxes throughout are drawn from real purchases that went wrong, because the lessons a housing market teaches for free are usually the ones someone else already paid a down payment to learn.

主動回想比重複閱讀有效。每個模組結尾都有一個小測驗——目的不是打分數，而是強迫回想，這才是把一個概念從「讀過一次」推進到「壓力下用得出來」的機制，而搶購熱潮或貸款期限施加的正是這種壓力。課程中穿插的「來自實戰」案例框取材自真實出過問題的購屋交易，因為房市免費教會你的教訓，通常是別人已經付出一筆頭期款代價換來的。

How This Course Teaches本課程的教學方法
Concrete before abstract具體先於抽象

Every concept opens with one real home's numbers, not a variable name.

每個概念都先從一間真實住宅的數字談起，而不是先給一個變數名稱。

Worked examples逐步演算範例

Full calculations shown step by step, not just the final answer.

完整計算過程逐步呈現，而非只給最終答案。

Retrieval practice主動回想測驗

Short quizzes force recall instead of passive re-reading.

簡短測驗強迫主動回想，而非被動重讀。

Case-based learning案例式學習

Real deals — wins and losses — carry the lesson, not abstract rules.

真實交易——成功與失敗——承載教訓，而非抽象規則。

Interleaving交錯練習

Topics mix and reappear across modules rather than being learned once and dropped.

主題交錯穿插並在不同模組反覆出現，而非學完一塊就丟開。

Learn-by-doing calculators動手試算器

Sliders let you move one variable and watch the deal's numbers change in real time.

拉桿讓你調整單一變數，即時看到交易數字如何變化。
