1. One Home, Six Numbers

一、一間住宅，六個數字

Assume (假設) a single home anchors this entire module: purchase price $600,000, achievable monthly rent $2,000, financed with 20% down ($120,000 cash) and a $480,000 mortgage at a fixed 6% rate over 30 years. Six numbers — price, rent, down payment, loan amount, rate, term — and every metric in this module is a different question asked of that same handful of inputs. Gross yield, net yield, monthly payment, cash-on-cash return, total return: none of them require new information once you have those six numbers. What changes is which of them you divide by which.

假設本課從頭到尾都繞著同一間住宅打轉：購買價格 $600,000，可達成的月租金 $2,000，以兩成頭期款（現金 $120,000）加上 $480,000 房貸融資，貸款利率固定 6%、期限 30 年。六個數字——價格、租金、頭期款、貸款金額、利率、年期——本課的每一個指標，都只是對這同一批輸入值提出的不同問題。總殖利率、淨殖利率、月付金、現金報酬率、總報酬：一旦有了這六個數字，都不需要額外資訊。真正變化的，只是用哪個數字去除哪個數字。

The number every downstream metric in this chapter depends on is gross rental yieldAnnual rent divided by purchase price, before any costs are subtracted — the headline number that is almost always quoted, and almost never the real return.年租金除以購買價格，尚未扣除任何成本——這是幾乎總是被報出來的頭條數字，但幾乎從來不是真正的報酬。. It is the easiest number to compute and the easiest one to mistake for the real return. Section 2 builds the full cost stack that separates the two.

本章後續所有指標都建立在同一個數字之上——總租金殖利率Annual rent divided by purchase price, before any costs are subtracted — the headline number that is almost always quoted, and almost never the real return.年租金除以購買價格，尚未扣除任何成本——這是幾乎總是被報出來的頭條數字，但幾乎從來不是真正的報酬。（總殖利率）。它是最容易算的數字，也是最容易被誤當成真正報酬的數字。第二節會把區分這兩者的完整成本堆疊建立起來。

2. Gross vs Net Rental Yield

二、總殖利率與淨殖利率

Gross yield is simple: annual rent divided by price. On the anchor home, $2,000 × 12 = $24,000 of annual rent divided by $600,000 gives a gross yield of 4.0%. That number, on its own, tells you almost nothing about what you'll actually keep. Net rental yieldGross yield minus the full stack of real operating costs — vacancy, management, maintenance reserve, property tax, insurance and HOA/strata fees — divided by price. The number that actually matters.總殖利率扣除完整的實際營運成本堆疊——空置、管理費、維護準備金、房產稅、保險與社區管理費——之後除以價格，是真正重要的數字。 is what's left after every real, recurring cost of owning and renting the property is subtracted. The gap between the two is not a rounding error — it routinely cuts the headline number by a third or more.

總殖利率算法很單純：年租金除以價格。以假設的這間住宅為例，年租金 $2,000 × 12 = $24,000 除以 $600,000，得出總殖利率 4.0%。這個數字本身，幾乎無法告訴你最後真正能留下多少。淨租金殖利率Gross yield minus the full stack of real operating costs — vacancy, management, maintenance reserve, property tax, insurance and HOA/strata fees — divided by price. The number that actually matters.總殖利率扣除完整的實際營運成本堆疊——空置、管理費、維護準備金、房產稅、保險與社區管理費——之後除以價格，是真正重要的數字。（淨殖利率）才是扣除持有與出租這間房產所有真實、經常性成本之後剩下的數字。兩者之間的落差不是誤差——它經常把頭條數字砍掉三分之一以上。

Worked Example — Building Net Yield From Gross (Hypothetical)逐步演算範例——從總殖利率堆疊出淨殖利率（假設數字）
  1. 1 Gross annual rent on the anchor home: $2,000/mo × 12 = $24,000/yr. Gross yield: $24,000 ÷ $600,000 = 4.00%. 假設住宅的年總租金：$2,000／月 × 12 = $24,000／年。總殖利率：$24,000 ÷ $600,000 = 4.00%。
  2. 2 Subtract a 5% vacancy allowance — months the unit sits empty between tenants, or rent that never gets collected: $24,000 × 5% = $1,200. Effective rent: $24,000 − $1,200 = $22,800. 扣除 5% 的空置準備——租客更替之間房子空著的月份，或收不到的租金：$24,000 × 5% = $1,200。有效租金：$24,000 − $1,200 = $22,800。
  3. 3 Subtract a property management fee at 8% of collected rent — even if you plan to self-manage, underwrite this cost, because your own time is not free and a future buyer will price it in: $22,800 × 8% = $1,824. Running total: $22,800 − $1,824 = $20,976. 扣除按已收租金 8% 計算的物業管理費——即使你打算自行管理，也要把這筆成本核算進去，因為你自己的時間不是免費的，未來的買方也會把這筆成本算進估值：$22,800 × 8% = $1,824。累計剩餘：$22,800 − $1,824 = $20,976。
  4. 4 Subtract a maintenance/capex reserve of $2,000/yr — a sinking fund for the roof, water heater and appliances that will eventually need replacing, even in a year nothing breaks. Running total: $20,976 − $2,000 = $18,976. 扣除 $2,000／年 的維護／資本支出準備金——為屋頂、熱水器與家電等終究會需要更換的項目預留的償債基金，即使某一年什麼都沒壞也要提列。累計剩餘：$20,976 − $2,000 = $18,976。
  5. 5 Subtract property tax of $2,400/yr (依司法轄區而異 — varies enormously by jurisdiction, see the benchmark table below). Running total: $18,976 − $2,400 = $16,576. 扣除房產稅 $2,400／年（依司法轄區而異——各地差異極大，詳見下方基準表）。累計剩餘：$18,976 − $2,400 = $16,576。
  6. 6 Subtract insurance of $1,000/yr (依司法轄區而異 — much higher in flood, wildfire or earthquake-exposed markets). Running total: $16,576 − $1,000 = $15,576. 扣除保險費 $1,000／年（依司法轄區而異——在洪水、野火或地震風險較高的市場會高出許多）。累計剩餘：$16,576 − $1,000 = $15,576。
  7. 7 Subtract HOA/strata fees of $576/yr (依司法轄區而異 — can be $0 for a detached house with no association, or several hundred dollars a month for a full-amenity condo). Net operating income: $15,576 − $576 = $15,000/yr. 扣除社區管理費（HOA／管委會費）$576／年（依司法轄區而異——沒有管委會的獨棟住宅可能是 $0，全套設施的公寓大樓則可能一個月就要數百美元）。淨營運收益：$15,576 − $576 = $15,000／年。
  8. 8 Net yield: $15,000 ÷ $600,000 = 2.5%. The 4.0% headline number collapsed to 2.5% — a loss of well over a third — before a single dollar of mortgage interest was even considered. This net figure, not gross yield, is what section 3 onward builds on. 淨殖利率：$15,000 ÷ $600,000 = 2.5%。4.0% 的頭條數字，在還沒算進任何一塊錢房貸利息之前，就已經萎縮到 2.5%——蒸發了超過三分之一。從第三節開始，本課建立在這個淨數字之上，而不是總殖利率。

3. Mortgage Math From Scratch

三、從頭建立房貸的數學

AmortizationThe process of paying down a loan through equal periodic payments, where each payment covers that period's interest and the remainder reduces the principal balance.透過等額分期還款逐步償還貸款的過程，每一期的還款先支付當期利息，剩餘部分才用來減少本金餘額。 is the mechanism behind a fixed-rate mortgage's fixed monthly payment — and the single most common source of confusion for a first-time investor. A fixed payment does not mean a fixed split between interest and principal. Early in the loan, most of each payment is interest; late in the loan, most of each payment is principal. Same dollar amount every month, completely different composition.

攤還（Amortization）The process of paying down a loan through equal periodic payments, where each payment covers that period's interest and the remainder reduces the principal balance.透過等額分期還款逐步償還貸款的過程，每一期的還款先支付當期利息，剩餘部分才用來減少本金餘額。是固定利率房貸月付金維持不變背後的機制——也是首購投資人最常搞混的一點。月付金固定，不代表利息與本金的比例固定。貸款早期，每一筆月付金大部分是利息；貸款後期，每一筆月付金大部分是本金。每個月的金額一模一樣，組成卻完全不同。

Why? Interest for any given month is charged only on the loan's remaining balance: interest = balance × monthly rate. In month 1, the balance is the full loan amount, so the interest charge is at its largest — and whatever's left of the fixed payment after covering that interest is all that goes to principal, which is at its smallest. As the balance shrinks month after month, the interest charge shrinks with it, which leaves more and more of the fixed payment free to go toward principal. The payment doesn't change; the split inside it does, automatically, as a mechanical consequence of the balance getting smaller.

為什麼？因為任何一個月的利息，都只針對貸款剩餘的本金餘額計算：利息 = 餘額 × 月利率。第一個月，餘額是全額貸款金額，所以利息負擔最大——固定月付金扣除這筆利息之後剩下的部分，就是還進本金的錢，此時是最少的。隨著餘額一個月一個月縮小，利息負擔也跟著縮小，這就讓固定月付金裡有越來越多的部分能還進本金。月付金本身沒有變，變的是它內部的比例——這是餘額變小之後，機械式、自動發生的結果。

Worked Example — The Anchor Mortgage, Amortized (Hypothetical)逐步演算範例——假設住宅的房貸攤還（假設數字）
  1. 1 Loan $480,000 at 6% fixed, 30 years (360 monthly payments). Monthly rate: 6% ÷ 12 = 0.5%. 貸款 $480,000，固定利率 6%，年期 30 年（共 360 期月付款）。月利率：6% ÷ 12 = 0.5%。
  2. 2 Using the standard amortizing-payment formula, the fixed monthly payment is $2,877.84 — the same figure every single month for 30 years. 用標準的分期還款公式，固定月付金為 $2,877.84——這個數字在整整 30 年內每個月都一樣。
  3. 3 Month 1's interest charge: $480,000 × 0.5% = $2,400. Principal paid in month 1: $2,877.84 − $2,400 = $477.84 — only about 17% of the payment. 第 1 個月的利息：$480,000 × 0.5% = $2,400。第 1 個月還進本金的部分：$2,877.84 − $2,400 = $477.84——只占月付金約 17%。
  4. 4 By year 30's final payments, the balance has shrunk to almost nothing, so almost the entire payment is principal — the interest/principal ratio has effectively flipped over the life of the loan, without the payment amount ever moving. 到了第 30 年的最後幾期，餘額已經幾乎歸零，所以幾乎整筆月付金都是本金——在整個貸款期間，利息與本金的比例實質上完全反轉，而月付金金額卻從未變動過。

「當年利息／本金（單月）」指的是該年最後一期月付款內部的拆分，不是整年的加總——它以縮影方式呈現了交叉點：到第 15 年，月付金仍以利息為主；到第 25 年，已大致反轉為以本金為主。

Now the reveal that surprises most first-time buyers: sum every interest payment over all 360 months, and the total comes to $556,023 — more than the original $480,000 loan amount itself, or about 116% of principal. Borrow $480,000 over 30 years at 6%, and you pay back roughly $1,036,000 in total: the original loan plus more than its own value again in interest. This is not a trick or an edge case — it is the ordinary arithmetic of a long-amortization loan at a plausible rate, and it is exactly why the interest rate on a mortgage matters as much as, or more than, the price of the home itself.

現在來看多數首購族最驚訝的一個真相：把 360 期的利息全部加總起來，總額是 $556,023——比原始 $480,000 的貸款金額本身還多，約占本金的 116%。以 6% 的利率借 $480,000、分 30 年攤還，總共要還大約 $1,036,000：原始貸款金額，再加上超過它本身價值一次的利息。這不是話術，也不是特殊個案——這是長年期攤還貸款在一個合理利率下的正常算術，也正是為什麼房貸利率跟房屋本身的價格一樣重要，甚至更重要。

🎙 From the Trenches 來自實戰

Much of the wave of US mortgage defaults that fed the 2008 financial crisis traces directly to a payment mechanic, not just a lending-standards failure. Millions of adjustable-rate and "teaser rate" mortgages issued from roughly 2004 to 2006 carried a low introductory interest rate — often fixed for just two or three years — that then reset to a much higher market-linked rate. Underwriting was frequently done against the low teaser payment, not the payment the loan would actually carry after reset. When large cohorts of these loans reset in 2007–2008, monthly payments jumped sharply for borrowers whose income had not grown to match, a phenomenon widely referred to at the time as "payment shock." Rising defaults on these resetting loans were a central thread in the broader crisis that followed. The lesson generalizes directly: underwriting a purchase off the rate you have today, when that rate is contractually scheduled to reset, is underwriting off the wrong number.

2008 年金融危機中，美國房貸違約潮很大一部分可以直接追溯到一項還款機制，而不只是放款標準失守。大約 2004 到 2006 年間發行的數百萬筆可調整利率與「優惠利率（teaser rate）」房貸，帶有一段偏低的入門利率——通常只固定兩到三年——之後就會重設為與市場連動、明顯更高的利率。核保時經常是以偏低的優惠期月付金去計算，而不是貸款重設之後實際要負擔的月付金。當大批這類貸款在 2007 至 2008 年集中重設時，收入沒有同步成長的借款人，月付金金額大幅跳升，當時普遍稱之為「還款衝擊（payment shock）」。這批重設貸款違約率上升，是隨後整場危機的核心線索之一。這個教訓可以直接推廣：如果核算購屋預算時用的是今天的利率，而這個利率依合約排定之後會重設，那就是用了錯誤的數字在核算。

⚠️
Pitfall:常見陷阱： Underwriting cash-on-cash return off a teaser or introductory mortgage rate instead of the rate it resets to. If a loan is fixed for only two or three years and then floats or resets, every return calculation in this module should be run twice — once at today's rate, once at the reset rate — before deciding the deal actually works. 用優惠利率或入門利率來核算現金報酬率，而不是用它重設之後的利率。如果一筆貸款只固定兩、三年、之後就會浮動或重設，本課的每一項報酬計算都應該算兩次——一次用今天的利率，一次用重設後的利率——才能判斷這筆交易是否真的划算。

4. Cash-on-Cash With Leverage, Both Directions

四、槓桿下的現金報酬率，兩個方向

Cash-on-cash returnAnnual net operating income minus the full annual mortgage payment (principal and interest), divided by the actual cash invested (down payment plus closing costs) — the return on the cash you actually put in, not on the property's full price.年淨營運收益扣除全年房貸月付金總額（本金加利息）之後，除以實際投入的現金（頭期款加交易成本）——這是你實際投入的現金所獲得的報酬，而不是整個物業總價的報酬。 asks a different question than net yield does. Net yield measures return on the full purchase price. Cash-on-cash measures return on only the cash actually invested — and because a mortgage lets a small amount of cash control a much larger asset, leverage can push cash-on-cash well above the unlevered net yield, or well below it, depending entirely on one comparison: is the property's net yield above or below the mortgage rate?

現金報酬率（Cash-on-Cash Return）Annual net operating income minus the full annual mortgage payment (principal and interest), divided by the actual cash invested (down payment plus closing costs) — the return on the cash you actually put in, not on the property's full price.年淨營運收益扣除全年房貸月付金總額（本金加利息）之後，除以實際投入的現金（頭期款加交易成本）——這是你實際投入的現金所獲得的報酬，而不是整個物業總價的報酬。問的是跟淨殖利率不同的問題。淨殖利率衡量的是整個購買價格的報酬。現金報酬率衡量的只是實際投入現金的報酬——而因為房貸讓一小筆現金能撬動一個大得多的資產，槓桿可以把現金報酬率推到遠高於無槓桿淨殖利率，也可能推到遠低於它，完全取決於一個比較：物業的淨殖利率高於還是低於房貸利率？

To see both directions cleanly, use a different, explicitly hypothetical (假設) property — one from a lower price-to-rent market where rental yields run higher relative to price. Assume a $180,000 property renting for $1,500/mo ($18,000/yr gross, a 10.0% gross yield), which after the same kind of cost stack built in Section 2 nets down to $11,700/yr of net operating income — a 6.5% net yield. Finance it with 25% down ($45,000 cash) and a $135,000 loan, 30-year fixed.

為了清楚看出兩個方向，這裡改用另一個明確標示為假設的物業——來自房價租金比較低、租金殖利率相對於價格較高的市場。假設一間 $180,000 的物業，月租金 $1,500（年租金 $18,000，總殖利率 10.0%），依照第二節同樣的成本堆疊邏輯，淨營運收益為 $11,700／年——淨殖利率 6.5%。融資方式為兩成五頭期款（現金 $45,000）加上 $135,000 貸款，30 年固定利率。

Worked Example — Positive Leverage (Rate Below Yield)逐步演算範例——正向槓桿（利率低於殖利率）
  1. 1 Mortgage rate: 5.0%, below the property's 6.5% net yield. Monthly payment on the $135,000 loan: $724.71. Annual debt service: $724.71 × 12 = $8,697. 房貸利率：5.0%，低於這間物業 6.5% 的淨殖利率。$135,000 貸款的月付金：$724.71。年度還款總額：$724.71 × 12 = $8,697。
  2. 2 Annual cash flow after debt service: $11,700 − $8,697 = $3,003. 扣除還款後的年度現金流：$11,700 − $8,697 = $3,003。
  3. 3 Cash-on-cash return: $3,003 ÷ $45,000 = 6.67% — higher than the 6.5% unlevered net yield. Because the mortgage rate (5.0%) is below the property's net yield (6.5%), every dollar borrowed earns more than it costs, and leverage pulls the return on cash up above the return on the whole property. 現金報酬率：$3,003 ÷ $45,000 = 6.67%——高於 6.5% 的無槓桿淨殖利率。因為房貸利率（5.0%）低於物業淨殖利率（6.5%），每借一塊錢賺到的都比它的成本多，槓桿把現金報酬率拉到高於整個物業報酬率之上。
Worked Example — Negative Leverage (Rate Above Yield)逐步演算範例——負向槓桿（利率高於殖利率）
  1. 1 Same property, same $135,000 loan — but now assume the rate available is 8.5%, above the property's 6.5% net yield. Monthly payment: $1,038.03. Annual debt service: $1,038.03 × 12 = $12,456. 同一間物業、同樣 $135,000 貸款——但這次假設能取得的利率是 8.5%，高於物業 6.5% 的淨殖利率。月付金：$1,038.03。年度還款總額：$1,038.03 × 12 = $12,456。
  2. 2 Annual cash flow after debt service: $11,700 − $12,456 = −$756. The property now costs money out of pocket every year, despite renting at a perfectly respectable 10.0% gross / 6.5% net yield. 扣除還款後的年度現金流：$11,700 − $12,456 = −$756。儘管租金殖利率相當不錯（總殖利率 10.0%／淨殖利率 6.5%），這間物業現在每年卻要倒貼現金。
  3. 3 Cash-on-cash return: −$756 ÷ $45,000 = −1.68%. Same property, same rent, same leverage ratio — only the rate changed, and it was enough to flip a 6.5% net yield into a negative cash-on-cash return. Leverage did not create this loss; it amplified an unfavorable spread between yield and rate into a larger swing than the unlevered numbers alone would show. 現金報酬率：−$756 ÷ $45,000 = −1.68%。同一間物業、同樣的租金、同樣的槓桿比例——只有利率變了，就足以把 6.5% 的淨殖利率翻轉成負的現金報酬率。虧損不是槓桿創造出來的；槓桿只是把殖利率與利率之間不利的利差，放大成比無槓桿數字本身更大的波動。

The rule generalizes cleanly: leverage amplifies whatever spread already exists between yield and borrowing cost. A favorable spread (yield above rate) becomes a more favorable cash-on-cash number than the unlevered yield alone. An unfavorable spread (rate above yield) becomes a worse cash-on-cash number — and can turn a perfectly reasonable rental yield into an annual cash drain. This is the same "leverage cuts both ways" lesson from Module 01, now with the actual cash-flow arithmetic behind it instead of just appreciation.

這個規則可以清楚地推廣：槓桿會放大殖利率與借款成本之間原本就存在的利差。有利的利差（殖利率高於利率）會讓現金報酬率比無槓桿殖利率本身更好看。不利的利差（利率高於殖利率）會讓現金報酬率變得更差——甚至可能把一個相當合理的租金殖利率，變成每年持續失血的現金黑洞。這正是第一課「槓桿是雙面刃」的同一個教訓，只是這次背後有實際的現金流算式支撐，而不只是增值。

5. Price-to-Rent and Price-to-Income Ratios

五、房價租金比與房價所得比

The price-to-rent ratioPurchase price divided by annual rent — the inverse of gross yield, expressed as a multiple of years' rent instead of a percentage.購買價格除以年租金——是總殖利率的倒數，以「幾年租金」的倍數表示，而不是百分比。 is simply gross yield expressed the other way around: price divided by annual rent instead of rent divided by price. On the anchor home, $600,000 ÷ $24,000 = 25 — the property is worth 25 years of its own rent. A low price-to-rent ratio signals a market priced for cash flow; a high one signals a market priced for something other than the rent it currently produces — usually expected appreciation, scarcity, or capital seeking a safe store of value rather than income.

房價租金比Purchase price divided by annual rent — the inverse of gross yield, expressed as a multiple of years' rent instead of a percentage.購買價格除以年租金——是總殖利率的倒數，以「幾年租金」的倍數表示，而不是百分比。其實就是把總殖利率反過來表示：用價格除以年租金，而不是租金除以價格。以假設的這間住宅為例，$600,000 ÷ $24,000 = 25——這間物業的價值等於它自身 25 年的租金。房價租金比低，代表這個市場的定價是為了現金流；房價租金比高，則代表這個市場的定價反映的是租金以外的其他東西——通常是預期增值、稀缺性，或是尋求保值而非收益的資金。

The price-to-income ratioMedian home price divided by median household income — an affordability and debt-serviceability indicator, distinct from price-to-rent, which is a yield-market indicator.房價中位數除以家戶所得中位數——這是可負擔性與償債能力的指標，跟屬於殖利率市場指標的房價租金比是不同的東西。 asks a completely different question. It divides median home price by median household income, and it signals affordability and debt-serviceability for local buyers — not yield for investors. A market can have a perfectly healthy price-to-rent ratio for an investor while having a punishing price-to-income ratio for a local family trying to buy, or the reverse. The two ratios are frequently confused because both put "price" in the numerator, but one is about what a renter's cash flow supports and the other is about what a local income can service.

房價所得比Median home price divided by median household income — an affordability and debt-serviceability indicator, distinct from price-to-rent, which is a yield-market indicator.房價中位數除以家戶所得中位數——這是可負擔性與償債能力的指標，跟屬於殖利率市場指標的房價租金比是不同的東西。問的是完全不同的問題。它用房價中位數除以家戶所得中位數，反映的是當地買家的可負擔性與償債能力——而不是投資人的殖利率。一個市場對投資人來說房價租金比可能相當健康，同時對想買房的當地家庭來說房價所得比卻高得驚人，反之亦然。這兩個比率經常被搞混，因為兩者分子都是「價格」，但一個講的是租客的現金流能支撐什麼，另一個講的是當地所得能償還什麼。

Concrete, well-known reference points help calibrate both without needing a single precise current statistic. Markets like Hong Kong and, at various points, cities such as Taipei or Vancouver have for years been cited in international affordability surveys and commentary as having among the highest price-to-income multiples in the world — housing priced at a level that requires extraordinary income multiples, extended family support, or very long holding horizons for a typical local household to buy. At the other end, parts of the US Midwest and former industrial "Rust Belt" cities have long carried a reputation for the opposite: home prices low enough, relative to achievable rents, that gross yields comfortably support positive cash-flow rental economics even before accounting for that market's typically slower appreciation. Neither reputation is a precise number you can underwrite today — for the actual, current multiple in a specific market, use the live dashboardThis site's cross-market residential dashboard, which tracks price-to-rent and price-to-income multiples across dozens of cities.本站的跨市場住宅儀表板，追蹤數十個城市的房價租金比與房價所得比。 covering 84 cities rather than a remembered reputation.

一些具體、廣為人知的參考點，能在不需要單一精確當前數字的情況下，幫助校準這兩個比率。像香港，以及在不同時期的台北、溫哥華等城市，多年來一直被國際可負擔性調查與評論引用為全球房價所得比數一數二高的市場——房價高到需要驚人的所得倍數、大家庭支援，或非常長的持有期限，一般當地家庭才買得起。在光譜另一端，美國中西部部分地區與昔日的工業「鐵鏽帶」城市，長期以來則有相反的名聲：相對於可達成的租金，房價低到即使不計入這類市場通常較慢的增值速度，總殖利率也能相當寬裕地支撐正現金流的租賃經濟。這兩種名聲都不是可以直接拿來核算的精確數字——若要查某個特定市場實際、當下的倍數，請使用涵蓋 84 個城市的即時儀表板This site's cross-market residential dashboard, which tracks price-to-rent and price-to-income multiples across dozens of cities.本站的跨市場住宅儀表板，追蹤數十個城市的房價租金比與房價所得比。，而不是憑印象中的名聲。

Neither ratio, alone, is a buy or sell signal. A low price-to-rent ratio can mean genuine value — or it can mean a market with structurally weak long-term price growth, which is exactly why rent yields have to be higher to attract capital in the first place. A high price-to-income ratio can mean a bubble — or it can mean a market with durable land scarcity and deep, persistent demand that has traded at elevated multiples for decades without a correction. Both ratios are inputs to judgment, not verdicts on their own; see the live dashboard for where a specific market sits today before drawing any conclusion.

單獨看任何一個比率，都不能當作買進或賣出的訊號。房價租金比低，可能代表真正的價值——也可能代表這個市場的長期房價成長結構性偏弱，而這正是為什麼租金殖利率一開始就必須更高才能吸引資金進場。房價所得比高，可能代表泡沫——也可能代表這個市場有持久的土地稀缺性與深厚、持續的需求，數十年來都以偏高的倍數交易卻從未修正。這兩個比率都只是判斷的輸入項，不是本身就能下結論的裁決；在得出任何結論之前，請先查即時儀表板了解某個特定市場目前所在的位置。

🎙 From the Trenches 來自實戰

Hong Kong has been ranked at or near the top of international housing affordability surveys — including the long-running Demographia International Housing Affordability report — for well over a decade, typically cited with a "median multiple" (median home price divided by median household income) in the high teens or beyond, several times the multiple most affordability frameworks consider "severely unaffordable." The structural drivers behind it are well documented and durable: an unusually constrained land supply relative to population density, and government land-sale and planning policy that has historically limited the pace at which new supply reaches the market. The result is a market where an ordinary local household's income, even a well-above-median one, has for years struggled to service the price-to-income multiple a home in the territory commands — a live illustration of a price-to-income ratio decoupled from what typical local income can actually support.

香港在國際住房可負擔性調查中——包括長期發布的 Demographia 國際住房可負擔性報告——十多年來一直排名在最頂端或接近最頂端，通常被引用的「所得倍數中位數」（房價中位數除以家戶所得中位數）落在接近二十倍甚至更高，是多數可負擔性框架認定「嚴重不可負擔」門檻的好幾倍。背後的結構性驅動因素有充分紀錄，且持久存在：相對於人口密度而言異常受限的土地供給，加上政府賣地與規劃政策長期以來限制了新供給進入市場的速度。結果是，即使是收入明顯高於中位數的一般本地家庭，多年來也難以負擔香港房價所隱含的所得倍數——這正是房價所得比與當地一般所得實際能負擔的水準脫鉤的活生生案例。

6. Total Return Decomposition

六、總報酬拆解

Cash-on-cash return only captures one slice of what a levered rental property actually returns — the cash that lands in your bank account each year. It misses two other real components of return: total return decompositionSplitting a property's full equity return into its component parts — operating income, mortgage interest cost, principal paydown (equity built via amortization) and price appreciation — rather than looking at cash flow alone.把一間物業完整的權益報酬拆解成各個組成部分——營運收入、房貸利息成本、本金攤還（透過分期還款累積的權益）與房價增值——而不是只看現金流。 pulls all of them together: the operating income earned, the interest cost paid, the equity quietly built through principal paydown even in a cash-flow-negative year, and any price appreciation. A property can show a negative cash-on-cash number and still be building real equity value — or the reverse.

現金報酬率只捕捉到一間有槓桿的出租物業真正報酬中的一小部分——也就是每年真正進到你銀行帳戶裡的現金。它遺漏了另外兩個真實的報酬組成部分：總報酬拆解Splitting a property's full equity return into its component parts — operating income, mortgage interest cost, principal paydown (equity built via amortization) and price appreciation — rather than looking at cash flow alone.把一間物業完整的權益報酬拆解成各個組成部分——營運收入、房貸利息成本、本金攤還（透過分期還款累積的權益）與房價增值——而不是只看現金流。把所有這些因素放在一起檢視：賺到的營運收入、付出的利息成本、即使在現金流為負的一年仍在悄悄累積的本金攤還權益，以及任何房價增值。一間物業可能現金報酬率是負的，卻仍在累積真正的權益價值——反之亦然。

Worked Example — Five-Year Hold on the Anchor Home (Hypothetical)逐步演算範例——假設住宅持有五年（假設數字）
  1. 1 Assume flat rent and flat costs for simplicity: net operating income stays at $15,000/yr for all 5 years. Cumulative NOI: $15,000 × 5 = $75,000. 為簡化計算，假設租金與成本維持不變：淨營運收益在整整 5 年都維持在 $15,000／年。累計淨營運收益：$15,000 × 5 = $75,000。
  2. 2 From the amortization schedule in Section 3, cumulative mortgage interest paid over the first 5 years on the $480,000/6% loan: $139,331. Cumulative principal paid down over the same 5 years: $33,339 — this is equity built, not a loss, since it converts cash into home ownership rather than spending it. 依照第三節的攤還時間表，$480,000、利率 6% 的貸款在頭 5 年累計支付的房貸利息：$139,331。同樣 5 年累計還進本金的部分：$33,339——這是累積出的權益，不是損失，因為它把現金轉換成了房屋所有權，而不是花掉。
  3. 3 Cash actually out of pocket over 5 years (NOI minus the full debt service of interest plus principal): $75,000 − $139,331 − $33,339 = −$97,671. On paper, this looks like a losing rental — but principal paydown isn't gone, it's now sitting in the home as equity. 5 年內實際自掏腰包的現金（淨營運收益扣除利息加本金的完整還款）：$75,000 − $139,331 − $33,339 = −$97,671。表面上看起來像是一筆虧錢的租賃投資——但攤還掉的本金並沒有消失，它現在以權益的形式留在房子裡。
  4. 4 Assume (假設) modest 3%/yr price appreciation: $600,000 × (1.03)^5 ≈ $695,564. Appreciation over 5 years: $695,564 − $600,000 = $95,564. 假設每年 3% 的溫和房價增值：$600,000 × (1.03)^5 ≈ $695,564。5 年累計增值：$695,564 − $600,000 = $95,564。
  5. 5 Add the pieces back together correctly: total 5-year equity dollar gain = cumulative NOI − cumulative interest + appreciation (the principal-paydown term cancels out algebraically, because it was subtracted as cash and added back as equity in the same amount): $75,000 − $139,331 + $95,564 = $31,233. 把各個部分正確地重新加總：5 年累計權益金額增益＝累計淨營運收益－累計利息＋增值（本金攤還這一項在代數上會互相抵銷，因為它先以現金形式被扣除、再以完全相同的金額以權益形式加回）：$75,000 − $139,331 + $95,564 = $31,233。
  6. 6 Ending equity: $120,000 (cash invested) + $31,233 = $151,233. Equity multiple: $151,233 ÷ $120,000 = 1.26x. Annualized equity return over the 5 years: 1.26^(1/5) − 1 ≈ 4.7%/yr — a positive result built almost entirely from appreciation and interest cost offsetting negative cash flow, not from cash flow itself. Change the appreciation assumption to zero, and this same property's total return collapses toward a loss. 期末權益：$120,000（投入現金）+ $31,233 = $151,233。權益倍數：$151,233 ÷ $120,000 = 1.26x。5 年年化權益報酬率：1.26^(1/5) − 1 ≈ 4.7%／年——這個正報酬幾乎完全來自增值與利息成本抵銷負現金流之後的結果，而不是來自現金流本身。如果把增值假設改成零，同一間物業的總報酬就會崩向虧損。

This is the real lesson of decomposition: a property that looks like a cash-flow loser on a year-by-year basis can still be a reasonable total-return investment if enough of the return is arriving as appreciation and equity buildup instead of cash flow — but that also means the investment's success now depends heavily on an appreciation assumption that, unlike rent, is not contractually guaranteed. Total return decomposition doesn't make a weak cash-flow deal safe; it makes explicit exactly how much of the deal's success rests on an assumption rather than a fact.

這正是報酬拆解真正的教訓：一間逐年來看像是現金流虧損者的物業，如果報酬有足夠比例是以增值與權益累積、而不是現金流的形式到來，仍然可能是一筆合理的總報酬投資——但這也代表這筆投資能否成功，如今高度仰賴一項增值假設，而這項假設不像租金那樣有合約保障。總報酬拆解並不會讓一筆現金流疲弱的交易變得安全；它只是把這筆交易的成功有多少比例建立在假設、而非事實之上，講得清清楚楚。

7. The Transaction Cost Round-Trip

七、交易成本來回一趟

Buying and later selling a home both carry real costs on top of the price itself. Buy-side costs typically include legal/conveyancing fees, a buyer's agent commission where applicable, mortgage-origination fees, and in many jurisdictions a transfer tax or stamp duty (依司法轄區而異 — rates and even which side of the transaction pays vary enormously by market). Sell-side costs typically include a listing agent's commission, closing/legal fees again, and in some jurisdictions capital gains tax on any price appreciation (依司法轄區而異). Combined, round-trip transaction costs commonly fall somewhere in a mid-single-digit to low-double-digit percentage of price, depending heavily on jurisdiction — and that combined percentage is not a small tax on the deal, it is often the difference between a short hold that works and one that quietly loses money even while the property itself appreciated.

買進與日後賣出一間住宅，除了價格本身之外，都帶有真實成本。買方一側的成本通常包括法律／過戶手續費、適用時的買方仲介佣金、貸款設定手續費，以及許多司法轄區課徵的過戶稅或印花稅（依司法轄區而異——稅率、甚至交易哪一方負擔，各市場差異極大）。賣方一側的成本通常包括銷售仲介佣金、再一次的過戶／法律手續費，以及某些司法轄區對房價增值課徵的資本利得稅（依司法轄區而異）。合計來看，來回一趟的交易成本，常落在價格的中個位數到低兩位數百分比之間，高度取決於司法轄區——而這個合計百分比並不是交易上的小額稅金，它經常正是一筆短期持有能不能真正划算、還是即使物業本身有增值卻仍悄悄虧錢的關鍵差異。

Worked Example — Breakeven Appreciation for a Round Trip (Hypothetical)逐步演算範例——來回一趟所需的損益兩平增值（假設數字）
  1. 1 Assume (假設) buy-side costs of 2% of price on the anchor home: $600,000 × 2% = $12,000. All-in cost basis: $600,000 + $12,000 = $612,000. 假設買方一側成本為價格的 2%：$600,000 × 2% = $12,000。全部投入的成本基礎：$600,000 + $12,000 = $612,000。
  2. 2 Assume (假設) sell-side costs of 6% of the eventual sale price (agent commission plus closing costs). To break even, the sale price must cover both the $612,000 basis and the 6% taken off the top of the sale itself. 假設賣方一側成本為最終成交價的 6%（仲介佣金加過戶成本）。要達到損益兩平，成交價必須同時覆蓋 $612,000 的成本基礎，以及從成交金額本身扣除的 6%。
  3. 3 Solving SalePrice × (1 − 6%) = $612,000 gives SalePrice = $612,000 ÷ 0.94 = $651,064. 求解 成交價 × (1 − 6%) = $612,000，得出 成交價 = $612,000 ÷ 0.94 = $651,064。
  4. 4 Appreciation needed just to break even: ($651,064 − $600,000) ÷ $600,000 ≈ 8.5%. Note this is higher than the simple 2% + 6% = 8% sum of the two cost percentages, because the sell-side cost is charged against the higher sale price, not the original purchase price — costs compound slightly against the seller. Anything less than roughly 8.5% price appreciation over the hold, and this round trip loses money even before financing costs or holding-period cash flow are considered. 單純為了損益兩平所需的增值：($651,064 − $600,000) ÷ $600,000 ≈ 8.5%。注意這個數字高於單純把兩項成本百分比相加的 2% + 6% = 8%，因為賣方一側的成本是按較高的成交價計算，而不是按原始購買價格——成本會對賣方形成些微複利效果。如果整個持有期間的房價增值低於大約 8.5%，那麼即使還沒把融資成本或持有期間的現金流算進去，這趟來回交易本身就已經在虧錢。

This is exactly why a short hold is a fundamentally different bet from a long hold, even on the identical property. A buyer planning to hold for one or two years needs price appreciation (or a below-market purchase) large enough to clear the round-trip cost hurdle in that short window. A buyer planning to hold for ten or fifteen years amortizes that same fixed round-trip cost over far more years of rental income and far more time for appreciation to accumulate — the transaction cost hurdle barely moves the long-hold math, while it can dominate the short-hold math entirely.

這正是為什麼即使是同一間物業，短期持有跟長期持有本質上是完全不同的賭注。計劃只持有一、兩年的買方，需要在這麼短的窗口內，達成足以跨過來回交易成本門檻的房價增值（或以低於市價買進）。計劃持有十年、十五年的買方，則能把同樣固定的來回交易成本，攤在多得多年份的租金收入與多得多的增值累積時間裡——交易成本門檻幾乎不會撼動長期持有的算式，卻可能完全主導短期持有的算式。

🎙 From the Trenches 來自實戰

Hong Kong and Singapore have both, at various points over the past two decades, imposed steep additional stamp duties specifically targeting short-hold residential resales and non-resident buyers, as a deliberate policy tool to cool speculative flipping. Hong Kong's Special Stamp Duty, introduced in 2010 and tightened in 2012, layered an extra charge of up to 20% of the transaction price on residential properties resold within a short holding window (originally two years, later extended to three), on top of the ordinary stamp duty already due — a combined round-trip friction large enough to make short-hold flipping arithmetic simply not work for most buyers. Singapore has run a broadly comparable Seller's Stamp Duty regime targeting resales within a set holding period. Both are well-documented, deliberate examples of a government using transaction-cost friction as a lever to discourage exactly the kind of short-hold speculation this section's breakeven math describes.

過去二十年間，香港與新加坡都曾在不同時期，針對短期持有的住宅轉售與非本地買家，課徵高額的額外印花稅，做為刻意用來為投機轉手降溫的政策工具。香港的額外印花稅（Special Stamp Duty）於 2010 年推出、2012 年加碼，對在短期持有窗口內（最初為兩年，後延長為三年）轉售的住宅物業，在原本已需繳納的一般印花稅之上，再疊加最高達成交價 20% 的額外稅負——合計的來回交易摩擦大到讓多數買方的短期轉手算式根本行不通。新加坡也運行著大致可比的賣方印花稅（Seller's Stamp Duty）制度，針對在特定持有期限內轉售的物業課稅。這兩者都是有充分紀錄、政府刻意運用交易成本摩擦做為槓桿、來遏止本節損益兩平算式所描述的那種短期持有投機行為的例子。

🎙 From the Trenches 來自實戰

The June 2021 collapse of the Champlain Towers South condominium in Surfside, Florida, in which nearly 100 residents died, triggered a wave of structural re-inspections across older Florida condo buildings and, in its wake, new state legislation requiring regular structural integrity reserve studies. The direct financial consequence for many existing condo owners, far beyond that single building, was a surge in large special assessments — one-time bills layered on top of ordinary HOA fees — as associations across the state moved to fund structural repairs and legally mandated reserve funds that many buildings had gone years without adequately building up. Owners who had underwritten a property's net yield off the advertised monthly HOA fee alone, with no allowance for an underfunded reserve, found that number could be dwarfed by a single special-assessment bill running into the tens of thousands of dollars. It is a documented, large-scale illustration of exactly the HOA/reserve-fund risk flagged in this module's cost-stack section.

2021 年 6 月，佛羅里達州瑟夫賽德（Surfside）的尚普蘭大廈南棟（Champlain Towers South）公寓倒塌，造成近百名住戶死亡，事件之後引發佛州各地老舊公寓大樓一波結構性複檢，並促成新的州立法，要求定期進行結構完整性準備金研究。對遠遠不只這一棟大樓的許多既有公寓業主而言，直接的財務後果，是大額特別攤提（special assessment）——疊加在一般 HOA 費用之上的一次性帳單——大量湧現，因為全州各地的管委會紛紛著手籌措資金，用於結構修繕以及許多大樓多年來未能足額提列的法定準備金。那些只憑廣告上的每月 HOA 費用去核算物業淨殖利率、卻沒有為準備金提列不足預留任何空間的業主，這才發現這個數字可能被單一一筆高達數萬美元的特別攤提帳單徹底比下去。這是一個有充分紀錄、大規模的真實案例，恰恰印證了本課成本堆疊章節所提醒的 HOA／準備金風險。
