The Rate Backdrop for Housing

住宅市場的利率背景

Module 05's payment-capacity math reduces to two numbers: the monthly payment a given loan size produces at a given rate, and how much of a buyer's income that payment consumes. A 2022-2024 global rate reset pushed that payment up sharply almost everywhere at once — this dashboard's own mortgage-rate column spans from 1.81% in Switzerland to 36.0% in Turkey, with most developed markets clustered between roughly 2.3% and 6.3% and several emerging markets running 7-13%. What's different city to city isn't the math itself — it's how each market transmits that rate into the price a buyer actually pays.

第 05 課的還款能力算術，最終可以化約成兩個數字：一筆特定金額的貸款在特定利率下，每月要繳多少錢，以及這筆月付金吃掉買方收入的比例。2022 到 2024 年的一次全球性利率重置，幾乎同時把這個月付金在多數地方大幅推高——本儀表板自己的房貸利率欄位，範圍從瑞士的 1.81% 到土耳其的 36.0%，多數已開發市場落在約 2.3% 到 6.3% 之間，部分新興市場則落在 7% 到 13%。城市與城市之間真正不同的不是這套算術本身——而是每個市場把這個利率傳導成買方實付價格的方式不一樣。

Two documented transmission mechanisms explain most of the dispersion in this dataset. First, fixed-rate lock-inIn markets dominated by long-term fixed-rate mortgages (the U.S. above all), homeowners who refinanced or bought at sub-4% rates before 2022 have little incentive to sell and re-borrow at today's rate — shrinking the supply of homes for sale even where demand has cooled.在以長期固定利率房貸為主的市場（尤其是美國），2022 年前以低於 4% 利率購屋或轉貸的屋主，幾乎沒有誘因出售並以今天的利率重新借款——即使需求已經降溫，可售房源也因此萎縮。, widely documented in the United States, means existing owners with pre-2022 sub-4% mortgages have little reason to sell and re-borrow at today's rate, which constrains the supply of listings independently of demand. Second, floating-rate pass-throughIn markets dominated by variable-rate or short fixed-term mortgages (Australia, Canada, the UK, New Zealand), a central bank rate hike shows up in existing owners' monthly payments within months, not just in new buyers' affordability — creating faster, more visible stress on current mortgage-holders.在以浮動利率或短期固定房貸為主的市場（澳洲、加拿大、英國、紐西蘭），央行升息會在數月內就反映到現有屋主的每月還款金額上，而不只是影響新買家的可負擔性——對現有房貸持有人形成更快、更明顯的壓力。, documented across Australia, Canada, the UK and New Zealand, means a central-bank hike shows up in existing owners' monthly payments within months — not just in new buyers' affordability math — creating faster, more visible stress on current mortgage-holders as loans reset or renew. The two mechanisms point to opposite failure modes: lock-in markets get a supply freeze; pass-through markets get a demand-and-distress shock. Both are visible in this dataset's regional reads below.

本資料集裡的分散程度，大致可以用兩個有紀錄可查的傳導機制來解釋。第一，固定利率鎖定效應In markets dominated by long-term fixed-rate mortgages (the U.S. above all), homeowners who refinanced or bought at sub-4% rates before 2022 have little incentive to sell and re-borrow at today's rate — shrinking the supply of homes for sale even where demand has cooled.在以長期固定利率房貸為主的市場（尤其是美國），2022 年前以低於 4% 利率購屋或轉貸的屋主，幾乎沒有誘因出售並以今天的利率重新借款——即使需求已經降溫，可售房源也因此萎縮。——在美國有廣泛紀錄——代表 2022 年前以低於 4% 利率承作的屋主，幾乎沒有誘因出售並以今天的利率重新借款，這會獨立於需求面壓縮可售房源。第二，浮動利率傳導效應In markets dominated by variable-rate or short fixed-term mortgages (Australia, Canada, the UK, New Zealand), a central bank rate hike shows up in existing owners' monthly payments within months, not just in new buyers' affordability — creating faster, more visible stress on current mortgage-holders.在以浮動利率或短期固定房貸為主的市場（澳洲、加拿大、英國、紐西蘭），央行升息會在數月內就反映到現有屋主的每月還款金額上，而不只是影響新買家的可負擔性——對現有房貸持有人形成更快、更明顯的壓力。——在澳洲、加拿大、英國與紐西蘭都有紀錄——代表央行一次升息，幾個月內就會反映在現有屋主的每月還款金額上，而不只是影響新買家的可負擔性算術，對貸款正在重置或續約的現有屋主形成更快、更明顯的壓力。這兩種機制指向相反的失衡型態：鎖定效應市場出現的是供給凍結；傳導效應市場出現的則是需求與壓力的直接衝擊。下方的區域研判段落裡都能看到這兩種型態的痕跡。

🎙 Live Case: This Cycle 這一輪週期的現在進行式

The U.S. lock-in effect is not a closed case study — it's still visibly shaping this dashboard's numbers. Every one of the 12 tracked U.S. cities carries the same 6.30% mortgage-rate figure, a level that's roughly double what a large share of existing American mortgage-holders locked in during 2020-2021. Widely reported since 2023, this has produced a documented listings shortage: owners who would otherwise sell simply don't, because moving would mean trading a sub-4% loan for one near 6.3%. That shows up in this data as a specific, checkable pattern — Boston's median days-on-market is just 12 and Silicon Valley's is 15, both among the fastest-selling markets in the entire 84-city dataset, even though Silicon Valley's price is down -2.5% year-over-year. Fast turnover with falling prices is the opposite of what a demand boom looks like; it's what a market with almost nothing for sale looks like. Whether this resolves via mortgage rates falling enough to "unlock" sellers, or via a slow multi-year thaw as owners eventually move anyway (death, divorce, job relocation, family growth), is one of the open questions in U.S. housing right now — and it hasn't finished playing out.

美國的鎖定效應並不是一個已經結束的案例研究——它此刻仍在明顯塑造著本儀表板的數字。本資料集追蹤的 12 座美國城市，房貸利率欄位全部都是同一個 6.30%，這個水準大約是 2020 至 2021 年間大量美國房貸持有人當初鎖定利率的兩倍。自 2023 年以來已被廣泛報導的一個結果，是一場有紀錄可查的房源短缺：原本會出售房子的屋主乾脆不賣了，因為搬家等於要把一筆低於 4% 的貸款，換成接近 6.3% 的新貸款。這在數據裡呈現出一種具體、可查核的型態——波士頓的中位待售天數僅 12 天，矽谷僅 15 天，兩者都是整個 84 座城市資料集裡數一數二快速成交的市場，即使矽谷的價格年減 -2.5%。「成交極快、價格卻在下跌」正好與需求榮景的樣貌相反；這是一個幾乎沒有東西可賣的市場才會出現的樣貌。這個局面最終會靠房貸利率跌到足以「解鎖」賣方而化解，還是靠屋主終究因為死亡、離婚、換工作、家庭成員增加等原因而緩慢地、花上多年才解凍，是目前美國房市最懸而未決的問題之一——而且還沒有結束。

Regional Cluster Reads

區域群組現況研判

Grouping the 84 tracked cities by geography and macro regime, rather than reading each one in isolation, is where the dashboard starts to tell a coherent story. Each cluster below is a read, not a rule.

把追蹤的 84 座城市按地理與總經制度分群、而不是逐一孤立解讀，正是這份儀表板開始講出一個連貫故事的地方。以下每一群都是一個研判，不是一條規則。

East Asia: Taiwan's correction, Japan's boom, Korea's split, Hong Kong's stabilization

東亞：台灣的修正、日本的熱潮、韓國的分裂、香港的止穩

Fifteen cities across Taiwan, Japan, Korea and Hong Kong tell four different stories. Taiwan's seven tracked cities are almost uniformly negative: Taipei -0.6%, Hsinchu -2.3%, New Taipei -3.0%, Taoyuan -3.7%, Tainan -2.6%, Taichung -6.8%, and Kaohsiung the dataset's second-worst at -7.3% — against rental yields as low as 1.8% (Taipei, the lowest in the entire dataset) to 3.0% (Kaohsiung). Japan runs the opposite direction: Tokyo +20.0% YoY, Sapporo +17.0%, Fukuoka +9.0%, Osaka +7.0%, Nagoya +4.2%, all on a rock-bottom 2.32% mortgage rate and vacancy at or below 3.5%. Korea is split within itself — Seoul +9.0% against a Land Transaction Permit Zone restricting even domestic flipping, Busan a much softer +1.0% with a -10.5% drawdown still open. Hong Kong shows the dataset's fourth-largest drawdown (-18.8%) but has turned modestly positive (+2.0% YoY), a stabilization read discussed further below.

台灣、日本、韓國與香港，這 15 座追蹤城市講的是四個不同的故事。台灣追蹤的七座城市幾乎全數為負：台北 -0.6%，新竹 -2.3%，新北市 -3.0%，桃園 -3.7%，台南 -2.6%，台中 -6.8%，高雄則是本資料集第二差的 -7.3%——租金殖利率低至 1.8%（台北，本資料集最低）到 3.0%（高雄）。日本則走向相反方向：東京年增 +20.0%，札幌 +17.0%，福岡 +9.0%，大阪 +7.0%，名古屋 +4.2%，全都建立在僅 2.32% 的超低房貸利率、以及 3.5% 以下的空置率之上。韓國內部本身就分裂——首爾 +9.0%，即使當地已劃為土地交易許可區、限制連本國人炒作都受限；釜山則溫和許多，僅 +1.0%，且 -10.5% 的跌幅缺口尚未回補。香港則呈現本資料集第四大的跌幅（-18.8%），但已轉為溫和正成長（年增 +2.0%），這是下文會進一步討論的止穩訊號。

What would change this read: if Taiwan's secondary cities (Taichung, Kaohsiung, Tainan) post two consecutive positive YoY readings, the correction is ending rather than deepening; if Tokyo's growth decelerates below +10% over the next several refreshes, Japan's boom is cooling on its own before any BOJ move forces it to; if Hong Kong's YoY turns negative again, the +2.0% reading above was noise, not a turn.

會讓這個判斷改變的條件：若台灣的次級城市（台中、高雄、台南）連續出現兩次正的年增率讀數，代表修正正在結束而非加深；若東京的成長率在接下來幾次更新中降到 +10% 以下，代表日本的熱潮正在自行降溫，而不需要等到日銀出手；若香港的年增率再度轉負，代表上述 +2.0% 只是雜訊，而不是真正的轉向。

Southeast Asia: an oversupply cluster with two hot exceptions

東南亞：一個供給過剩群組，加上兩個火熱的例外

Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam, Indonesia and the Philippines form an eight-city cluster defined by long selling times and, in several cases, very high vacancy: Jakarta carries the dataset's single highest vacancy rate at 40.0% on a 90-day DOM, Manila runs 26.0% vacancy on an 80-day DOM, and Kuala Lumpur runs 18.0% vacancy on a 90-day DOM. Bangkok CBD and Buenos Aires (in the Other Emerging cluster below) share the dataset's longest DOM at 120 days. Ho Chi Minh City is the sharp outlier at +23.0% YoY — the dataset's third-highest price growth — while Singapore itself sits apart from the rest of the region on tight, low-vacancy fundamentals (3.2% vacancy, 25-day DOM) rather than the oversupply pattern its neighbors show.

新加坡、馬來西亞、泰國、越南、印尼與菲律賓，組成一個以成交時間長、部分市場空置率極高為特徵的八城市群組：雅加達的空置率高達 40.0%，是本資料集單一最高，待售天數 90 天；馬尼拉空置率 26.0%，待售天數 80 天；吉隆坡空置率 18.0%，待售天數 90 天。曼谷 CBD 與下文「其他新興市場」群組裡的布宜諾斯艾利斯，並列本資料集待售天數最長的 120 天。胡志明市是鮮明的例外，年增 +23.0%——是本資料集第三高的價格成長——而新加坡本身則以緊俏、低空置的基本面（空置率 3.2%，待售天數 25 天）與區域其他市場的供給過剩型態明顯不同。

What would change this read: two or more of Jakarta, Manila, and Kuala Lumpur's vacancy rates turning down simultaneously would suggest regional absorption is finally catching a multi-year supply wave rather than each market's own local story; Ho Chi Minh City's growth decelerating sharply toward single digits would suggest its outlier run is normalizing.

會讓這個判斷改變的條件：若雅加達、馬尼拉、吉隆坡的空置率有兩個以上同時開始下降，代表區域吸納力終於追上了一波持續多年的供給浪潮，而不只是各市場自己的個別故事；若胡志明市的成長率快速降溫到個位數，代表這波離群走勢正在回歸常態。

ANZ & Canada: same rate shock, opposite outcomes

澳紐與加拿大：同一場利率衝擊，相反的結果

Australia, New Zealand and Canada all ran through the same 2022-2024 rate-hiking cycle under floating-rate pass-through mortgage systems, yet the ten cities in this cluster split sharply. Australia (ex-Sydney) has decisively re-accelerated: Perth +24.3% YoY (the dataset's second-highest, on just 0.7% vacancy and a 12-day DOM tied for the fastest in the dataset), Brisbane +19.1%, Adelaide +11.4%, Gold Coast +10.5%, Melbourne +3.4%, with only Sydney flat at 0.0%. Canada has not: Toronto -7.4% (the dataset's single worst YoY reading) with an -18.5% drawdown still open, and Vancouver -6.8% with a -13.1% drawdown. New Zealand sits in between and shows the clearest level-versus-momentum split in the whole dataset: Auckland carries the dataset's largest cumulative drawdown at -24.7%, yet its current YoY has narrowed to just -1.2% — a market that hasn't recovered any lost ground but may be flattening — while Christchurch has already turned convincingly positive at +6.7%.

澳洲、紐西蘭與加拿大都經歷了同一場 2022 到 2024 年的升息週期，且都採用浮動利率傳導型的房貸制度，但這個群組裡的十座城市卻出現劇烈分化。澳洲（雪梨除外）已經明確重新加速：伯斯年增 +24.3%（本資料集第二高，空置率僅 0.7%，待售天數 12 天，與本資料集最快並列），布里斯本 +19.1%，阿德萊德 +11.4%，黃金海岸 +10.5%，墨爾本 +3.4%，只有雪梨持平於 0.0%。加拿大則沒有：多倫多 -7.4%（本資料集單一最差的年增率讀數），跌幅缺口仍有 -18.5% 尚未回補；溫哥華 -6.8%，跌幅缺口 -13.1%。紐西蘭則介於兩者之間，呈現整個資料集裡最清楚的「水位 vs. 動能」分裂：奧克蘭的累積跌幅是本資料集最大的 -24.7%，但目前的年增率已收斂到僅 -1.2%——一個尚未回補任何失土、但可能正在趨於平緩的市場——而基督城則已明確轉為正成長 +6.7%。

The most likely explanation for the split, consistent with widely documented supply data, is that Australia's chronic under-building and strong net migration absorbed the rate shock within a couple of years, while a larger presale-condo pipeline in Toronto specifically amplified it. What would change this read: Toronto or Vancouver posting two consecutive positive YoY readings would say Canada's correction is ending; Perth or Brisbane's vacancy rising meaningfully off its current sub-1.5% floor would say the Australian boom is running out of room; Auckland turning decisively positive (not just less negative) would confirm New Zealand has moved from bottoming to recovery.

與廣泛紀錄可查的供給數據一致，這種分化最可能的解釋是：澳洲長期的建量不足加上強勁的淨移民，在一兩年內就吸收了這場利率衝擊；而多倫多特別龐大的預售型公寓供給管線，則放大了衝擊。會讓這個判斷改變的條件：若多倫多或溫哥華連續出現兩次正的年增率讀數，代表加拿大的修正正在結束；若伯斯或布里斯本的空置率從目前 1.5% 以下的低點明顯回升，代表澳洲這波熱潮已經沒有太多空間；若奧克蘭轉為明確的正成長（而不只是跌幅縮小），將證實紐西蘭已經從探底走向復甦。

🎙 Live Case: This Cycle 這一輪週期的現在進行式

The Bank of Canada and the Reserve Bank of Australia both raised rates aggressively through 2022-2023 to fight the same inflation shock, and both countries' mortgage markets are dominated by variable-rate and short-term-fixed loans that transmit hikes to existing borrowers quickly — the floating-rate pass-through mechanism described above. What diverged was the supply side already in motion when the hikes hit. Toronto in particular carried a large pipeline of presale condominium units bought years earlier by investors betting on continued appreciation; as carrying costs rose and resale prices softened, a widely reported wave of those units came to market or fell into completion-and-resale stress simultaneously, deepening the correction this dashboard still shows as Toronto's -18.5% open drawdown. Australia's housing shortage, by contrast, had been building for years independent of interest rates — chronic under-approval of new dwellings against strong net overseas migration — so even a sharp rate shock only interrupted the price trend for roughly a year before scarcity reasserted itself, consistent with Perth and Brisbane's return to double-digit growth. Both episodes are still unfolding: Canada's federal ban on most non-resident residential purchases remains in force through at least January 2027, and Australia's own established-dwelling foreign-buyer ban has been extended into the 2026-27 budget cycle — both policy responses to the same underlying affordability pressure, arriving from opposite starting points in the price cycle.

加拿大央行與澳洲央行都在 2022 到 2023 年間積極升息，對抗同一波通膨衝擊，而兩國的房貸市場都以浮動利率與短期固定利率貸款為主，會很快把升息傳導到現有借款人身上——正是前文所述的浮動利率傳導機制。真正出現分歧的，是升息當下、已經在醞釀中的供給面。多倫多尤其背負著一大批數年前由投資人買進、賭房價會持續上漲的預售公寓管線；隨著持有成本上升、轉售價格走軟，廣泛報導指出這批單位同時湧入市場、或陷入完工後求售的壓力，加深了這份儀表板至今仍顯示、多倫多尚未回補的 -18.5% 跌幅缺口。相對地，澳洲的房屋短缺早在利率變動之前就已經醞釀多年——長期核准新建量不足，加上強勁的海外淨移民——因此即使遭遇劇烈的利率衝擊，也只中斷了價格趨勢約一年左右，稀缺性便重新主導市場，與伯斯、布里斯本重返雙位數成長的走勢一致。這兩段故事都還在進行中：加拿大針對多數非居民住宅購買的聯邦禁令，至少持續有效到 2027 年 1 月；澳洲自身針對既有住宅的外國買家禁令，也已延伸進 2026-27 年度預算週期——兩者都是對同一種深層可負擔性壓力的政策回應，只是從價格週期裡截然相反的起點出發。

United States: coastal softness, gateway tightness, Sunbelt bifurcation

美國：沿海走軟、門戶市場緊俏、陽光帶分裂

The 12 U.S. cities in the dataset split into three groups that don't map neatly onto any single national story. California's big coastal metros are flat-to-negative: Silicon Valley -2.5%, Los Angeles -1.6%, San Diego -1.5%, San Francisco the exception at +2.1% — all against extreme price-to-income ratios (Silicon Valley 19.5, San Francisco 18.0, the dataset's two highest). Northeastern and Midwestern gateways are firm: NYC +5.2%, Chicago +5.4%, Boston +3.3%, all supply-tagged "tight" with vacancy at or below 3.5%. The Sunbelt is genuinely split — Denver -3.6% and Phoenix -1.5% are soft, Austin is the outlier with the dataset's second-largest drawdown (-24.6%) still open at -3.0% YoY, while Miami runs +10.6% YoY against a rent figure that's actually falling (-1.8%) and a "loose" supply tag — the sharpest valuation-stretch flag among the major U.S. metros, discussed further in the yield-repair section below.

本資料集的 12 座美國城市，分裂成三個群組，且無法乾脆地套進任何單一的全國性故事。加州的大型沿海都會區持平到下跌：矽谷 -2.5%，洛杉磯 -1.6%，聖地牙哥 -1.5%，例外的是舊金山 +2.1%——三者都對應著極端的房價所得比（矽谷 19.5，舊金山 18.0，本資料集最高的兩個）。東北部與中西部的門戶市場則穩健：紐約 +5.2%，芝加哥 +5.4%，波士頓 +3.3%，供給標籤全都是「緊俏」，空置率皆在 3.5% 以下。陽光帶則真正呈現分裂——丹佛 -3.6% 與鳳凰城 -1.5% 偏軟，奧斯丁是例外，背負著本資料集第二大、尚未回補的跌幅缺口（-24.6%），目前年增率仍是 -3.0%；邁阿密則年增 +10.6%，對照的租金數字卻實際上是下跌的（-1.8%），供給標籤是「寬鬆」——是美國主要都會區中最尖銳的估值拉伸訊號，下方殖利率修復段落會進一步討論。

Europe: multi-speed, from Turkish inflation to Irish scarcity

歐洲：多速前進，從土耳其的通膨到愛爾蘭的稀缺

Europe's 28 tracked cities run at genuinely different speeds. The UK's regional cities are firm and tight — Newcastle +5.1% YoY with the dataset's second-highest rent growth outside emerging markets at +9.9%, Manchester +4.4%, all with sub-2% vacancy and yields of 5.6-6.1% — while London itself is down -3.0% YoY with a -7% open drawdown despite positive rent growth (+2.2%), a classic core-versus-regional split. Ireland is the tightest reading in Europe by vacancy: Dublin 0.5%, Cork just 0.6%, with Cork's rent growth at +13.3% the dataset's fourth-highest overall. Germany and France are still working through open drawdowns from the 2022-2023 correction — Munich -10%, Frankfurt -11%, Paris -12%, and Lyon the dataset's fifth-largest drawdown at -18.5% — even as most of their current YoY readings have turned modestly positive. Iberia is the hottest sub-region: Valencia +14.4%, Madrid +14.0%, Barcelona +10.4% (the latter with rent actually falling -3.9%, another stretch flag). And Turkey is the outlier that breaks the regional pattern entirely: Istanbul's +27.8% price growth and +35.0% rent growth sit against a 36.0% mortgage rate — the dataset's highest by a wide margin — a strong tell that lira depreciation and domestic inflation, not real appreciation, explain most of the headline number.

歐洲追蹤的 28 座城市，走的是真正不同的速度。英國的區域城市穩健且緊俏——紐卡斯爾年增 +5.1%，租金成長 +9.9%，是本資料集除新興市場外第二高，曼徹斯特 +4.4%，空置率全都在 2% 以下，殖利率 5.6% 到 6.1%——而倫敦本身卻年減 -3.0%，仍有 -7% 的跌幅缺口尚未回補，儘管租金成長為正（+2.2%），是典型的核心區 vs. 區域城市分裂。愛爾蘭以空置率而言是全歐洲最緊俏的：都柏林 0.5%，科克僅 0.6%，科克的租金成長 +13.3%，是本資料集整體第四高。德國與法國仍在消化 2022 到 2023 年修正留下的跌幅缺口——慕尼黑 -10%，法蘭克福 -11%，巴黎 -12%，里昂則是本資料集第五大的跌幅 -18.5%——即使多數目前的年增率讀數都已轉為溫和正值。伊比利半島是最火熱的次區域：瓦倫西亞 +14.4%，馬德里 +14.0%，巴塞隆納 +10.4%（後者的租金其實是下跌的 -3.9%，又是一個拉伸訊號）。而土耳其則是徹底打破區域型態的離群值：伊斯坦堡 +27.8% 的價格成長與 +35.0% 的租金成長，對照的是 36.0% 的房貸利率——遠遠是本資料集最高——強烈暗示里拉貶值與國內通膨、而非真實增值，解釋了這個headline數字的絕大部分。

What would change these reads: Lyon or Paris posting two consecutive positive YoY readings with the drawdown narrowing would say France's correction is ending; Spain's growth decelerating sharply toward single digits would say the current boom is cooling on its own; Istanbul's price and rent growth continuing even after Turkish inflation and lira depreciation slow would say more than a currency effect is at work.

會讓這些判斷改變的條件：若里昂或巴黎連續出現兩次正的年增率讀數、跌幅缺口同步收窄，代表法國的修正正在結束；若西班牙的成長率快速降溫到個位數，代表目前這波熱潮正在自行降溫；若伊斯坦堡的價格與租金成長，在土耳其通膨與里拉貶值放緩之後仍然持續，代表背後不只是貨幣效應在起作用。

High-growth emerging: India, the Gulf, and Latin America's nominal boom

高成長新興市場：印度、波灣與拉美的名目熱潮

India's four tracked cities show broad-based double-digit growth — Delhi NCR +15.0%, Bangalore +13.0%, Mumbai +11.0% with the dataset's third-highest rent growth at +17.5% — all against a shared 7.7% mortgage rate. Latin America runs even hotter in headline terms: Buenos Aires shows the dataset's single largest rent-versus-price gap (rent +31.3% against price +5.6%, a 25.7-point spread) on a 120-day DOM and a still-open -11.8% drawdown, while Lima carries the dataset's third-largest drawdown at -20.9%. Both India and Argentina share the same tell as Istanbul above: high local mortgage rates (7.7% and 10.0% respectively) sitting alongside high nominal growth, a pattern worth re-checking against each currency's own inflation trend before treating the growth number as pure real appreciation. Dubai, tracked separately, shows a tighter version of the same emerging-market pattern — +12.5% YoY, +4.1% rent, 8.5% vacancy — without India or Argentina's inflation-rate tell (its mortgage rate sits at a moderate 3.70%, closer to the developed-market range).

印度追蹤的四座城市，呈現廣泛的雙位數成長——德里首都圈 +15.0%，班加羅爾 +13.0%，孟買 +11.0%，且租金成長 +17.5% 是本資料集第三高——三者共用一個 7.7% 的房貸利率。拉丁美洲的headline數字甚至更火熱：布宜諾斯艾利斯呈現本資料集單一最大的租金與價格落差（租金 +31.3% 對照價格 +5.6%，落差高達 25.7 個百分點），待售天數 120 天，跌幅缺口 -11.8% 仍未回補；利馬則背負著本資料集第三大的跌幅 -20.9%。印度與阿根廷都與前述的伊斯坦堡共享同一個線索：偏高的當地房貸利率（分別為 7.7% 與 10.0%）與偏高的名目成長並存，這種型態值得在把這個成長數字當成純粹的真實增值之前，重新對照各自貨幣的通膨走勢做核對。獨立追蹤的杜拜，則呈現同一種新興市場型態較緊俏的版本——年增 +12.5%，租金 +4.1%，空置率 8.5%——但沒有印度或阿根廷那種通膨利率線索（其房貸利率為溫和的 3.70%，更接近已開發市場的區間）。

Of the 81 cities carrying both figures, 40 — very close to half — show rent growth exceeding price growth. But the gap's sign matters less than what's happening underneath it. Sydney (price flat at 0.0%, rent +7.8%) is repair from healthy rent growth on a plateaued price base, consistent with Australia's well-documented rental supply crunch pushing income up even as purchase prices pause. Toronto (price -7.4%, rent -0.8%) technically qualifies too, but this is the least-bad reading of a market still in outright decline — rent simply fell less than price did — not a healthy signal, and worth distinguishing from Sydney's case. Cork (price +4.2%, rent +13.3%, vacancy just 0.6%) is the cleanest genuine-scarcity repair story in the dataset. Several Taiwanese cities (Kaohsiung, Taichung, Hsinchu) also show technical repair, but only because prices are correcting faster than a flat, index-driven +1.7% rent figure — a reminder to re-check the data-vintage caveat from this page's opening banner before reading too much into that specific number.

在同時擁有兩個數字的 81 座城市裡，有 40 座——非常接近一半——租金成長超過價格成長。但落差的正負號，重要性其實不如落差底下究竟發生了什麼。雪梨（價格持平於 0.0%，租金 +7.8%）是健康租金成長、價格持平所帶來的修復，與澳洲有廣泛紀錄可查的租屋供給緊縮一致——即使購屋價格暫停，收益仍在上升。多倫多（價格 -7.4%，租金 -0.8%）在技術上也符合條件，但這是一個仍在明確下跌中的市場、最不糟的一種讀法——租金只是跌得比價格少——並非健康訊號，值得與雪梨的情況區分開來。科克（價格 +4.2%，租金 +13.3%，空置率僅 0.6%）是本資料集中最乾淨的真正稀缺型修復故事。幾個台灣城市（高雄、台中、新竹）也顯示技術上的修復，但那只是因為價格修正的速度快過一個持平、指數驅動的 +1.7% 租金數字——這提醒我們，在對這個特定數字做過多解讀之前，先回頭核對本頁開場橫幅提到的資料版本警示。

On the stretch side, checking vacancy and the supply tag alongside the gap changes the read materially. Perth carries the dataset's largest price-minus-rent gap (+21.9 points), but sits on 0.7% vacancy and a "tight" supply tag — more consistent with a genuine, if extreme, scarcity-driven run than pure speculation. Miami's much smaller gap (+12.4 points) is paired with rent that's actually falling (-1.8%), 7.5% vacancy, and a "loose" supply tag — a fragile combination, and the sharpest stretch flag among the major U.S. metros. Berlin shows a similar-looking negative rent figure (-4.3% against +3.0% price growth), but Germany's rent-control regime (the Mietpreisbremse and related local rent caps) means a falling measured-rent figure there can partly reflect regulation rather than weak demand — a caveat worth applying before assuming Berlin's gap means the same thing as Miami's.

在拉伸端，把空置率與供給標籤搭配落差一起看，會實質改變解讀。伯斯背負著本資料集最大的「價格減租金」落差（+21.9 個百分點），但空置率僅 0.7%、供給標籤是「緊俏」——與其說是純粹投機，更貼近一場真正的、只是極端的稀缺型走勢。邁阿密的落差小得多（+12.4 個百分點），但對照的租金其實在下跌（-1.8%），空置率 7.5%，供給標籤是「寬鬆」——是一個脆弱的組合，也是美國主要都會區中最尖銳的拉伸訊號。柏林顯示出看起來相似的負租金數字（-4.3%，對照價格成長 +3.0%），但德國的租金管制制度（Mietpreisbremse 租金煞車機制及相關地方租金上限）代表當地被衡量出來的租金下跌，可能有一部分反映的是法規、而不是需求疲弱——這是在假設柏林的落差與邁阿密代表同一件事之前，值得先套用的一個但書。

The Foreign-Buyer Regime Snapshot

外國買家制度現況快照

The dashboard's own foreign-buyer cost data spans a genuine restriction spectrum. At the taxed-but-open end, Singapore lets foreigners buy private condos without approval but charges a 60% Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty since April 2023, on top of standard duty up to 6% — one of the costliest entry tickets in the developed world, while still technically "free" to buy. At the outright-restricted end, Canada's federal ban on most non-resident purchases of existing homes runs through at least January 2027; Australia bans foreign buyers from established (resale) dwellings in NSW/Sydney under a federal ban extended into the 2026-27 budget cycle, alongside a 9% NSW surcharge duty; New Zealand bans ordinary foreign individuals from buying existing Auckland homes outright, with only narrow developer and ultra-high-net-worth carve-outs; Switzerland's decades-old Lex Koller requires cantonal authorization and gives Zurich zero holiday-home quota; South Korea designated all of Seoul a Land Transaction Permit Zone in August 2025, requiring buyers to actually move in — blocking pure non-resident investment; and India bars non-NRI foreign nationals from buying residential property under FEMA rules. At the open end, the U.S., Japan, Ireland, and most Latin American markets tracked here (Mexico, Brazil, Chile, Argentina, Peru, Colombia) apply no foreign-buyer surcharge or restriction at all, and Hong Kong removed its 15% non-resident stamp duty in the February 2024 budget.

本儀表板自身的外國買家成本數據，橫跨一個真實的限制光譜。在「課稅但開放」的一端，新加坡讓外國人可以無需核准就購買私人公寓，但自 2023 年 4 月起，會在最高 6% 的標準印花稅之上，再課徵 60% 的額外買方印花稅——是已開發世界裡進場成本數一數二高的門票，卻仍在技術上「自由」可買。在「直接限制」的一端，加拿大針對多數非居民購買既有住宅的聯邦禁令，至少持續到 2027 年 1 月；澳洲則在延伸進 2026-27 年度預算週期的聯邦禁令下，禁止外國買家購買新南威爾斯州／雪梨的既有（轉售）住宅，並加徵新南威爾斯州 9% 的附加印花稅；紐西蘭直接禁止一般外國個人購買奧克蘭的既有住宅，僅有狹窄的開發商與超高淨值人士例外條款；瑞士行之有年的 Lex Koller 法規要求州級核准，且蘇黎世州的度假屋配額為零；韓國自 2025 年 8 月起把整個首爾劃為土地交易許可區，要求買方實際入住——藉此阻擋純粹的非居民投資性購買；印度則依 FEMA 規定，禁止非 NRI 的外國國民購買住宅不動產。在「開放」的一端，美國、日本、愛爾蘭，以及本頁追蹤的多數拉丁美洲市場（墨西哥、巴西、智利、阿根廷、秘魯、哥倫比亞），都完全不課徵外國買家附加稅或設限，香港也已在 2024 年 2 月的財政預算案中取消了 15% 的非永久居民印花稅。

Read together, restriction intensity tracks each country's own domestic affordability and political pressure more than it tracks any global standard: Canada, Australia, New Zealand and Singapore — all markets where this dashboard also shows acute local price pressure or a sustained affordability crisis — have moved toward restriction or heavy taxation of foreign demand, while Japan, facing a demographic backdrop of population decline rather than affordability-driven political pressure, has stayed entirely open even as Tokyo posts this dataset's second-highest price growth. See the full restriction table, updated independently of this page, at the residential dashboard.

整體來看，限制強度追蹤的與其說是任何全球標準，不如說是各國自身的國內可負擔性與政治壓力：加拿大、澳洲、紐西蘭與新加坡——這些市場在本儀表板上同時也顯示出明顯的當地價格壓力或持續的可負擔性危機——都已轉向限制或重稅外國需求；而日本面對的是人口減少的人口結構背景、而非可負擔性驅動的政治壓力，即使東京目前的價格成長是本資料集第二高，日本依然完全保持開放。完整的限制對照表，會獨立於本頁持續更新，請見住宅儀表板。

🎙 Live Case: This Cycle 這一輪週期的現在進行式

China's mainland cities aren't tracked in this residential dataset, but their housing crisis is a documented backdrop for several markets that are. Evergrande's 2021 default marked the start of a rolling developer liquidity crisis that spread across the sector, rooted in the presale (预售) financing model: buyers paid in full for apartments years before completion, and developer cash was routinely diverted to other projects rather than held for the specific building being sold. When the "three red lines" leverage rules (2020) cut off developers' easy refinancing, huge numbers of paid-for, unbuilt units were left stranded, prompting local governments to launch "guaranteed delivery" (保交楼) programs from 2022 onward to force completion of stalled projects — a program still working through its backlog years later. The aftermath rippled well beyond mainland balance sheets. Hong Kong's property market, closely linked to mainland capital and sentiment, entered its own multi-year downturn from 2022 that shows up in this dashboard as an -18.8% open drawdown — the dataset's fourth-largest — even as HK's YoY has turned modestly positive. And outbound Chinese capital that had been a significant bid in Vancouver, Toronto, Sydney and Auckland through the 2015-2019 period retrenched sharply, meaning the foreign-buyer restrictions those markets later imposed landed on a capital flow that had already shrunk for reasons unrelated to the policy itself — worth keeping in mind when reading how much credit any single ban deserves for cooling a market.

中國大陸城市並未被納入這個住宅資料集的追蹤範圍，但它們的房市危機，是本頁追蹤的好幾個市場背後有紀錄可查的背景。恒大於 2021 年違約，標誌著一場延燒整個板塊、根植於預售制度的滾動式開發商流動性危機的開端：買方在完工前數年就已付清全額房款，而開發商的現金卻經常被挪用到其他專案，而非留給特定在售建案。當「三條紅線」槓桿限制（2020 年）切斷開發商的容易再融資管道後，大量已付款、卻未完工的單位便被擱置，促使地方政府自 2022 年起推出「保交楼」計畫，強制完成停滯專案——這項計畫數年後至今仍在消化積壓。這場餘波的影響遠遠超出了中國大陸自身的資產負債表。與中國大陸資金及情緒緊密連動的香港房地產市場，自 2022 年起進入了自己長達數年的下行週期，反映在本儀表板上就是尚未回補的 -18.8% 跌幅缺口——本資料集第四大——即使香港的年增率已轉為溫和正值。而 2015 到 2019 年間曾是溫哥華、多倫多、雪梨與奧克蘭重要買盤的中國海外資金，也大幅緊縮，這代表這些市場後來祭出的外國買家限制措施，其實是落在一個早已因與政策本身無關的原因而萎縮的資金流上——在解讀任何單一禁令對市場降溫「功勞」有多大時，值得記住這一點。

Cycle-Position Calls

週期定位研判

Everything below is framed as 研判 — a research judgment made from the data available right now — not a prediction of fact. Each call comes with 2-3 falsifiable markers: specific, checkable signals that, if they show up, mean this particular call was wrong.

以下所有內容，都以「研判」的方式框定——這是根據目前可取得的數據所做出的研究判斷——而不是對未來事實的預測。每一則判斷，都搭配 2 到 3 個可證偽的觀察指標：具體、可查核的訊號，一旦出現，就代表這個特定判斷是錯的。

Taiwan: late-cycle correction, not yet confirmed as bottoming

台灣：週期尾聲的修正，尚未確認探底

Six of seven tracked Taiwanese cities are still negative YoY, with Kaohsiung, Taichung and Tainan carrying open drawdowns of -6% to -8% and the lowest rental yields in the dataset (1.8-3.0%). This reads as a correction still in progress rather than a completed cycle.

追蹤的七座台灣城市裡有六座年增率仍為負，高雄、台中與台南背負著 -6% 到 -8% 的未回補跌幅缺口，租金殖利率也是本資料集最低（1.8% 到 3.0%）。這讀起來像是一場仍在進行中的修正，而不是一個已經結束的週期。

  • This call is wrong if: Taichung, Kaohsiung or Tainan post two consecutive positive YoY readings, signaling the correction is ending.這個判斷會錯，如果：台中、高雄或台南連續出現兩次正的年增率讀數，代表修正正在結束。
  • This call is wrong if: Taipei or New Taipei vacancy (currently 7.1% and 7.5%, already elevated for East Asia) climbs further rather than stabilizing.這個判斷會錯，如果：台北或新北市的空置率（目前 7.1% 與 7.5%，以東亞而言已偏高）持續攀升而非趨於穩定。

Japan: Expansion phase, with the least payment-capacity cushion in the dataset

日本：擴張期，卻是本資料集還款能力緩衝最薄的市場

Tokyo's +20.0% YoY on a 2.32% mortgage rate is a textbook Expansion-quadrant read — cheap leverage plus strong price momentum — but it's also the most rate-sensitive call in this dataset: a BOJ move that lifts Japan's uniquely low mortgage rate would hit the payment-capacity math directly, not just sentiment.

東京在 2.32% 房貸利率下年增 +20.0%，是教科書等級的擴張期讀數——廉價槓桿加上強勁的價格動能——但這也是本資料集裡對利率最敏感的判斷：一旦日銀出手，抬升日本這個獨特偏低的房貸利率，會直接打擊還款能力的算術，而不只是市場情緒。

  • This call is wrong if: Tokyo's YoY decelerates below +10% over the next several dashboard refreshes without any BOJ move — that would say the boom is cooling on its own, not being forced to.這個判斷會錯，如果：東京的年增率在接下來幾次儀表板更新中降到 +10% 以下、且日銀並未出手——這將顯示熱潮正在自行降溫，而不是被迫降溫。
  • This call is wrong if: Japan's mortgage rate rises meaningfully off its current 2.32% floor without a corresponding cooling in Tokyo's price growth — that would say the market hasn't yet repriced for the new rate.這個判斷會錯，如果：日本的房貸利率從目前 2.32% 的低點明顯上升，卻沒有伴隨東京價格成長的相應降溫——這將顯示市場尚未針對新利率重新定價。

ANZ (ex-Sydney) vs. Canada: a rate-shock divergence still resolving

澳洲（雪梨除外）vs. 加拿大：一場尚未化解的利率衝擊分歧

Australia's boom (Perth +24.3%, Brisbane +19.1%) and Canada's open correction (Toronto -7.4%, Vancouver -6.8%) both trace back to the same 2022-2024 rate shock under floating-rate mortgage systems, diverging on local supply. New Zealand — bottoming (Auckland) alongside recovered (Christchurch) — sits in between.

澳洲的熱潮（伯斯 +24.3%，布里斯本 +19.1%）與加拿大仍未結束的修正（多倫多 -7.4%，溫哥華 -6.8%），兩者都能追溯到同一場 2022 到 2024 年、發生在浮動利率房貸制度下的利率衝擊，只是因為當地供給狀況不同而分歧。紐西蘭——奧克蘭正在探底、基督城已經復甦——則介於兩者之間。

  • This call is wrong if: Toronto or Vancouver posts two or more consecutive positive YoY readings, or their DOM (currently 45 and 50 days) shortens sharply — either would say Canada's correction is ending, not deepening.這個判斷會錯，如果：多倫多或溫哥華連續出現兩次以上正的年增率讀數，或其待售天數（目前 45 天與 50 天）明顯縮短——兩者都將顯示加拿大的修正正在結束，而非加深。
  • This call is wrong if: Perth's or Brisbane's vacancy (currently 0.7% and 1.2%) rises meaningfully off its near-zero floor — an early tell that Australia's boom is running out of scarcity to price.這個判斷會錯，如果：伯斯或布里斯本的空置率（目前 0.7% 與 1.2%）從接近於零的低點明顯回升——這是澳洲這波熱潮已無稀缺性可以定價的早期訊號。

United States: lock-in-constrained, not demand-driven, outside a few stretch cases

美國：受鎖定效應限制，而非需求驅動——除了少數幾個拉伸案例

Fast DOM alongside flat-to-negative price growth in California and the gateway metros looks most consistent with a supply-frozen market rather than a demand boom; Miami's positive-price/negative-rent combination is the clear exception worth watching separately.

加州與門戶都會區「待售天數快、價格卻持平或下跌」的組合，比較貼近一個供給凍結的市場，而不是需求驅動的榮景；邁阿密「價格為正、租金為負」的組合則是明顯的例外，值得單獨追蹤。

  • This call is wrong if: the 6.30% U.S. mortgage figure falls meaningfully and DOM in currently-fast markets (Boston 12, SF 13) extends rather than stays flat — that would say the lock-in freeze is starting to thaw.這個判斷會錯，如果：6.30% 的美國房貸利率數字明顯下降，而目前成交極快的市場（波士頓 12 天，舊金山 13 天）的待售天數卻拉長，而非持平——這將顯示鎖定效應正開始解凍。
  • This call is wrong if: Miami's rent growth stays negative while price growth stays double-digit for two more readings — that would confirm the stretch flag is a real pattern, not noise, and this call should be revised to flag genuine risk rather than just watch it.這個判斷會錯，如果：邁阿密的租金成長連續兩次以上讀數維持負值、價格成長卻維持雙位數——這將證實這個拉伸訊號是真實型態、而非雜訊，屆時這個判斷應改為明確標記真正的風險，而不只是持續觀察。

High-nominal-growth markets: apply the currency-decomposition discipline

高名目成長市場：套用貨幣拆解紀律

Istanbul (+27.8% price, 36.0% mortgage rate), Buenos Aires (rent +31.3%, 10.0% mortgage), and India's cities (9-15% price growth, 7.7% mortgage) all pair unusually high nominal growth with unusually high local mortgage rates — the residential-market equivalent of checking what a headline cap-rate spread is really pricing before treating it as comparable across currencies.

伊斯坦堡（價格 +27.8%，房貸利率 36.0%）、布宜諾斯艾利斯（租金 +31.3%，房貸利率 10.0%）與印度各城市（價格成長 9% 到 15%，房貸利率 7.7%），都把異常高的名目成長與異常高的當地房貸利率配對在一起——這是住宅市場版本的「在把headline資本化率利差當成可跨貨幣比較之前，先確認它究竟在為什麼定價」。

  • This call is wrong if: Istanbul's or Buenos Aires' growth continues even after Turkish/Argentine inflation and currency depreciation slow — that would say more than a nominal effect is at work.這個判斷會錯，如果：即使土耳其／阿根廷的通膨與貨幣貶值放緩，伊斯坦堡或布宜諾斯艾利斯的成長仍持續——這將顯示背後不只是名目效應在起作用。
  • This call is wrong if: India's mortgage rate (currently a flat 7.7% across all four tracked cities) falls meaningfully while rent growth stays in the double digits — that would suggest real, rate-independent demand rather than an inflation-linked story.這個判斷會錯，如果：印度的房貸利率（目前四座追蹤城市一致為 7.7%）明顯下降，而租金成長仍維持雙位數——這將暗示背後是真實、與利率無關的需求，而非通膨連動的故事。

What This Means for a Learner

對學習者的意義

If you're evaluating a specific property right now, this page is a starting lens, not a substitute for underwriting it yourself. A few direct pointers: sizing a mortgage against today's rate environment — re-read Module 05's leverage mechanics before letting this page's rate-backdrop framing bias your own payment math. Comparing a city's headline growth number across countries — re-read the yield-repair section above and this page's Istanbul/Perth pairing before treating any single price-growth figure as directly comparable across currencies. Evaluating a specific market or district — re-read Module 13's market-and-district tools and Module 06's cycle mechanics, and treat this page's falsifiable markers as your literal watch-list rather than a standing conclusion. Weighing a cross-border purchase — re-read Module 16 (tax and holding structures) and Module 17 (cross-border buying) alongside the foreign-buyer regime section above, since a restriction or surcharge can change the underwriting math as much as the price itself.

如果你正在評估一個具體的物件，本頁是一個起手的視角，不能取代你自己親手做的估值評估。幾個直接的指引：針對今天的利率環境核算房貸規模——先重讀第 05 課的槓桿機制，再讓本頁的利率背景框架影響你自己的月付金算術。跨國比較一座城市的headline成長數字——先重讀上方的殖利率修復段落，以及本頁伊斯坦堡與伯斯的配對案例，再把任何單一的價格成長數字當成可以跨貨幣直接比較的東西。評估一個具體的市場或區域——重讀第 13 課的市場與區域工具，以及第 06 課的週期機制，並把本頁的可證偽觀察指標當成你自己真正要盯的清單，而不是一個恆常不變的結論。衡量一筆跨境購屋——重讀第 16 課（稅務與持有架構）與第 17 課（跨境置產），並搭配上方的外國買家制度段落，因為一項限制或附加稅，對估值算術的影響可能不亞於價格本身。
