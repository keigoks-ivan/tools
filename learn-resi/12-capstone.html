Two closings, two sets of numbers, and no shortcuts. The first is a $480,000 two-bedroom condo (hypothetical example) with an asking price, a claimed rent, an HOA fee and a mortgage quote — four numbers that all sound reasonable in isolation and are all wrong in a way that only closed comps, meeting minutes and a second interest-rate scenario can expose. The second is a homeowner deciding whether to sell and trade up or keep the current place as a rental and buy a second property — a decision that lives or dies on debt-service math, not on which option feels more exciting.

兩筆成交，兩組數字，沒有捷徑。第一筆是一間開價 $480,000 的兩房公寓（假設範例）——開價、宣稱的租金、管理費與房貸報價，這四個數字單獨看都很合理，但每一個都藏著只有靠已成交比較物件、管委會會議紀錄與第二種利率情境才能揭穿的問題。第二筆是一位屋主要決定：賣掉現有住宅換大坪數，還是保留現屋出租、另外再買一間——這個決定的成敗取決於償債能力的數學，而不是哪個選項聽起來比較讓人心動。

Work both deals in order. Every later task in a deal depends on the numbers established in an earlier one, exactly like a real closing — you cannot skip to the yield calculation without first fixing what rent you are actually underwriting.

請依序完成兩筆交易的每一項任務。每一項任務都依賴前面任務所建立的數字，就像真實成交一樣——你沒辦法跳過「先確認到底該核算多少租金」這一步，就直接去算殖利率。

Deal One: The Rental Condo

交易一：出租公寓

The listing (hypothetical example): a two-bedroom, two-bathroom condo in a 120-unit mid-rise building, asking $480,000. The listing agent's marketing sheet states the unit "rents easily at $2,800/mo" and lists a monthly HOA fee of $380. A mortgage broker has quoted a loan at a 2-year fixed rate of 3.5%, described in the fine print as reverting to the lender's standard variable rate — currently 6.5% — once the fixed period ends. On paper, that is four clean numbers: $480,000 price, $2,800/mo rent, $380/mo HOA, 3.5% financing. None of the four should be underwritten as given.

這筆物件（假設範例）：一間位於 120 戶中樓層社區的兩房兩衛公寓，開價 $480,000。銷售仲介的行銷資料上寫著這戶「租金行情輕鬆達每月 $2,800」，並列出每月管理費（HOA）$380。房貸經紀人報出一筆貸款，前兩年固定利率 3.5%，但條款細字寫明優惠期結束後會回復到貸方的標準浮動利率——目前為 6.5%。表面上看，這是四個乾淨的數字：開價 $480,000、租金 $2,800/mo、管理費 $380/mo、融資利率 3.5%。但這四個數字沒有一個能直接拿來核算。

Start with the rent claim, because every downstream number in this deal — net operating income, net yield, monthly carry, debt-service ratio — is built on top of it. The listing's $2,800/mo figure is a claim, not evidence. What does the building's actual leasing history say?

先從租金這項宣稱開始，因為這筆交易後續的每一個數字——淨營運收益、淨殖利率、每月現金流、償債能力比率——全都建立在它之上。物件廣告上的 $2,800/mo 只是一項宣稱，不是證據。這棟大樓實際的出租歷史又是怎麼說的？

Four two-bedroom units in the same building closed leases in the past six months: Unit 4B at $2,350/mo, Unit 7A at $2,450/mo, Unit 2C at $2,400/mo, and Unit 9D at $2,400/mo. These are signed leases with tenants actually paying them — not asking prices, not a property manager's optimistic pro forma.

同一棟大樓裡有四戶兩房單位，在過去六個月內完成租約簽訂：4B 戶每月 $2,350、7A 戶每月 $2,450、2C 戶每月 $2,400、9D 戶每月 $2,400。這些都是房客實際在付款的已簽租約——不是開價，也不是物業管理公司過度樂觀的預估表。

🎙 From the Trenches 來自實戰

A buyer under contract on a two-bedroom unit in a mid-rise building anchored their whole offer on the listing agent's claim of $2,800/mo rent — a figure that turned out to be copied from the seller's own optimistic rental-site ad from eighteen months earlier, never actually collected from a tenant. The first signed lease after closing came in at $2,350/mo. The buyer had underwritten monthly carry assuming $2,800/mo of income; the real number left them short from the very first tenant onward (hypothetical example).

一位買方在簽約承購一間中樓層社區的兩房單位時，把整筆出價的假設全建立在售屋仲介所稱的每月 $2,800 租金上——後來才發現，這個數字其實是從賣方十八個月前一則過於樂觀的租屋網站廣告直接抄來的，從未真正向任何房客收到過。成交後簽下的第一份租約，租金只有每月 $2,350。買方原本核算的每月持有現金流是以 $2,800 的租金收入為前提，實際數字讓他們從第一位房客開始，每個月都出現短缺（假設範例）。

⚠️
Pitfall:常見陷阱： Any listing's claimed rent is marketing copy until proven otherwise. Before underwriting a single dollar of income, ask for closed, signed leases on comparable units — not the property manager's pro forma, not the seller's target rent, and not a rental-site "estimate." If the seller cannot produce real leases, assume the asking rent is optimistic until comps say otherwise. 任何物件廣告上所稱的租金，在被證實之前都只能算是行銷文案。核算收入之前，一定要先索取同類型單位「已成交、已簽約」的租約，而不是物業管理公司的預估表、賣方設定的目標租金，或租屋網站的「估算」數字。如果賣方拿不出真正的租約，就該假設開價租金偏樂觀，直到有可比物件證明並非如此。

With rent underwritten at $2,400/mo — $28,800/yr — the next step is the full monthly cost stack, not just the HOA fee the listing advertises. Property tax on the $480,000 assessment runs $450/mo. Building insurance runs $60/mo. A maintenance reserve, set at roughly 5% of rent per standard rule of thumb, runs $120/mo. The HOA fee is the listing's stated $380/mo. And a vacancy allowance, also set at 5% of rent to cover the months the unit sits empty between tenants, runs another $120/mo. Add those five lines together: $450 + $60 + $120 + $380 + $120 = $1,130/mo in operating costs, before any mortgage payment.

在把租金核算為 $2,400/mo（年租金 $28,800）之後，下一步是建立完整的月成本結構，而不是只看廣告上寫的管理費。以 $480,000 的估價計算，房產稅每月約 $450。大樓保險每月約 $60。依常見經驗法則、以租金的 5% 估算的維護準備金，每月約 $120。管理費採廣告上宣稱的 $380/mo。另外，同樣以租金 5% 估算的空置率準備金——用來因應房客更替之間的空置月份——再加 $120/mo。把這五項加總：$450 + $60 + $120 + $380 + $120 ＝ $1,130/mo 的營運成本，這還不含房貸月付金。

Financing: the buyer puts 20% down, or $96,000, on the $480,000 price, leaving a $384,000 loan. The mortgage broker's quote is a teaser rateAn introductory interest rate offered for a fixed, limited period — e.g. two years — that is set below the lender's standard rate specifically to make the initial monthly payment look more affordable than the loan will be once the introductory period ends.在固定、有限的期間內（例如兩年）提供的優惠起始利率——刻意設定得比貸方的標準利率更低，目的是讓貸款初期的月付金，看起來比優惠期結束後的實際負擔更負擔得起。 of 3.5% fixed for 2 years, calculated on a standard 30-year amortization schedule. On $384,000 at 3.5%, the monthly principal-and-interest payment comes to approximately $1,724/mo.

融資條件：買方支付 20% 頭期款，也就是 $96,000，購買 $480,000 的物件，剩下貸款 $384,000。房貸經紀人的報價是一種優惠期利率An introductory interest rate offered for a fixed, limited period — e.g. two years — that is set below the lender's standard rate specifically to make the initial monthly payment look more affordable than the loan will be once the introductory period ends.在固定、有限的期間內（例如兩年）提供的優惠起始利率——刻意設定得比貸方的標準利率更低，目的是讓貸款初期的月付金，看起來比優惠期結束後的實際負擔更負擔得起。（teaser rate）：前兩年固定 3.5%，以標準 30 年攤還時程計算。以 $384,000、利率 3.5% 計算，每月本息還款金額約為 $1,724/mo。

The mortgage's fine print matters. After the 2-year fixed period, the loan reverts to the lender's standard variable rate — currently 6.5%. For this exercise, recompute the payment on the same $384,000 balance over a fresh 30-year amortization at 6.5%; in practice only a small sliver of principal will have amortized during the 2-year intro period, so this approximation is close. At 6.5%, the monthly principal-and-interest payment comes to approximately $2,427/mo — up nearly $703/mo from the teaser-rate payment, with no change to the loan balance, the property, or the rent.

房貸條款裡的細字很重要。兩年固定期結束後，貸款會回復到貸方的標準浮動利率——目前為 6.5%。為了這項練習，我們在同一筆 $384,000 餘額上，重新以 6.5% 利率、全新 30 年攤還時程計算月付金；實務上兩年優惠期內攤還掉的本金其實很少，所以這個簡化算法相當接近真實情況。以 6.5% 計算，每月本息還款金額約為 $2,427/mo——比優惠期利率下的月付金高出將近 $703/mo，而貸款餘額、物件與租金全都沒有任何改變。

🎙 From the Trenches 來自實戰

The pattern is not hypothetical. As central banks raised policy rates sharply through 2022 and 2023, Australia, the United Kingdom and Canada each faced a well-documented "fixed-rate cliff" or "mortgage renewal shock": large cohorts of borrowers who had locked in ultra-low fixed rates during 2020–2021 rolled off those terms into monthly payments that central-bank and regulator commentary — including from the Reserve Bank of Australia and the Bank of England — estimated could rise by several hundred to well over a thousand dollars a month for an average mortgage, purely from the rate reset, with no change to the loan balance, the property, or the borrower's income. The mechanism is exactly the teaser-to-reversion shock this deal just walked through, at national scale.

這個模式並非假設，而是有紀錄可查的真實現象。隨著各國央行在 2022 至 2023 年間大幅升息，澳洲、英國與加拿大都出現了廣泛紀錄在案的「固定利率懸崖」（fixed-rate cliff）或「房貸展期衝擊」（mortgage renewal shock）：大量在 2020 至 2021 年間鎖定超低固定利率的借款人，固定期一到就滾動進入新的月付金，根據澳洲儲備銀行（RBA）與英格蘭銀行（BOE）等機構的評論估算，光是利率重設本身——貸款餘額、物件與借款人收入都沒有任何變化——就可能讓一般房貸的月付金增加數百美元、甚至超過一千美元。這正是這筆交易剛剛演練過的「優惠利率到回復利率」衝擊機制，只是規模放大到了全國。

⚠️
Pitfall:常見陷阱： A monthly carry or DSR calculated only at a mortgage's teaser or introductory rate is not a real underwriting number — it is the number the loan is designed to look attractive on paper before the reversion rate takes over. Every deal with an introductory-rate mortgage needs two DSR calculations: one at the quoted rate, and one at whatever the loan reverts to. If the deal only works at the teaser rate, it does not actually work. 只用房貸的優惠期利率算出來的每月現金流或 DSR，並不是一個能拿來核算的真實數字——那只是這筆貸款在回復利率接手之前，被設計來讓帳面看起來好看的數字。任何帶有優惠期利率的房貸，都必須算兩次 DSR：一次用報價利率，一次用貸款到期回復後的利率。如果一筆交易只有在優惠期利率下才成立，那它其實根本不成立。

One more line item has not been billed yet, and it will not show up in any monthly cost stack until the day it does. The HOA board's most recent meeting minutes, obtained during due diligence, include the building's reserve study: a roof, facade and elevator modernization project estimated at $3,000,000, needed within the next five years, against a current reserve fund balance of only $540,000 — a funding ratio of 18%. Due-diligence professionals generally treat a reserve fund funded below roughly 70% of a documented capital-project need as a red flag for a coming special assessmentA one-time, mandatory extra charge levied on every unit owner in a condo or HOA building to cover a capital expense — such as a roof, facade or elevator project — that the building's ongoing reserve fund cannot cover. It is separate from, and additional to, the regular monthly HOA fee.公寓或社區管委會向每一位屋主徵收的一次性強制性額外費用，用來支應例如屋頂、外牆或電梯等重大工程支出——這類支出是社區既有的儲備金無法支應的。特別攤提款獨立於、且額外於每月常態管理費之外。. This building's reserve fund sits far below that threshold, and the shortfall does not appear anywhere in the $380/mo HOA fee the listing advertises.

It is separate from, and additional to, the regular monthly HOA fee.公寓或社區管委會向每一位屋主徵收的一次性強制性額外費用，用來支應例如屋頂、外牆或電梯等重大工程支出——這類支出是社區既有的儲備金無法支應的。特別攤提款獨立於、且額外於每月常態管理費之外。（special assessment）的紅色警訊。這棟大樓的儲備金遠低於這個門檻，而這個缺口完全不會出現在廣告宣稱的 $380/mo 管理費裡。

🎙 From the Trenches 來自實戰

Eighteen months after closing on a similar building, another buyer received a letter from the HOA board: the roof and facade project the reserve study had been flagging for years could no longer be deferred, and the shortfall between the reserve fund and the project cost meant a special assessment of just over $20,000 per unit, payable within ninety days. None of that was disclosed in the listing — the reserve study had been sitting in board meeting minutes the whole time, available to anyone who asked for them before closing (hypothetical example).

在一棟類似大樓成交十八個月後，另一位買方收到管委會來信：儲備金研究報告多年來一直示警的屋頂與外牆整修工程，已經無法再拖延，而儲備金餘額與工程總成本之間的缺口，意味著每戶要繳交超過 $20,000 的特別攤提款，並須於九十天內付清。這封信裡的內容，在物件的銷售資料中完全沒有揭露——儲備金研究報告其實一直躺在管委會會議紀錄裡，只要在成交前主動索取，任何人都能拿到（假設範例）。

Pull every number together: NOI of $15,240/yr, a monthly carry that is negative even at the teaser rate and deeply negative at reversion, and a plausible $20,500 special-assessment exposure sitting in the wings. Suppose the required net rental yield for a deal carrying this much reset risk and reserve-fund risk is a conservative 5.0% — still not aggressive, just enough margin to make the math work without hoping for appreciation to bail it out.

把每一項數字放在一起看：年 NOI 為 $15,240、即使在優惠利率期間現金流都已為負、回復利率後更是嚴重為負，還有一筆合理可能高達 $20,500 的特別攤提款曝險潛伏在旁。假設這樣一筆背負著利率回升風險與儲備金風險的交易，所要求的最低淨租金殖利率，保守設為 5.0%——這並不算激進，只是留出足夠的安全邊際，讓數字能夠成立，而不必寄望於未來的房價上漲來救場。

Deal Two: Sell-and-Upgrade or Keep-and-Rent

交易二：賣房換大坪數，還是保留出租

A different homeowner, a fresh scenario (hypothetical example): a household currently owns a home worth $650,000 with a remaining mortgage balance of $380,000 at a 4.0% fixed rate, carrying a monthly principal-and-interest payment of $2,000/mo. Household gross monthly income is $13,500/mo. They are considering trading up to a $950,000 home and weighing two paths.

換一位屋主，一個全新的情境（假設範例）：某家庭目前擁有一間市值 $650,000 的住宅，貸款餘額尚有 $380,000，固定利率 4.0%，每月本息還款金額為 $2,000/mo。家庭每月稅前總收入為 $13,500/mo。他們正考慮換購一間 $950,000 的住宅，並權衡以下兩種路徑。

Path 1 — sell the current home and buy the bigger one. Selling costs run 6% of the current home's value: 5% agent commission plus 1% closing costs. Buying costs on the new $950,000 home run 3.5%: 1.5% closing costs plus 2% transfer/stamp tax.

路徑一——賣掉現有住宅，換購較大的住宅。賣方成本為現有住宅價值的 6%：5% 仲介佣金加上 1% 成交相關費用。購買 $950,000 新住宅的買方成本為 3.5%：1.5% 成交相關費用加上 2% 產權移轉稅／印花稅。

After selling, the $380,000 mortgage on the current home is paid off and the $39,000 in sale costs are deducted: $650,000 − $380,000 − $39,000 = $231,000 in net proceeds, used entirely as the down payment on the $950,000 new home. The remaining $719,000 is financed at today's market rate of 5.5% on a 30-year loan, with a monthly principal-and-interest payment of approximately $4,083/mo.

出售之後，現有住宅 $380,000 的貸款會被清償，並扣除 $39,000 的出售成本：$650,000－$380,000－$39,000＝$231,000 的淨收入，全部用作 $950,000 新住宅的頭期款。剩餘 $719,000 依目前市場利率 5.5%、30 年期貸款融資，每月本息還款金額約為 $4,083/mo。

Path 2 — keep the current home as a rental and buy a new primary residence. No sell-side costs at all. Instead, the down payment on the new $950,000 home comes from $200,000 in separate household savings (hypothetical example), financing the remaining $750,000 at the same 5.5% rate over 30 years — a monthly principal-and-interest payment of approximately $4,259/mo. The existing home's $2,000/mo mortgage payment continues unchanged, since that loan is not being paid off. Market comps show the current home could rent for about $2,900/mo if it were listed.

路徑二——保留現有住宅出租，另外購買一間新的自住宅。完全不需要賣方成本。取而代之的是，$950,000 新住宅的頭期款來自家庭另外準備的 $200,000 存款（假設範例），剩餘 $750,000 以相同的 5.5% 利率、30 年期融資——每月本息還款金額約為 $4,259/mo。現有住宅每月 $2,000/mo 的貸款月付金維持不變，因為這筆貸款並不會被清償。市場可比物件顯示，若把現有住宅拿出去出租，大約可租得 $2,900/mo。

Both properties in Path 2 sit in the same city, exposed to the same local job market, the same local new-supply pipeline and the same local price cycle. That is a very different risk profile from Path 1's single, simpler property — even though Path 2 avoided $72,250 in transaction costs to get there.

路徑二的兩間物件都座落在同一個城市，暴露在相同的當地就業市場、相同的當地新供給管道，以及相同的當地房價週期之下。這與路徑一單一、較簡單的物件相比，是截然不同的風險輪廓——即便路徑二是靠省下 $72,250 的交易成本才換來的。

Lay the two paths side by side one last time: Path 1 costs $72,250 to execute but lands at a comfortable 30.2% single-property DSR with no cross-market concentration. Path 2 costs nothing in transaction fees, keeps the rental income and long-term optionality of the old home, but carries a 46.4% combined DSR and both properties riding the same local cycle.

最後把兩條路徑並排再看一次：路徑一執行成本為 $72,250，但換來單一物件、相對從容的 30.2% DSR，且沒有跨市場集中度問題。路徑二不需要任何交易成本，保留了舊屋的租金收入與長期彈性，但合併 DSR 高達 46.4%，且兩間物件都乘著同一個當地週期起伏。
