Bottom line總結 Almost every variable that moves Dubai property is set somewhere else. The dirham has been fixed at 3.6725 to the dollar since November 1997, so the Central Bank of the UAE follows the Federal Reserve almost mechanically and has no independent instrument. It raised its base rate to 3.90% on 17 September 2026, one day after the Fed raised its target range. The household base is roughly 88% foreign, so it is set by visa policy and by the hiring decisions of employers rather than by births — the fertility rate is 1.21 and the population still grew 88-fold in sixty-five years. And the downside, when it has been tested, has been absorbed by Abu Dhabi's balance sheet rather than by any market mechanism.

The one long series that describes the underlying economy is real GDP per capita, and it runs the wrong way. It peaked at US$117,073 in 1980, stood at US$41,605 in 2024, and is now below the 1970 level. Output grew throughout; population grew faster. That is the arithmetic behind a market where prices have never been driven by residents becoming richer, and where the growth story has always been a headcount story.

Two things have genuinely improved. The fiscal breakeven oil price has fallen from US$91.60 in 2014 to US$50.00 in 2025, which makes an oil shock materially less binding on government spending than it was in the cycle that produced the last six-year property decline. And the non-oil economy has compounded faster than the oil economy for most of two decades. Against that, the 2026 war has left the central bank forecasting growth of 1.6% for 2026, after 3.0% in the first quarter, with a rebound to 10.4% pencilled in for 2027. The transaction-level consequences are set out in the companion Dubai Property Market Report. 驅動杜拜房產的變數，幾乎都在別處決定。迪拉姆自 1997 年 11 月起固定在 3.6725 兌 1 美元，阿聯央行因此幾乎機械式地跟著聯準會走，沒有獨立的工具。聯準會 2026 年 9 月 16 日調升目標區間，阿聯央行次日把基準利率升到 3.90%。家戶基礎約 88% 是外籍人士，取決於簽證政策和雇主的聘僱決定，不取決於出生數：總生育率 1.21，人口卻在六十五年內增為 88 倍。至於下檔風險，過去每次受到考驗，都是由阿布達比的資產負債表吸收，沒有任何市場機制承擔。

描述底層經濟的唯一長序列是實質人均 GDP，它的方向是向下：1980 年見頂，為 117,073 美元，2024 年為 41,605 美元，現在已低於 1970 年的水準。總產出一路成長，人口成長得更快。這就是這個市場的基本算式：房價從來不由居民變富所推動，成長故事一直是人數的故事。

有兩項確實改善。財政平衡油價從 2014 年的 91.60 美元，降到 2025 年的 50.00 美元，油價衝擊對政府支出的約束，因此比上一次為期六年的房價下跌前明顯減輕。非石油經濟近二十年的大部分時間，複合成長也快於石油經濟。相對地，2026 年的戰事使央行預測 2026 年成長 1.6%，第一季實際成長 3.0%，2027 年預測反彈到 10.4%。成交層面的後果，見姊妹篇《杜拜住宅市場報告》。

UAE consumer prices rose 12.3% in 2008 and fell 4.7% in 2009. They were negative in 2019 at −1.9%, in 2020 at −2.1% and in 2021 at −0.1%, then rose 4.8% in 2022. Housing carries a heavy weight in that basket, so the years of consumer price deflation and the years of falling rent are substantially the same years. Why this matters more here than in Bangkok or Kuala Lumpur這一點在此地，比在曼谷或吉隆坡更重要

Thailand's central bank has held its policy rate at 1.00% since February 2026 while consumer prices turned from deflation to a 2.82% rise in September. Malaysia keeps a domestic policy rate with room to move. Both have a domestic instrument. The UAE has none, and the offsetting compensation is fiscal: government spending, project awards and visa policy are the levers that respond to local conditions. An investor reading UAE property should watch federal spending and immigration rules where they would watch the policy rate elsewhere.泰國央行自 2026 年 2 月起把政策利率維持在 1.00%，消費者物價則從通縮轉為 9 月年增 2.82%。馬來西亞有本國的政策利率，還有調整空間。兩者都有本國的政策工具，阿聯沒有，補償手段在財政面：政府支出、工程發包、簽證政策，是回應本地情勢的槓桿。判讀阿聯房產的投資人，應該用聯邦支出與移民規則，取代其他市場中觀察的政策利率。

Breakeven price vs production平衡油價 vs 產量

The fiscal breakeven oil price is the level at which the budget balances. It rose from US$13.00 in 2000 to a peak of US$91.60 in 2014 as spending expanded against a high oil price, and has since fallen to US$50.00 in 2025. Production over the same period rose from 2.19 to 3.21 million barrels a day. What a migration-driven household base changes about property risk移民驅動的家戶基礎，如何改變房產風險

In Bangkok or Taipei, demographic risk is slow, one-directional and visible thirty years ahead in the birth series. In Dubai it is fast, two-directional and invisible in advance. The 2020 fall took twelve months. Part 3 · The Politics — Who Sets the Rules政治篇 · 規則的制定者

Dubai writes its own property law and does not hold its own backstop杜拜自訂房產法規，但不持有自己的最後支撐

The UAE is a federation of seven emirates governed by a Federal Supreme Council of their rulers. Abu Dhabi holds the presidency and the overwhelming majority of the federation's hydrocarbon reserves; Dubai holds the vice-presidency and the premiership. Each emirate retains control of its own land, natural resources and, critically for this analysis, its own property law. Dubai's freehold regime is emirate-level legislation — Law No. 7 of 2006 with the designated areas set out in Regulation No. 3 of 2006 — and it exists because the Ruler of Dubai decided in 2002 that it should.阿聯是七個酋長國組成的聯邦，由各國統治者組成的聯邦最高委員會治理。阿布達比持有總統職位和聯邦絕大部分的油氣蘊藏。杜拜持有副總統與總理職位。各酋長國保留對自己土地與自然資源的控制權。對本分析最關鍵的是，各國保留自己的房產法規。杜拜的自由持有制度屬於酋長國層級的立法，即 2006 年第 7 號法律，指定區域列在 2006 年第 3 號規則。這套制度的起點，是杜拜統治者 2002 年的決定。

The asymmetry matters at the point of stress. Dubai sets the rules that create the market and runs its own budget, but the reserves that absorbed the last crisis are Abu Dhabi's.   • External capital access × imported dollar valuation. The access regime is the most open in this coverage, and the currency it is priced in is not chosen locally. How this model dies模型的可證偽條件

Stated before the conclusion. Each condition is observable in a published series.這些條件在結論之前列出。每一項都能在公開序列中觀察到。

Falsified to the upside if出現以下情形，向上證偽

  • Real GDP per capita rises for five consecutive years. This breaks argument two: output would finally be compounding faster than headcount, and a domestic income base would exist for the first time in the record.實質人均 GDP 連續五年上升。這會推翻論點二：產出終於比人數成長得更快，紀錄中第一次出現本地所得基礎。
  • Dubai construction sector GDP turns negative while population growth holds above 250,000 a year. The self-reinforcing loop in Part 2 would be broken by the professional-migration channel, and the supply case in the report is too pessimistic.杜拜營建業 GDP 轉為負成長，人口年增仍維持在 250,000 人以上。經濟結構篇所述的自我強化迴路，已被專業移民管道打斷，報告對供給的判斷過於悲觀。
  • The real effective exchange rate falls below 92 and holds. The imported valuation term becomes a sustained tailwind for foreign demand, which is the mechanism that ended the 2015–19 decline.實質有效匯率跌破 92 並維持。輸入的估值項變成外資需求的持續順風，2015 至 2019 年下跌的終結，靠的就是這個機制。

Falsified to the downside if出現以下情形，向下證偽

  • Dubai population growth falls below 100,000 in a year, or the total declines as it did in 2020. The numerator of the model fails, and every absorption assumption on both pages fails with it. The central bank's first-half figure, more than 150,000, is above that line.杜拜年度人口增量跌破 100,000 人，或像 2020 年一樣出現總量下降。模型的分子失效，兩頁的所有吸納假設隨之失效。阿聯央行估計的上半年增量在 150,000 人以上，高於這條線。
  • A Dubai government-related entity restructures without federal or Abu Dhabi support. This breaks argument three: the standing backstop would be shown not to be standing, and the risk premium on every Dubai asset would reprice.杜拜政府關聯機構，在沒有聯邦或阿布達比支持下完成重組。這會推翻論點三：常設的支撐被證明並非常設，杜拜所有資產的風險溢酬都要重新定價。
  • The fiscal breakeven oil price returns above US$75 while Hormuz volumes stay depressed. Government spending is the substitute for the monetary instrument the peg removed; losing it removes the only counter-cyclical lever the emirate has.財政平衡油價回升到 75 美元以上，荷莫茲海峽通行量仍然低迷。政府支出是釘住制度移除貨幣工具之後的替代品。這項替代品失效，該酋長國僅有的反循環槓桿就沒了。
  • The golden visa threshold is raised, or the property route is closed. Access has widened continuously since 2019 and that widening is embedded in current pricing. The Answer — Where, What, When結論篇 · 區位、產品、時點

Where區位

Established freehold communities with a delivery pipeline below the citywide average. That excludes Business Bay, Jumeirah Village Circle, Dubai South, Dubai Science Park and Dubai Hills Estate, which between them take more than a third of 2026 scheduled deliveries. The pipeline is the model's binding term, and it is geographically concentrated, so the term can be avoided by location in a way the others cannot.交屋供給低於全市平均的成熟自由持有社區。這排除 Business Bay、Jumeirah Village Circle、Dubai South、Dubai Science Park 與 Dubai Hills Estate，這五區合計占 2026 年排程交屋量的三分之一以上。供給是模型的約束項，而且分布集中，所以這一項可以靠區位避開，其他各項則不行。

What產品

Completed apartments, not villas and not off-plan. Apartments yield 7.08% gross against the villa's 4.54%, and the villa's 194% run since January 2021 has already paid for its scarcity. Completed stock avoids both the 20% off-plan premium and the 2027 handover cohort. The return on this asset is the rent line; buy the product with the rent line.只買已完工公寓，別墅和預售都不碰。公寓毛收益率 7.08%，別墅 4.54%。別墅 2021 年 1 月以來漲 194%，稀缺性已經反映完了。已完工的存量，同時避開 20% 的預售溢價和 2027 年的交屋批次。本資產的報酬來自租金線，所以要買有租金線的產品。

When時點

The information that resolves the decision is the quarterly delivery ratio, and the 2027 readings arrive quarter by quarter. Below 45% the pipeline is absorbed; above 60% the third-crash scenario becomes the base case. The latest readings are 42.6% for the first quarter and about 40% for the second. Prices have fallen 10.4% from the February peak and have been flat since June; the June transaction rebound of 46.8% month-on-month did not last. Neither fact settles the question.決定這個判斷的資訊是季度交屋達成率，2027 年的讀數會逐季揭曉。低於 45%，供給可被吸納。高於 60%，第三次崩跌情境成為基準情境。最新讀數是第一季 42.6%，第二季約 40%。房價比 2 月高點低 10.4%，6 月起持平。6 月成交月增 46.8% 的反彈沒有延續。這兩項事實都不足以定論。

What would change the answer什麼會改變這個答案

A Dubai population print below 100,000 growth, or a real effective exchange rate below 92. The first would remove the demand base entirely; the second would restore the foreign-buyer valuation tailwind that ended the last six-year decline. Both are published, both are free, and both move before price does.杜拜人口年增低於 100,000 人，或實質有效匯率低於 92。前者會完全移除需求基礎，後者會恢復外資買方的估值順風，也就是終結上一次六年下跌的那個因素。兩者都是公開資料，取得都沒有成本，變動都先於價格。

What this page concludes本頁的結論

Dubai is the most open property market covered on this site and the one with the least domestic control over its own cycle. Freehold title including land, no ownership quota, no income tax on rent, no capital gains tax and a residency of five to ten years at AED 2m, depending on which official source is right, are terms no other market here offers a foreigner. The price of that openness is that the monetary policy, the currency and the demand base are all set elsewhere.杜拜是本站涵蓋的市場中最開放的，也是對自己的循環擁有最少本地控制權的。含土地的永久產權、沒有持有配額、租金不課所得稅、沒有資本利得稅，加上 200 萬迪拉姆換 5 至 10 年的居留（要看哪個官方來源正確），這些條件，本站其他市場都沒有提供給外國人。這種開放的代價，是貨幣政策、幣值和需求基礎，全都在別處決定。

The record does not support underwriting capital growth. On the BIS series, which ends in the first quarter of 2024, real prices are below both the 2008 and the 2014 peaks after sixteen years, and the series contains completed drawdowns of 36.5% and 30.2%. The return is the rent, and the rent is the most volatile of any market compared here. A 6.57% gross yield falls to roughly 5.3% once the service charge is deducted, which is still the widest in this coverage and still worth owning, provided it is underwritten as income and not as appreciation.歷史紀錄不支持把資本利得放進承作假設。BIS 序列止於 2024 年第一季，歷經十六年，實質房價低於 2008 年與 2014 年兩個高點，序列內有幅度 36.5% 與 30.2% 的兩次完整崩跌。報酬來自租金，而這條租金線是本頁比較的市場中波動最大的。6.57% 的毛收益率，扣除社區服務費後降到約 5.3%，仍是本站涵蓋範圍中最高的，仍然值得持有，前提是把它當收益型資產承作，不是當增值型資產。

The single question that decides the next three years is whether 337,700 scheduled units become about 140,000 delivered ones, at the first-half ratio of 41%, or about 200,000, at 60%. That is a construction-management question, not a monetary or demographic one, and it is answered quarter by quarter in a published statistic. Transaction-level detail is in the Dubai Property Market Report.決定未來三年的單一問題是：337,700 戶排程供給，最終變成約 140,000 戶實際交屋（依上半年 41% 的達成率），還是約 200,000 戶（依 60% 計）。這是工程管理的問題，不是貨幣或人口的問題，而且逐季由一項公開統計給出答案。成交層面的細節見《杜拜住宅市場報告》。
