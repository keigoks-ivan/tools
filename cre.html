COLUMN DEFINITIONS & SOURCES 欄位定義與資料來源

Cap Rate 資本化率

Net operating income ÷ asset price — the return a buyer requires. Most figures here are "prime yield" for the top-tier, best-located assets in each city, not an all-grades market average. Cross-city comparisons should be read directionally; check the ⓘ source note for the exact basis and quarter.

資本化率＝年淨營運收益（NOI）÷資產價格，反映買方要求的報酬率。本頁多數為各城市「頂級、地段最佳資產」的指標性殖利率（prime yield），並非全市場均值。跨城市比較僅供方向性參考，確切計算基礎與季度請見各列ⓘ來源說明。

Rent YoY 租金年增率

Asking or effective rent, year-over-year, for the broker-defined submarket cited in the source. Green = growth, red = decline, "—" = not found in reliable named-broker sources (never estimated).

掛牌或有效租金之年增率，依來源引註中經紀商所定義的子市場計算。綠色為成長，紅色為下滑，「—」代表未能在可靠具名經紀商來源中找到對應數字（絕不估算）。

Vacancy 空置率

<8% tight (green) · 8–15% balanced (yellow) · >15% elevated (red). Thresholds are uniform across all three sectors for comparability. In practice, logistics and retail "natural vacancy" tends to run lower than office, so treat these office-calibrated bands as directional (not sector-specific) for those two sectors.

<8% 緊俏（綠）・8%至15% 平衡（黃）・>15% 偏高（紅）。門檻在三大類別間統一使用以利比較。實務上物流與零售的「自然空置率」通常低於辦公室，此門檻對這兩類別僅供方向性參考，非各別校準。

標示來源研究判定為該城市本週期最突出的資產類別——是一個「招牌類別」，並非特定資產或交易名稱（原始研究資料記錄的是每個城市的類別標籤，而非個別資產名稱）。

Not Investment Advice

This page aggregates third-party brokerage research for reference only. Commercial real estate cap rates and rent figures vary widely by asset grade, exact location and methodology even within a single city; always verify against primary sources before acting.

本頁彙整第三方經紀商研究僅供參考，非投資建議。即使在同一城市內，商用不動產的資本化率與租金數字也會因資產等級、確切地段與統計方法而有很大差異；行動前請務必以原始來源覆核。
