Cap Rate 資本化率

Net operating income ÷ asset price — the return a buyer requires. Most figures here are "prime yield" for the top-tier, best-located assets in each city, not an all-grades market average. Cross-city comparisons should be read directionally; check the ⓘ source note for the exact basis and quarter.

資本化率＝年淨營運收益（NOI）÷資產價格，反映買方要求的報酬率。本頁多數為各城市「頂級、地段最佳資產」的指標性殖利率（prime yield），並非全市場均值。跨城市比較僅供方向性參考，確切計算基礎與季度請見各列ⓘ來源說明。

Spread 利差

Cap rate minus the local 10-year government bond yield, as of 2026-08. A wider spread compensates the investor for illiquidity and asset-specific risk; a spread under 0.5pp means the yield sits near or below the local risk-free rate. AE (Dubai) uses the 10-year US Treasury as a proxy — there is no liquid AED government bond and the currency is pegged to USD.

資本化率減去當地十年期公債殖利率（資料截至2026年8月）。利差越寬，代表投資人因流動性與資產特定風險獲得的補償越高；利差低於0.5個百分點，代表殖利率貼近或低於當地無風險利率。杜拜（阿聯酋）因無流動性充足的迪拉姆公債、且貨幣盯住美元，故以美國十年期公債殖利率作為替代指標。

Cap Rate Trend 資本化率趨勢

▼ compressing (prices firming) · ▶ stable · ▲ expanding (softening), reflecting H1-2026 broker commentary next to the cap rate value. European "stable" entries are largely inferred from a Savills Europe roundup that named only the cities where yields moved (Dublin, Milan, Brussels down; Oslo, Dusseldorf up) — cities not named are classified stable by omission, a lower-confidence call than a direct city-specific quote. No arrow = no broker commentary found.

▼壓縮（價格走堅）・▶持平・▲擴張（走軟），標示於資本化率數值旁，反映2026年上半年經紀商評論。歐洲的「持平」多數是由Savills歐洲報告推論而來——該報告僅點名殖利率有變動的城市（都柏林、米蘭、布魯塞爾下滑；奧斯陸、杜塞道夫上升），未被點名的城市則歸類為持平，信心水準低於直接引用該城市數據的判斷。無箭頭代表未找到相關經紀商評論。

Rent YoY 租金年增率

Asking or effective rent, year-over-year, for the broker-defined submarket cited in the source. Green = growth, red = decline, "—" = not found in reliable named-broker sources (never estimated).

掛牌或有效租金之年增率，依來源引註中經紀商所定義的子市場計算。綠色為成長，紅色為下滑，「—」代表未能在可靠具名經紀商來源中找到對應數字（絕不估算）。

Prime Rent 頂級租金

Prime / Grade-A CBD headline office rents, office tab only, converted to USD per square foot per year at Aug-2026 FX rates. Bases differ slightly by market — Toronto and Vancouver figures are NET rents (excluding operating costs), Hong Kong uses the One/Two IFC top-of-market benchmark rather than a broad Grade A average, and Melbourne uses net-effective (post-incentive) rent. Cross-city comparison is therefore directional, not exact; hover a city's rent figure for its specific local basis.

頂級／甲級核心商業區辦公室頂級租金（僅辦公分頁顯示），以2026年8月匯率換算為每平方英尺每年美元金額。各市場計算基礎略有差異——多倫多與溫哥華為淨租金（不含營運成本），香港採用One／Two IFC頂級大樓指標而非甲級平均，墨爾本則採用淨有效租金（扣除優惠後）。因此跨城市比較僅供方向性參考，並非精確對比；詳細計算基礎請將游標移至各城市租金數值查看。

Vacancy 空置率

<8% tight (green) · 8–15% balanced (yellow) · >15% elevated (red). Thresholds are uniform across all three sectors for comparability. In practice, logistics and retail "natural vacancy" tends to run lower than office, so treat these office-calibrated bands as directional (not sector-specific) for those two sectors.

<8% 緊俏（綠）・8%至15% 平衡（黃）・>15% 偏高（紅）。門檻在三大類別間統一使用以利比較。實務上物流與零售的「自然空置率」通常低於辦公室，此門檻對這兩類別僅供方向性參考，非各別校準。

Not Investment Advice

This page aggregates third-party brokerage research for reference only. Commercial real estate cap rates and rent figures vary widely by asset grade, exact location and methodology even within a single city; always verify against primary sources before acting.

本頁彙整第三方經紀商研究僅供參考，非投資建議。即使在同一城市內，商用不動產的資本化率與租金數字也會因資產等級、確切地段與統計方法而有很大差異；行動前請務必以原始來源覆核。
